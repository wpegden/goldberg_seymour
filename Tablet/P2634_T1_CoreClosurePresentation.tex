\begin{lemma}[An exact corresponding PE core-closure presentation]
Let \(\mathcal E:\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}(G,k,e_0,\rho,T,\varphi)\)
be a maximum ETT in a finite loopless multigraph, with terminal kind PE,
and suppose \(\noderef{ModColoring}(\mathcal E,\varphi)\).
Let \(B:\noderef{HierarchyBase}(\mathcal E,\varphi)\), and let \(T_n\)
be the terminal ladder tree. Assume that \(B.\mathit{core}\) is reached from
\(T_n\) by Tashkinov augmentations under \(\varphi\) and has strictly larger
length. Then there exist a tree-sequence \(Z\), a certificate
\(C:\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}(\mathcal E,\varphi,Z)\),
and a base \(B_Z\) for \(C.\mathit{replacementETT}\) under \(\varphi\),
with the following properties. The replacement ETT is maximum; \(Z\) is a
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} of \(B.\mathit{core}\) under \(\varphi\);
its exact final series tree is \(Z\); its exact terminal ladder tree is
\(T_n\); its terminal kind is PE; and
\[
 B_Z.\mathit{core}=B.\mathit{core},\qquad
 B_Z.\mathit{closure}=B.\mathit{closure},\qquad
 B_Z.\mathit{supportPosition.val}=B.\mathit{supportPosition.val}.
\]
All tree identities concern full ordered edge-valued tree-sequences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a closure \(Z\) of \(B.\mathit{core}\) under \(\varphi\) by
\noderef{TashkinovClosureExists}. Its certificate contains an augmentation
route from \(B.\mathit{core}\) to \(Z\), and closedness of \(V(Z)\), by
\noderef{TashkinovClosure}. Concatenate the given route from \(T_n\) to
\(B.\mathit{core}\) with this route, using the reflexive-transitive-chain
definition of \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}. This gives a route
from \(T_n\) to \(Z\). By \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment},
both \(T_n\) and \(B.\mathit{core}\) are exact initial segments of \(Z\).
In particular, \(|T_n|<|B.\mathit{core}|\leq|Z|\).

Apply the universal PE clause of the given \noderef{ModColoring} to \(Z\),
its exact initial-segment relation, this strict inequality, and the
concatenated route. It provides the certificate \(C\) relative to the
original \(\mathcal E\). By \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport}, its
replacement ETT is maximum. By \noderef{HierarchyBaseCorrespondingTransport},
there is a replacement base \(B_Z\) with the three stated base identities.

The concatenated route and closedness of \(V(Z)\) make \(Z\) a closure
of \(T_n\), again by \noderef{TashkinovClosure}. Apply
\noderef{CorrespondingTerminalClosureEq} to \(C\), with PE seed exactly
\(T_n\), and that closure. It identifies the final tree of the replacement
series carried by \(C\) with \(Z\), as a full ordered tree.
The rung-agreement and heterogeneous series-presentation fields of
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} identify this replacement
series with the actual series of its replacement ETT after transporting
the rung index. Thus the replacement ETT's own final series output is \(Z\).

For completeness, \(n\geq1\) by \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}.
The index \(q=n-1\) lies in \(\operatorname{Fin}(n)\), with successor
the terminal stored index \(n\). The ladder-tree agreement field of \(C\)
at \(q\) identifies the terminal ladder trees. Its extension-kind agreement
field at \(n\) identifies the terminal kinds, which are therefore PE.
These identifications use the same rung and series transports, so they
concern the actual replacement ETT, not merely a separately chosen series.
\end{proof}
