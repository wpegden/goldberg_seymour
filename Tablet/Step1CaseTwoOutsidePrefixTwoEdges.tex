\begin{lemma}[Step 1, Subcase 2.1: exact prefix and two-edge construction]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph retaining individual parallel-edge
identities, let $e_0$ be distinguished, and let $[k]$ be the color set.
Let $T$ be a positive-rung certified ETT with generating coloring
$\varphi$, maximum property, properness, and exactly $e_0$ uncolored.
Assume the complete six-part lower-rung package.  Let $H$ be a good
hierarchy over a hierarchy base, with $d<q_0$ dividers.  Assume uniformly
that every same-rung maximum ETT built from an arbitrary certified input
and generating coloring and having a good hierarchy with fewer than
$q_0$ dividers is elementary.  Assume the large bound
$|\overline\varphi(T_n)|\geq2n+11$, the separate terminal certificate
\[
 T\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\eta\in D^H_{n,d+1}}\Gamma^d_\eta\right)^-
 \text{-closed under }\varphi,
\]
and elementarity of $V(T)$.  Put $A=H.\mathit{starred}(d)$.

Let $\alpha\ne\beta$, with $\alpha$ missing and singleton-closed on $T$
and $\beta$ not missing on $T$.  Let $P_1,P_2,P_3$ be pairwise
support-disjoint exact exit paths for $\alpha,\beta$.  Their outside ends
$a_1,a_2,a_3$ and tree ends $b_1,b_2,b_3$ are pairwise distinct and
$b_1\prec_T b_2\prec_T b_3$.  Retain, for every $j$, the exact
$\beta$-colored boundary-edge identity $f_j$ on $P_j$ incident with
$b_j$.  Suppose $b_3\notin V(A)$.

Then there exist a color $\gamma\ne\beta$, a proper coloring $\mu$
leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and an exact ordered tree $J$, together
with a corresponding same-rung ETT $\mathcal E_J$ under $\mu$, a
maximum-property certificate, and a good hierarchy $K_J$ on $J$ with
exactly $d$ dividers, such that the following all hold.
The coloring $\mu$ is $(T_n,D_n,\varphi)$-stable and is a mod-coloring of
the original ETT, the replacement has the
same large missing-color estimate, and neither $\gamma$ nor $\beta$
belongs to the union of the last indexed Gamma pairs of $K_J$.
The color $\beta$ is missing at $b_3$ under $\mu$.  There are three exact
$(T,\mu,\{\gamma,\beta\})$-exit paths $R_1,R_2,R_3$ with the same
respective endpoints, supports, and lengths as $P_1,P_2,P_3$; hence their
supports remain pairwise disjoint.  Finally $b_1,b_2,b_3$ lie in $J$, and
both endpoints of each retained individual edge identity $f_1$ and $f_2$
lie in $J$.  More precisely, $C$ is the exact ordered prefix of $T$
ending at $b_3$, $J_1=C+f_1$, and $J=J_1+f_2$ in the sense of
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} and
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{Step1CaseTwoOutsideRecoloredPrefix} to the displayed ETT,
hierarchy, elementarity and terminal-closure data, and the three exact
exit paths.  It supplies $\gamma,\mu,B^-,C$ and transformed paths
$R_1,R_2,R_3$.  Retain all of its conclusions: exact local coloring and
missing-set agreement on $B^-$, the one-edge prefix $B^-\prec C$ ending
at $b_3$, stability on the last ladder tree, the old and new missingness
at $b_3$, the exact color of $f_3$, the two retained boundary identities
$f_1,f_2$ with distinct outside endpoints, and the support and length
equalities for the three transformed paths.

These are exactly the explicit prefix inputs of
\noderef{Step1CaseTwoOutsidePrefixHierarchy}.  Apply that lemma with the
given maximum property, complete lower-rung package, large estimate, and
the original hierarchy.  It gives the mod-coloring certificate, the
eligible PE or exact SE/RE route to $C$, and a corresponding maximum ETT
$\mathcal E_C$ with an actual base and a good hierarchy $K_C$.  Retain
its equality of divider counts, transported large estimate, and the two
last-Gamma exclusions.

Now apply \noderef{Step1CaseTwoOutsideTwoEdgeExtension} to this complete
$C$-level package, the two retained boundary identities, their distinct
outside endpoints, and the fact that $\beta$ is missing at $b_3\in C$.
It returns exact trees $J_1=C+f_1$ and $J=J_1+f_2$, a corresponding
maximum ETT $\mathcal E_J$, a rebuilt base, and a good hierarchy $K_J$.
Its divider-count equality, composed with that for $K_C$, identifies the
divider count with that of $H$; it also gives the required large estimate,
Gamma exclusions, endpoint inclusions, and membership of $b_1,b_2,b_3$
in $J$.

Choose the witnesses from these three applications.  Properness, exact
one-uncoloredness, ladder stability, $\beta$-missingness at $b_3$, and
all transformed-path assertions come from the first lemma.  The
mod-coloring assertion comes from the second.  Maximum property, the
final hierarchy data, the large estimate, both Gamma exclusions, the two
exact adjoin relations, and all endpoint conclusions come from the third.
The exact-prefix and last-vertex assertions for $C$, and the support and
length equalities, come from the first.  This enumerates every conjunct
of the asserted witness package.
\end{proof}
