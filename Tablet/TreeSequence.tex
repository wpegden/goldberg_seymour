\begin{definition}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a multigraph and let $e_0$ be an edge identity.  A
tree-sequence with respect to $G$ and $e_0$ is an alternating ordered
sequence
\[
  T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_n,y_n),\qquad n\geq 1,
\]
whose vertices $y_0,\ldots,y_n$ are pairwise distinct, whose edge identities
$e_1,\ldots,e_n$ are pairwise distinct, whose first edge is $e_1=e_0$, and
such that, for every $1\leq j\leq n$, the edge $e_j$ has ends $y_j$ and
$y_i$ for some $0\leq i<j$.  The ordering is part of the data; equal
endpoint pairs do not identify distinct parallel edge identities.
\end{definition}
