\begin{definition}
For indexed Tashkinov-series data, define (D_0=\varnothing).  For
\(i>0\), define
\[
 D_i=\left(\bigcup_{0\leq h\leq i}S_h\right)
      \setminus \overline{\varphi_i}(T_i).
\]
Here the union and difference are finite-set operations, and
\(\overline{\varphi_i}(T_i)\) is the set of colors missing at some vertex
of the ordered tree-sequence (T_i).  This is the stored-color convention
of Algorithm 3.1, including its explicit convention (D_0=\varnothing).
\end{definition}
