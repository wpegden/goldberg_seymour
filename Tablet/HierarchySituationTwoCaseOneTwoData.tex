\begin{definition}
Fix the graph, palette, distinguished edge, exact terminal tree \(T\),
generating coloring \(\varphi_n\), positive-rung ETT, hierarchy base,
minimum hierarchy counterexample \(C\), and its
\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext}.  A
\emph{Situation 7.2, Subcase 1.2 datum} is a
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoCommonTailData}, extended by colors
\(\alpha,\gamma_1,\gamma_2\in[k]\) such that \(\alpha\) is missing at
both \(y_{p-1}\) and \(y_p\), and
\[
 \alpha\in
 \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}
   (C.\mathit{hierarchy},\operatorname{Fin.last}
      (C.\mathit{hierarchy}.\mathit{dividerCount})),
 \qquad
 C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^q_\alpha=\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2\},
 \qquad \gamma_1\ne\gamma_2.
\]
The final branch field is exactly
\[
                 \theta\in\overline\varphi_n(y_{p-1}).
\]
Thus this datum carries the Case 1 fields and the Subcase 1.2 condition
directly over the branch-neutral common tail.  It does not inherit the
contradictory theta-present field of
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoTailData}.
\end{definition}
