\begin{definition}
A \emph{hierarchy counterexample} consists of a positive-rung extended
Tashkinov tree $T$ under its generating coloring $\varphi_n$, a hierarchy
of $T$ and Gamma sets making that hierarchy good, and a certificate that
$T$ satisfies maximum property, such that $V(T)$ is not elementary under
$\varphi_n$.  Thus the definition packages exactly conditions (6.2) and
(6.3); minimality is imposed separately using the finite measure below.
\end{definition}
