\begin{definition}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite multigraph, let $\varphi$ be a partial
$k$-edge-coloring, and let $X\subseteq V$.  The set $X$ is strongly closed
with respect to $\varphi$ if it is closed with respect to $\varphi$ and any
two distinct colored edge identities in $\partial_G(X)$ receive distinct
colors.  Thus parallel boundary edges are compared as distinct edges even
when they have the same endpoint pair.
\end{definition}
