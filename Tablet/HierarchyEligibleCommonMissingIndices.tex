\begin{definition}
For a fixed minimum hierarchy counterexample (C) with exact terminal
tree (T), put

\[
 \ell=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailLength}(C),\qquad
 T(y_i)=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,i).
\]

The finite set of \emph{eligible common-missing tail indices} is the
classical filter of the integer interval ({1,\ldots,\ell-1}) consisting
of those (i) for which there are a
\noderef{HierarchyReplacementPresentation} (P) in the paper's family
(mathcal K) and an exact color (alpha) such that (alpha) is missing,
under the replacement coloring of (P), both at the last vertex of the
exact prefix (T(y_i)) and at the final displayed vertex of (T).
The replacement presentations themselves are not assumed to form a finite
type; only their possible natural-number indices are filtered inside the
finite interval.
\end{definition}
