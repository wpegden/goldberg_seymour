\begin{lemma}[Existence of a maximum stable closure]
Let \(G\) be a finite loopless multigraph, let \(e_0\) be a distinguished
edge identity, and let \(T\) and \(S\) be tree-sequences based at \(e_0\).
Fix a set of colors \(C\subseteq [k]\) and a partial \(k\)-edge-coloring
\(\varphi\).  Suppose that there are a partial \(k\)-edge-coloring \(\pi\)
and a tree-sequence \(U\), based at \(e_0\), such that \(\pi\) is proper,
leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, is \((T,C,\varphi)\)-stable, and \(U\) is
a closure of the exact prescribed seed \(S\) under \(\pi\).

Then there are such a coloring \(\pi_*\) and such a closure \(U_*\) of
\(S\) for which the following maximum property holds: for every proper
partial \(k\)-edge-coloring \(\rho\) that leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored
and is \((T,C,\varphi)\)-stable, and every closure \(W\) of the same exact
seed \(S\) under \(\rho\),
\[
                         |V(W)|\leq |V(U_*)|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Call a pair \((\sigma,X)\) eligible when \(\sigma\) is proper in the sense
of \noderef{IsProperEdgeColoring}, leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored in the
sense of \noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored}, is
\((T,C,\varphi)\)-stable in the sense of \noderef{StableColoring}, and
\(X\) is a \noderef{TashkinovClosure} of the prescribed seed \(S\) under
\(\sigma\).  Thus the assumed pair \((\pi,U)\) is eligible.

Put \(N=|V(G)|\).  For every tree-sequence \(X\), its vertex set
\(V(X)\), as defined in \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}, is a finite subset
of the \(N\)-element vertex set of \(G\).  Consequently
\(|V(X)|\leq N\).

Let
\[
  \mathcal A=\{\,n\in\{0,1,\ldots,N\}:
       \text{there is an eligible pair }(\sigma,X)
       \text{ with }|V(X)|=n\,\}.
\]
This is a finite set.  It is nonempty because the assumed eligible pair
\((\pi,U)\) contributes the integer \(|V(U)|\), which lies in the displayed
range by the preceding cardinality bound.  Hence \(\mathcal A\) has a
largest member \(m\).  Membership of \(m\) in \(\mathcal A\) supplies an
eligible pair \((\pi_*,U_*)\) with \(|V(U_*)|=m\); in particular \(U_*\) is
a closure of the exact seed \(S\), with no change to that seed.

Now let \(\rho\) and \(W\) satisfy all the conditions in the conclusion.
Then \((\rho,W)\) is eligible.  The universal cardinality bound gives
\(|V(W)|\leq N\), so \(|V(W)|\) lies in the range
\(\{0,1,\ldots,N\}\); its eligibility therefore gives
\(|V(W)|\in\mathcal A\).  By maximality of \(m\),
\(|V(W)|\leq m=|V(U_*)|\), which is the required inequality.
\end{proof}
