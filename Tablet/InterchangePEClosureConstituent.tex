\begin{lemma}[Interchangeability on the closure constituent of a PE]
Let $T$ be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree constructed from a
$k$-triple $G,e_0,\varphi$, with arbitrary certified input coloring,
terminal ladder tree $T_n$, terminal generating coloring $\varphi_n$,
and terminal stored-color set $D_n$.  Assume that $T$ has maximum
property and that its terminal extension is a PE.  Let $R_n$ and
$T_n\vee R_n$ be the closure and exact ordered union in a hierarchy
base under $\varphi_n$ (respectively `base.closure' and `base.core'
in Lean).

Assume, uniformly over every certified input coloring and generating
coloring, that every positive-rung ETT $T'$ of rung number $s<n$ with
maximum property satisfies all six lower-rung assertions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V(T')$ is elementary under its generating coloring;
\item under every proper coloring leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored and
stable on its final ladder closure, that closure is interchangeable for
two colors whenever at least one of them is missing there;
\item stability descends at each PE support to the prefix before the
support and to its first-appearance ladder stage, and stored pairs at
distinct PE slots with distinct supports and the same first-appearance
index are disjoint;
\item at a terminal PE, the stored two-color component through the
support meets the terminal ladder tree only at that support;
\item every nonminimum endpoint of a defective boundary color precedes
each later PE support or SE extension vertex allowed by its
first-appearance index; and
\item every proper terminal stable coloring leaving exactly $e_0$
uncolored is a mod-coloring of the ETT.
\end{enumerate}

Let $\sigma_n$ be proper, leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and be
$T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n$-stable.

For arbitrary $\alpha,\beta\in[k]$,
\[
 \alpha\in\overline\sigma_n(R_n)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \alpha,\beta\text{ are $R_n$-interchangeable under }\sigma_n.
\]
All boundaries, trees, and two-color components use the original edge
type, so distinct parallel edges retain their identities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $X=V(T_n)$, $Y=V(R_n)$, and
$U=V(T_n\vee R_n)$, with vertex sets understood as in
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}.  The generating-color field of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} identifies $\varphi_n$ with the last
coloring of the certified series.  Eliminate the last certificate in
\noderef{TashkinovSeries} using the terminal-PE hypothesis.  The PE
constructor of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} supplies the exact support
$v_n$, exact connecting-edge identity $f_n$, and colors
$\gamma_n,\delta_n$.  It also supplies properness and exact
one-uncoloredness of $\varphi_n$, and the equations recording the
terminal $\gamma_n,\delta_n$-switch.  No endpoint pair is substituted
for $f_n$.

The parallel clause of \noderef{HierarchyBase} says that $R_n$ is a
$\varphi_n$-closure of the ordered prefix $T_n(v_n)$, and supplies
an \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} certificate with
\[
                         U=X\cup Y.                       \tag{1}
\]
It also says that the missing-color set of $T_n$ under the base
coloring is its missing-color set under $\varphi_n$.
The two conjuncts of \noderef{CombinedStableColoring}, with stability
expanded as in \noderef{StableColoring}, say that
$\sigma_n$ is $(T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable and simultaneously
$(R_n,\varnothing,\varphi_n)$-stable.  Applying
\noderef{CombinedStableClosurePreserved} gives, for every displayed edge
identity $g$ of either tree,
\[
             \sigma_n(g)=\varphi_n(g),                    \tag{2}
\]
and proves that the same ordered tree $R_n$, with the same edge
identities, is a $\sigma_n$-closure of $T_n(v_n)$.

We now prove conclusion (i).  Let $j=m(v_n)$ be the first ladder
stage containing the support, as represented by
\noderef{ETTFirstAppearanceIndex}.  The ordered-entry equalities in
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} identify the prefix through
$v_n$ with the corresponding prefix of $T_j$.  Apply
\noderef{ExtensionRules} to the current ETT and the full displayed
lower-rung package.  Its first conclusion says that $\sigma_n$ is
stable, with the correct earlier stored set and generating coloring,
both before $v_n$ in the sense of
\noderef{StableColoringBeforeVertex} and on $T_{j-1}$.

We next check the construction, rather than merely naming $R_n$ a
lower-rung ETT.  Each augmentation which constructs the prefix through
$v_n$ has an exact new edge identity and a missing-color witness at an
earlier vertex by \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}.  Unfolding the vertex
and incident-edge clauses of \noderef{StableColoringBeforeVertex},
stability before $v_n$ preserves that witness and the controlled color of
that same edge identity.  Hence every original augmentation can be copied
under $\sigma_n$.  Compose this copied finite sequence with the
$\sigma_n$-closure sequence for $R_n$ obtained above; transitivity of
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} proves that $R_n$ is a closure
of the exact seed prescribed at stage (j-1).

If $j>1$, repeatedly apply \noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor}, beginning
with $T$ and stopping after the certified rung $j-1$ is reached.  Its
array equalities identify the terminal tree and generating coloring at
every step, while its maximum-property conclusion is inherited at every
step.  Hence the resulting displayed ETT has positive rung $j-1<n$,
terminal tree exactly the required $T_j$, and the exact retained input,
coloring, stored-set, kind, support, and connecting-edge data.  Apply
\noderef{Td} to this restricted ETT; its lower hypotheses are obtained
by restricting the displayed universal package once more.  The stable
relation just proved makes $\sigma_n$ a \noderef{ModColoring} of that
ETT.  Its universal extension clause, applied only after the exact seed,
strict extension, and augmentation sequence have been verified, returns
a genuine \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} displayed on
$R_n$.  By
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} it has maximum property.  Lower
assertion (ii), instantiated with its actual replacement input and
generating coloring, makes its final ladder closure interchangeable for
$\alpha,\beta$, since $\alpha$ is missing already on its displayed
initial segment $R_n$.  Expanding \noderef{TreeInterchangeable}, any
two path components meeting $R_n$ also meet that closure, so the
conclusion restricts to $R_n$.

If $j=1$, the composed augmentation sequence starts at the initial
one-edge tree.  Together with the original graph and numerical fields of
\noderef{KTriple}, and the stated properness and exact
one-uncoloredness, it forms the certified initial closure ending at
$R_n$.  The interchangeability clause of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase} gives the same conclusion.  Thus in all
cases
\[
 \alpha\in\overline\sigma_n(R_n)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \alpha,\beta\text{ are }R_n\text{-interchangeable}.       \tag{7}
\]
This proves $i$, including the lower-rung closure instance used in the
paper.
\end{proof}
