\begin{helper}[Retained good-hierarchy reconstruction for stable-change paths]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph with exact edge identities, palette
$[k]$, and distinguished edge $e_0$.  Fix an ETT $\mathcal E$ on $T$
generated by $\varphi$, a \noderef{HierarchyBase} $B$, a
\noderef{HierarchyCounterexample} $C$ on $B$, and a
\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext} for these data.
Write $H=C.\mathit{hierarchy}$, $q=H.\mathit{dividerCount}$,
$n=\mathcal E.\mathit{rounds}$, $T_n$ for its terminal ladder tree,
$D_n$ for its computed terminal stored set, $R=B.\mathit{closure}$,
$A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)$, and
\[
 Y=T|_{\min\{|A|+(|T|-|A|-1),|T|\}}.
\]
Lengths count displayed edges and subtraction is truncated.  Assume
$Y$ elementary under $\varphi$.  Let distinct colors $\alpha,\beta$ be
missing on $Y$, let $Q$ be the full component through a node $z$, with a
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} certificate, and let
$\sigma=\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}(G,\varphi,\alpha,\beta,z)$.
Put $a=T.\mathit{vertex}(\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}(\varphi,T,\alpha))$.
Assume $Q$ disjoint from the full $\alpha,\beta$ component through $a$, and
assume
\[
 (q>0\ \land\ \alpha\in\overline\varphi(H.\mathit{stage}(q)))
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \bigl(q=0\ \land\
   [\,\alpha\in\overline\varphi(T_n)\ \text{or}\
      (\alpha\in\overline\varphi(A),\ \alpha,\beta\notin D_n)\,]\bigr).
\]
If $q>0$, there are an input coloring and an actual maximum ETT
$\mathcal E_A$ on the exact tree $A$ generated by $\sigma$, a hierarchy
base $B_A$ for it, and a good hierarchy $K$ such that:
the terminal extension kind of $\mathcal E_A$ equals that of $\mathcal E$;
$K$ has $q-1$ dividers;
$B_A.\mathit{core}=B.\mathit{core}$ and
$B_A.\mathit{closure}=B.\mathit{closure}$;
for every $0\le j\le q$ its stage is exactly $H.\mathit{stage}(j)$;
for every $0\le j<q$ its computed hierarchy stored set equals that of
$H$ at $j$, and its Gamma pair at every color in that stored set equals
$C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^j_\eta$.
In addition,
\[
 \noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}
 \left(G,\sigma,H.\mathit{stage}(q),
   D_{n,q}\mathop{\textstyle\bigcup}_{\eta}
        C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^{q-1}_\eta\right)
\]
holds, where the union is indexed by $\eta\in D_{n,q}$ and
$D_{n,q}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,q)$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix $q>0$.  The case hypothesis then says that $\alpha$ is missing on
$H.\mathit{stage}(q)$, and
$A=H.\mathit{stage}(q)$ by \noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}.
The lower-rung inputs come from \noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext};
positivity, maximum property, and the original good witness come from
\noderef{HierarchyCounterexample}.
Use \noderef{P2813_T1_BaseControl} to obtain the exact base stability,
missing-set equality on $A$, properness and one-uncoloredness of $\sigma$,
and its outside-recoloring conclusion for each affected missing closed
starred-stage color.  Use \noderef{P2813_T1_DisplayedEdges} to obtain
pointwise equality of every displayed edge value on $A$.
Every retained stage, starred stage, and base constituent lies in $A$,
by \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}, \noderef{HierarchyBase}, and the PE
vertex-union equation of \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion}.  All their vertex
missing sets therefore agree under the two colorings.  Unions of these
equal sets give equality of their tree-missing sets by
\noderef{TreeMissingColors}.

Apply \noderef{GoodHierarchyPrefixRepackage} at the last positive
index $q$ under the old coloring.  It returns a maximum ETT
$\mathcal E^0_A$ on $A$, with the original input, original generating
coloring, the same rung number and the same entire certified series;
a base $B^0_A$ with exactly the original core and closure; a hierarchy
$K^0$ with $q-1$ dividers and exactly the retained original stages and
divider positions; a good witness $g^0$ whose retained stored sets and
Gamma pairs agree with the original ones; and the separate old
terminal minus-closed assertion at stage $q$.  We retain all these
equalities.  In particular equality of the entire series identifies
the old prefix's terminal kind, raw stored sets, terminal ladder tree,
and computed terminal stored set with those of the original ETT.

We describe the exact route under $\sigma$ used to reconstruct $A$.
\noderef{P2813_T1_TerminalRoute} supplies the actual terminal certificate's
route under $\varphi$, either from $T_n$ in PE or from its exact
singleton connecting seed $S=T_n+f$ in SE/RE.
The strict stage chain gives $|T_n|<|A|$.
In SE/RE, $|S|=|T_n|+1\le|A|$; the route makes $S$ an exact prefix
of $T$ by \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment}.
As $A$ is also an exact prefix of $T$, comparison of their entries
makes $S$ an exact prefix of $A$.
In PE $T_n$ is already an exact prefix of $A$.
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} therefore restricts the
appropriate route to $A$.  In either restricted finite chain, the added
edge of every \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation} is a displayed edge of $A$
and is fixed.  Its missing witness lies in $A$ by
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset} and retains its
missing-color set.  Reusing its length and prefix equations gives the
same augmentation under $\sigma$.  Induction on
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} transports the whole route.
In SE/RE the connecting edge and singleton connecting set are unchanged
identities, not new choices.

In non-PE, use the certificate supplied by
\noderef{P2813_T1_PrefixReconstruction}, whose replacement tree is $A$
and has maximum property.  Its
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} agreements identify the
new terminal ladder tree with $T_n$ and the new terminal kind with
the original kind.  Transport the old support position through the
exact equality of terminal ladder trees.  Define the new base with
core $B.\mathit{core}$ and closure $B.\mathit{closure}$.
Its support positivity transports from the old base.  Its terminal
missing-color field is the defining identity under $\sigma$.
Its PE implication is vacuous because the corresponding terminal kind
is non-PE, and its nonparallel field is the old core equality with
$T_n$ transported through the new terminal ladder-tree equality.
These are every field of \noderef{HierarchyBase}.
Copy the old prefix's stage and divider arrays, using the ladder-tree
equality for the base-stage equation and the core equality for the
core-inside-first equation.  The other nesting, strictness, positivity,
increasing-position, and divider-stage fields are identical, by
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}.

In PE, instead apply \noderef{HierarchySplitter} to the old prefix ETT
$\mathcal E^0_A$, its base $B^0_A$, and hierarchy $K^0$, with the
combined stability just obtained and the transported PE route to $A$.
The lower-rung package is applicable because the old prefix's rung
equals the original rung and its maximum property was returned by
prefix repackaging.  Properness and one-uncoloredness of both colorings
are available.  The splitter returns its own corresponding certificate,
maximum witness, replacement base, and replacement hierarchy.
Choose this certificate's own ETT as $\mathcal E_A$, not a certificate
chosen independently by mod-coloring.
The returned \noderef{SameHierarchyStages} fixes the core, divider count,
stage array, divider positions, and PE status; its additional PE
closure equality fixes the closure.  Combining these with the old-prefix
equalities gives precisely the desired core, closure, stages and count.
The corresponding certificate fixes the actual terminal kind, and the
old prefix's entire-series equality fixes it to the original kind.
Thus the stronger terminal-kind equality, not just PE status, is
retained in both branches.

In either branch call the resulting base and hierarchy $B_A,K$.
Correspondence fixes every raw stored set.
The terminal coloring is now $\sigma$, but the terminal ladder tree
has exactly its old missing set.  Expanding
\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors} therefore identifies its computed
terminal stored set with $D_n$.
Every starred stage of $K$ is the identical core or retained stage of
$H$, with the same missing set.  Expanding
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} gives
$D^K_{n,j}=D^H_{n,j}$ at each retained index $j<q$.
The same argument with \noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors} identifies
every retained stage-missing set.

Define $g.\Gamma^j_\eta=g^0.\Gamma^j_\eta$ for every retained index and
every color, including colors outside the stored set.
The agreements returned by prefix repackaging identify these pairs
with the original pairs.  We now check every field of
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} for $g$.

The two-color cardinality and pairwise disjointness fields are the
corresponding old fields, since their stored sets and pairs are equal.
For avoidance fix $j<q$ and a stored color $\eta$.  Its copied Gamma
pair lies in the unchanged stage-missing set.  To exclude a new
occurrence in the segment, take its exact edge index in the retained
next stage $U=H.\mathit{stage}(j+1)\preccurlyeq A$.
Apply \noderef{P2813_T1_PrefixSegmentTransport} with the exact prefixes
$U\preccurlyeq A\preccurlyeq T$ and lower cutoff equal to the length of
the identical starred stage at $j$.
It identifies the new segment window, computed using the totalized
first-missing position in $A$ under $\sigma$, with the old window
computed in $T$ under $\varphi$, exactly as defined by
\noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}.  A new Gamma occurrence would thus be
an old occurrence, contrary to the old avoidance field.
This argument is pointwise on exact edge identities and uses the prefix
cutoff even when $\eta$ has no missing witness on $A$.
It does not assert a generic color-set invariance under Kempe changes.

For unique refresh, each exact stage difference has the same vertex
set in $K$ and the old prefix.  All its vertex missing sets agree.
Its stored set and all compared Gamma pairs agree as well.
Consequently the predicate specifying a refreshed color is identical
under the two presentations; the old existence and uniqueness supply
exactly the new existence and uniqueness.

We check the PE base field separately.  If the original terminal kind
is non-PE, the new exact terminal-kind equality makes this implication
vacuous.  In PE put
$R_0=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,0}}C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^0_\eta$.
The old PE base field supplies an exact boundary identity $f$ of the
core with old color $c\in\overline\varphi(B.\mathit{core})-R_0$:
expand failure of \noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed} through
\noderef{TreeColorSetClosed} and \noderef{BoundaryColorEdges}.
We prove this witness persists.

Suppose some affected color missing on $A$ is closed on some starred
stage.  Choose its largest closed index $r$.  It is missing there too:
if its least missing starred index were later, stage elementarity
(restricted from $Y$ by exact-prefix vertex inclusion and
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}) and
\noderef{GoodHierarchyNewMissingClosed} would make it closed at that
later index.  The outside conclusion of
\noderef{P2813_T1_BaseControl} applies at $r$.  Since the core lies in
that starred stage, \noderef{OutsideKempeRecoloringStable} fixes the
exact core-boundary edge $f$.

Otherwise no affected color missing on $A$ has a closed starred stage.
For either affected color missing on the core, apply the no-closed
clause of \noderef{rutcor}.  The predecessor-elementarity premise comes
from \noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase} at rung one, or from
\noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor} and lower elementarity at larger
rungs; the other prefix-elementarity premise is the assumed one on $Y$.
At the first positive index, this clause places the color in
$\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,1}}\Gamma^0_\eta$.
By \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAntitone}, $D_{n,1}\subseteq D_{n,0}$,
so this union is contained in $R_0$.
Our witness color $c$ is missing on the core but outside $R_0$, and
hence is neither affected color.  The definition
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} therefore fixes $f$ in this branch too.
Missing-set equality on the core makes $c$ missing under $\sigma$,
while its boundary membership depends only on endpoints by
\noderef{BoundaryEdges}.
Stored-set and Gamma equality identify the new reserved union with
$R_0$.  Thus the same exact identity and color witness failure of new
minus-closedness.  This proves the first PE base conjunct explicitly.
The second conjunct is the old lower bound of four on the common
missing colors of $T_n$ and the closure outside $R_0$; both constituent
missing sets and $R_0$ are unchanged, so its cardinality is identical.

Finally consider any original internal stage $H.\mathit{stage}(j)$,
$1\le j\le q$, and its old minus-closed reserve
$\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,j}}\Gamma^{j-1}_\eta$.
Old minus-closedness is a field of \noderef{GoodHierarchy}.
This stage equals \noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}$(H,j)$, so the outside
hypothesis needed in \noderef{P2813_T1_MinusClosedTransport} is exactly
the corresponding conclusion of \noderef{P2813_T1_BaseControl}.
Missing-set equality restricts from $A$.  That transport lemma proves
new minus-closedness with this unchanged reserve.
For $1\le j<q$ these are all the internal minus-closed fields of $K$,
using the equal retained stored sets and pairs.
The separate instance $j=q$ gives precisely the terminal assertion
displayed in the claim, whose reserve is still the original
$D_{n,q}$ and the preceding pair family.
Thus every field of the good witness is proved, including PE
non-minus-closedness and each internal and terminal minus-closed field.
The chosen maximum ETT, base, stages, exact kind, count, stored sets,
and copied Gamma pairs have all the required agreements.
\end{proof}
