\begin{lemma}[Situation 7.2, Subcase 1.1]
Every \noderef{HierarchySituationTwoCaseOneData} is impossible.
Equivalently, in Situation 7.2, Case 1 cannot occur when the color
\(\theta=\varphi_n(e_p)\) is present at \(y_{p-1}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let
\[
 D_{n,q}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(C.\mathit{hierarchy},q),
\qquad
 \Gamma^q=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,q}}
   C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^q_\eta .
\]
If \(\theta\notin\Gamma^q\), apply
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoSubcaseOneOneNonexceptional}, using its
first alternative.

Otherwise, the definition of the finite union supplies a color
\(\eta_h\in D_{n,q}\) with
\(\theta\in\Gamma^q_{\eta_h}\).  If \(\eta_h\) is not missing at
\(y_{p-1}\), the second alternative of
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoSubcaseOneOneNonexceptional} gives a
contradiction.  If it is missing there, all hypotheses of
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoSubcaseOneOneStoredException} hold, and
that lemma gives a contradiction.  These cases exhaust membership in
\(\Gamma^q\), so the Case 1 datum is impossible.
\end{proof}
