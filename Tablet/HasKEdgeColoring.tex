\begin{definition}
A multigraph $G$ has a $k$-edge-coloring if there exists a partial coloring
$\varphi:E(G)\to [k]\cup\{\text{uncolored}\}$ that is both proper and full.
Equivalently, $\varphi$ assigns one of $k$ colors to every edge and distinct
adjacent edges receive different colors.
\end{definition}
