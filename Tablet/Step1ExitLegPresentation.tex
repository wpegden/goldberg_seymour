\begin{definition}
Fix a finite loopless multigraph $G$, a distinguished edge $e_0$, and
integers $n,q$.  A \emph{Step 1 exit-leg presentation of size $(n,q)$}
consists of an arbitrary certified input coloring, a proper generating
coloring $\psi$ leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and an $n$-rung maximum
ETT $\mathcal U$ on an exact terminal tree $U$.  It also contains a
hierarchy base and a good hierarchy $K$ having fewer than $q$ dividers,
such that
\[
 2n+11\leq |\overline\psi(X)|,
 \qquad X=\mathcal U.\mathit{series.tree}(n),
\]
and $U$ is minus-closed for the union of the terminal stored-color set
$D^K_{n,d+1}$, where $d=K.\mathit{dividerCount}$, through the terminal
Gamma family $\Gamma^d$.

Finally, it contains distinct colors $a,b$, with $a$ missing and
singleton-closed on $U$, and two exact support-disjoint
$(U,\psi,\{a,b\})$-exit paths $P_1,P_2$.  Their outside ends $a_1,a_2$
and tree ends $b_1,b_2$ are distinct, the tree ends satisfy
$b_1\prec b_2$, and the data retain exact $b$-colored boundary-edge
identities $f_i$ lying on $P_i$ and incident with $b_i$ for $i=1,2$.
\end{definition}
