\begin{lemma}[Transitivity of corresponding ETT presentations]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph \(G\), a palette of \(k\) colors, a
distinguished edge \(e_0\), an input coloring \(\iota\), an exact ordered
terminal tree \(T\), partial colorings \(\varphi,\sigma,\tau\), and an
extended Tashkinov tree \(\mathcal E_0\) constructed from \(\iota\) on
\(T\) and generated by \(\varphi\).  Suppose
\[
 \mathcal C_0:
 \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}
   (\mathcal E_0,\sigma,T)
\]
is a corresponding presentation, and suppose
\[
 \mathcal C_1:
 \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}
   (\mathcal C_0.\mathit{replacementETT},\tau,T)
\]
is a corresponding presentation relative to the intermediate replacement
ETT.  Then there is a corresponding presentation
\[
 \mathcal C_{10}:
 \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}
   (\mathcal E_0,\tau,T)
\]
relative to the original ETT.  Its replacement input is equal to that of
\(\mathcal C_1\), and its dependent replacement series and replacement ETT
are heterogeneously equal to those of \(\mathcal C_1\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the tree-changing transitivity theorem
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTreeTransTrees} with all three
displayed-tree arguments equal to the given exact ordered tree \(T\).
Its first certificate is then exactly \(\mathcal C_0\), and its second
certificate is exactly \(\mathcal C_1\).  The resulting composite is a
corresponding presentation from \(\mathcal E_0\) to \(\tau\) displayed on
\(T\), and the theorem returns precisely the asserted replacement-input
equality and the two asserted heterogeneous equalities.
\end{proof}
