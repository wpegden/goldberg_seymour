\begin{definition}
A \emph{normal globally maximum triple} consists of a globally maximum
triple
\[
 T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_{n-1},y_{n-1})
\]
together with colors $\alpha\in\overline\varphi(y_0)$ and
$\beta\in\overline\varphi(y_1)$, an integer
$2\le p\le n=|V(T)|$, and a closing edge identity
$q\in E_G(y_{p-1},y_0)$ such that
\[
 P(y_1,e_2,y_2,\ldots,e_{p-1},y_{p-1},q,y_0)
\]
is a simple $(\alpha,\beta)$-chain.  Its chosen height is $p$.  Formally
the chain is stored as a bipartite incidence path of length $2(p-1)$; its
vertex-node support is exactly $\{y_0,\ldots,y_{p-1}\}$ and its edge-node
support is exactly the individual identities
$\{e_2,\ldots,e_{p-1},q\}$.  In particular, the corrected source bound
$p\le n$ permits the trunk to contain every vertex of the globally maximum
tree.
\end{definition}
