\begin{lemma}[Hierarchy preservation under a combined-stable coloring]
Let the last step be a PE.  Suppose $T$ has maximum property and carries
a hierarchy under $\varphi_n$, and let $\sigma_n$ be proper, leave
exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and be
$(T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable.  Keep $G,k,e_0$ fixed but
allow the certified input coloring to vary.  Assume that for every
positive-rung ETT $T'$ with arbitrary certified input coloring, maximum
property, and rung number $s<n$, the following six assertions hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V(T')$ is elementary under its generating coloring
$\varphi'_s$.
\item Under every proper $(T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)$-stable coloring
leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored, $T'_{s+1}$ is interchangeable for any
two colors of which at least one is missing on it.
\item At every PE slot, stability descends to the prefix before the
support and to the ladder stage indexed by the support's first
appearance; stored pairs at distinct PE slots with distinct supports
and equal first-appearance indices are disjoint.
\item At a terminal PE, the stored two-color component through its
support meets the terminal ladder tree only at that support.
\item Every nonminimum tree endpoint of a defective boundary color
precedes each later PE support or SE extension vertex permitted by its
first-appearance index.
\item Every proper terminal stable coloring leaving exactly $e_0$
uncolored is a $\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s$ coloring.
\end{enumerate}
If the displayed terminal tree $T$ can be built from $T_n$ by Tashkinov
augmentation under $\sigma_n$, then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $T$ carries an actual corresponding ETT certificate under
$\sigma_n$ and has maximum property; and
\item the same ordered stages and divider positions form a hierarchy
under $\sigma_n$, with the same $R_n$ and $T_n\vee R_n$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{CombinedStableClosurePreserved}, the two colorings agree on
every displayed identity of $T_n$ and $R_n$, and the same ordered
$R_n$ is a closure of $T_n(v_n)$ under $\sigma_n$.  Hence the hierarchy
base from \noderef{HierarchyBase} can be rebuilt under $\sigma_n$ using
the same supporting position, closure, ordered-union witness, and starred
base tree.

The first conjunct of combined stability is the required
$(T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable relation.  Apply \noderef{Td} to the stated
lower-rung package, maximum property, properness, and unique-uncolored
edge.  Current assertion (vi) follows: $\sigma_n$ is a
$\varphi_n\bmod T_n$ coloring.

The definition \noderef{ModColoring} applies to every eligible ordered
tree strictly above $T_n$.  The initial-segment and strictness fields of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} make $T$ such a strict extension, while
the displayed augmentation hypothesis is precisely the PE eligibility
alternative.  Therefore an actual
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate exists on the
same terminal tree $T$ under $\sigma_n$.
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} transfers maximum property to that
replacement ETT.

Correspondence identifies all ladder trees and the terminal PE kind.
Use the rebuilt hierarchy base and copy the old divider count, every
ordered stage, and every divider position.  Each proper-prefix,
divider-prefix, and core-inside-first proof is unchanged because it is a
statement only about those same ordered tree-sequences.  This constructs
a \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} for the replacement ETT.  By construction
the bases, stage families, and divider positions satisfy every equality
in \noderef{SameHierarchyStages}, proving the second conclusion.
\end{proof}
