\begin{definition}
For a hierarchy counterexample with $q$ dividers, define its finite
counterexample measure by
\[
 \mathfrak m(T)=\bigl(p(T),\ |T|-|T_{n,q}^{*}|\bigr).
\]
Since a tree-sequence has one more vertex than displayed edges, the
second coordinate is equivalently the difference of the two sequence
lengths.  Counterexamples are minimized lexicographically: first the
path number as in (6.4), and, subject to that, the terminal-tail size as
in (6.5).
\end{definition}
