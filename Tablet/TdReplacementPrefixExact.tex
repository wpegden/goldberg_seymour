\begin{helper}[Exact replacement prefix through the penultimate ladder tree]
Let (T) be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree generated by
\[
 \mathcal T=\{(T_i,\varphi_{i-1},S_{i-1},F_{i-1},\Theta_{i-1}):
                   1\leq i\leq n+1\},
\]
and suppose that (T) has the maximum property.  Keep the graph (G),
palette size (k), and distinguished uncolored edge (e_0) fixed, but
allow the certified input coloring to vary arbitrarily.  Assume explicitly
the following six assertions for every positive-rung ETT (T') on these
fixed data, with arbitrary certified input coloring \(\varphi'_0\), maximum
property, and rung number (s<n), writing \(\varphi'_s\) for its generating
coloring and \(\mathcal T'\) for its certified series:
\begin{enumerate}
\item (V(T')) is elementary with respect to \(\varphi'_s\).
\item Under every proper \((T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (T'_{s+1}) is interchangeable for
any pair of colors of which at least one is missing on (T'_{s+1}).
\item For each PE slot (a\leq s) with supporting vertex (u_a) and
(m(u_a)=b), every proper \((T'_a,D'_a,\varphi'_a)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored is
\((T'(u_a)-u_a,D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  When (b>1), it is
also \((T'_{b-1},D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  Moreover, the stored
color pairs at two distinct PE slots with distinct supporting vertices
and the same first-appearance index are disjoint.
\item If the terminal slot is PE with supporting vertex (u_s) and
stored colors \(\gamma'_s,\delta'_s\), then under every proper
\((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving precisely (e_0)
uncolored, the \((\gamma'_s,\delta'_s)\)-component through (u_s)
contains exactly the one vertex (u_s) of (T'_s).
\item If \(\tau\) is any proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable
coloring leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (d) is defective on
(T'_s) under \(\tau\), and (x) is a nonminimum vertex of
\(I[\partial_{\tau,d}(T'_s)]\), then
(x\preceq_{T'}u_a) for every PE supporting vertex or SE extension
vertex (u_a) with (m(x)\leq a\leq s).
\item Every proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving
precisely (e_0) uncolored is a \(\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s\) coloring.
\end{enumerate}

Then for every proper stable terminal coloring \(\sigma_n\) leaving
exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, there are a proper coloring
\(\sigma_{n-1}\), a replacement input coloring, and a certified
Tashkinov series with \(n-1\) rungs whose exact final tree is \(T_n\)
and whose exact final coloring is \(\sigma_{n-1}\).  In an SE or RE
terminal slot, \(\sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n\).  In a PE terminal slot there
are the recorded support vertex and ordered colors \(v_n,\gamma_n,\delta_n\)
and
\[
 \sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n/P_{v_n}(\gamma_n,\delta_n,\sigma_n).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let the final positive certificate be fixed.  In an SE or RE slot put
\(\sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n\).  In a PE slot use the recorded support
vertex and colors and undo the terminal component switch.  Current assertion
(iv), obtained from \noderef{ExtensionBase}, says that the switched
component meets \(T_n\) exactly at its support vertex.  Therefore
\noderef{ParallelExtensionUndoStable} proves that the undone coloring is
proper, leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, and is stable on \(T_n\)
relative to the preceding coloring and stored set.  In the SE and RE cases
the corresponding stability follows from the constructor equations,
\noderef{HkuMissingStoredMonotone}, and transitivity \noderef{sc2}.

Copy the old closure leading to \(T_n\) edge by edge under
\(\sigma_{n-1}\).  At every copied augmentation, the missing witness is
a vertex of \(T_n\), so vertex stability preserves its missing color.
The new edge is an exact displayed edge identity of \(T_n\);
\noderef{HkuTreeColorsControlled} and the edge clause of stability preserve
its color.  Thus each copied step is again a
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}.  If a boundary edge of \(T_n\) had a
color missing on \(T_n\), the reverse edge criterion \noderef{sc1}
would give the identical forbidden boundary edge in the old closure.
Hence the copied terminal tree is closed.

If \(n>1\), apply \noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at rung \(n-1\).
It yields the actual restricted ETT with exact terminal tree \(T_n\),
exact old generating coloring, inherited maximum property, and all retained
step certificates.  Apply lower assertion (vi) to the stable coloring just
constructed.  Its universal mod-coloring clause, instantiated with the copied
closure, supplies a corresponding replacement prefix.  Normalize only its
last closure witness to the exact copied closure; the ladder stages on which
maximum property depends are unchanged.

If \(n=1\), no positive lower ETT exists.  Retain the original one-edge
seed, use \(\sigma_0\) as the replacement input, and use the copied
closure as the initial constructor.  Properness and exact one-edge
uncoloredness give the coloring fields of the original
\noderef{KTriple}; its graph, criticality, and chromatic-index fields are
independent of the coloring.  This directly constructs a zero-rung
Tashkinov series ending at the exact \(T_1=T_n\).  The two cases prove
the asserted existential package, including the displayed formulas for
\(\sigma_{n-1}\).
\end{proof}
