\begin{helper}[Exact replacement prefix through the penultimate ladder tree]
Let (T) be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree generated by
\[
 \mathcal T=\{(T_i,\varphi_{i-1},S_{i-1},F_{i-1},\Theta_{i-1}):
                   1\leq i\leq n+1\},
\]
and suppose that (T) has the maximum property.  Keep the graph (G),
palette size (k), and distinguished uncolored edge (e_0) fixed, but
allow the certified input coloring to vary arbitrarily.  Assume explicitly
the following six assertions for every positive-rung ETT (T') on these
fixed data, with arbitrary certified input coloring \(\varphi'_0\), maximum
property, and rung number (s<n), writing \(\varphi'_s\) for its generating
coloring and \(\mathcal T'\) for its certified series:
\begin{enumerate}
\item (V(T')) is elementary with respect to \(\varphi'_s\).
\item Under every proper \((T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (T'_{s+1}) is interchangeable for
any pair of colors of which at least one is missing on (T'_{s+1}).
\item For each PE slot (a\leq s) with supporting vertex (u_a) and
(m(u_a)=b), every proper \((T'_a,D'_a,\varphi'_a)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored is
\((T'(u_a)-u_a,D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  When (b>1), it is
also \((T'_{b-1},D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  Moreover, the stored
color pairs at two distinct PE slots with distinct supporting vertices
and the same first-appearance index are disjoint.
\item If the terminal slot is PE with supporting vertex (u_s) and
stored colors \(\gamma'_s,\delta'_s\), then under every proper
\((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving precisely (e_0)
uncolored, the \((\gamma'_s,\delta'_s)\)-component through (u_s)
contains exactly the one vertex (u_s) of (T'_s).
\item If \(\tau\) is any proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable
coloring leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (d) is defective on
(T'_s) under \(\tau\), and (x) is a nonminimum vertex of
\(I[\partial_{\tau,d}(T'_s)]\), then
(x\preceq_{T'}u_a) for every PE supporting vertex or SE extension
vertex (u_a) with (m(x)\leq a\leq s).
\item Every proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving
precisely (e_0) uncolored is a \(\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s\) coloring.
\end{enumerate}

Then every proper \((T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)\)-stable coloring \(\sigma_n\)
leaving exactly \(e_0\) uncolored admits a proper coloring
\(\sigma_{n-1}\), also leaving exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, such that
\[
 \sigma_{n-1}\text{ is }(T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})\text{-stable}.
 \tag{A}
\]
In an SE or RE terminal slot, \(\sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n\).  In a PE
terminal slot there are the exact recorded support vertex and ordered
colors \(v_n,\gamma_n,\delta_n\), and
\[
 \sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n/P_{v_n}(\gamma_n,\delta_n,\sigma_n).
\]

The following exact reconstruction package also exists.
If \(n=1\), there are a replacement input coloring and an actual
certified zero-rung Tashkinov series whose final ordered tree is exactly
\(T_1=T_n\) and whose final coloring is exactly \(\sigma_0=\sigma_{n-1}\).

If \(n>1\), there is an actual certified restriction \(R\) of the
original ETT to rung \(n-1\).  Its displayed tree and generating coloring
are exactly \(T_n\) and \(\varphi_{n-1}\), it has the maximum property,
and, at every retained index, its ordered trees, colorings, raw stored
sets, exact connecting-edge sets, extension kinds, and computed sets
\(D_i\) agree pointwise with those of the original series.  There is a
corresponding replacement-prefix certificate for
\((\sigma_{n-1},T_n)\) relative to \(R\) whose certified replacement
series has exact final ordered tree \(T_n\) and exact final coloring
\(\sigma_{n-1}\), and whose replacement ETT has the maximum property.
In particular, the certificate itself retains all ordered ladder trees,
raw stored sets, exact connecting-edge sets, and extension kinds; every
one of its colorings is proper and leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored; and
each positive-stage replacement coloring is stable relative to the
corresponding original restricted stage.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let \(p=n-1\), so the preceding stored set and coloring are
\(D_p\) and \(\varphi_p\).  Inspect the positive terminal certificate in
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  Its constructor is exactly one of RE, SE,
or PE.

In the RE case define \(\sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n\).  The RE constructor gives
\(\varphi_n=\varphi_{n-1}\).  The first inclusion in
\noderef{HkuMissingStoredMonotone} says
\[
 \overline\varphi_{n-1}(T_n)\cup D_{n-1}
 \subseteq \overline\varphi_n(T_n)\cup D_n.
\]
Thus the edge clause of the assumed terminal stability applies to every
edge controlled by the left side, by the definition
\noderef{StableColoring}, while the vertex clause of that definition gives
the same missing sets as \(\varphi_n=\varphi_{n-1}\) at every vertex of
\(T_n\).
This proves (A) in the RE case.

In the SE case again define \(\sigma_{n-1}=\sigma_n\).  The SE constructor
of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} identifies \(\varphi_n\) with its selected
coloring and states that this selected coloring is
\((T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})\)-stable.  Its vertex clause identifies the
two reference missing-color sets vertex by vertex, by
\noderef{StableColoring}; the union definition in
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} therefore identifies their tree-missing-color
sets on \(T_n\).  Combining that equality with the same monotonicity
inclusion, and using both clauses of \noderef{StableColoring}, shows that
\(\sigma_n\) is \((T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_n)\)-stable.  Transitivity in
\noderef{sc2}, first from \(\varphi_{n-1}\) to \(\varphi_n\) and then to
\(\sigma_n\), proves (A).  Properness and the exact one-uncolored-edge
condition in these two cases are the corresponding hypotheses on
\(\sigma_n\).

Finally suppose the terminal kind is PE.  The exact kind, support, gamma,
and delta equations in \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} identify its recorded
data as \(v_n,\gamma_n,\delta_n\).  The lower six-assertion package and the
maximum property meet precisely the hypotheses of \noderef{ExtensionBase};
its conclusion says that the \((\gamma_n,\delta_n)\)-component of \(v_n\)
under \(\sigma_n\) meets \(T_n\) only at \(v_n\).  Apply
\noderef{ParallelExtensionUndoStable}.  With
\[
 \sigma_{n-1}:=
 \sigma_n/P_{v_n}(\gamma_n,\delta_n,\sigma_n),
\]
that lemma gives properness, the exact one-uncolored-edge condition, and
exactly stability (A).  We have therefore constructed \(\sigma_{n-1}\)
and proved all common assertions in all three exhaustive cases.

We next reconstruct the last old closure under \(\sigma_{n-1}\).  The
vertex clause of (A), namely the second clause of
\noderef{StableColoring}, preserves every vertexwise missing-color witness
on every prefix of \(T_n\); the union characterization in
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} lifts these equalities to the corresponding
tree-missing-color sets.  Every edge added in the old closure is an exact
displayed edge identity of \(T_n\).  Its augmentation attachment makes it
incident with the preceding prefix by the attachment clause of
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}, hence with \(T_n\), in the sense of
\noderef{TreeIncidentEdge}.  By \noderef{HkuTreeColorsControlled}, its old
color lies in the set controlled by (A), so the edge clause of (A) from
\noderef{StableColoring} preserves that color.  Consequently every old
one-edge augmentation is again a \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation} under
\(\sigma_{n-1}\), with the same ordered tree and edge identity.  In an SE
or RE slot, \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge} records the exact connecting
edge and its incident attachment, so the same stability edge clause first
preserves that edge and then every closure edge; in a PE slot it applies
directly from the preceding tree.  The reflexive-transitive construction
in \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} forms and composes these copied
steps into the required augmentation sequence to the exact ordered tree
\(T_n\).  Moreover, by \noderef{BoundaryEdges}, an edge on the boundary of
\(T_n\) has exactly one end in its vertex set and is therefore incident
with \(T_n\) by \noderef{TreeIncidentEdge}.  If such an edge had under
\(\sigma_{n-1}\) a color missing on \(T_n\), the reverse implication of
the stability criterion \noderef{sc1} would give the same edge and color
under \(\varphi_{n-1}\), contradicting that the old terminal tree is a
closure.  Thus the copied augmentation sequence is a
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} ending exactly at \(T_n\).

Assume first that \(n>1\).  Apply \noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at
rung \(n-1\).  It returns an actual restricted ETT \(R\) whose displayed
tree is exactly the old tree at index \(n\), namely \(T_n\), whose
generating coloring is exactly \(\varphi_{n-1}\), and whose rung number is
\(n-1\).  Its statement also gives the maximum property and, pointwise,
equality of every retained ordered tree, coloring, raw stored set, exact
connecting-edge set, extension kind, and computed stored set with the
corresponding original entry.  These are exactly the restriction clauses
asserted in the conclusion.

Restrict (A) from \(T_n\) to the preceding ladder tree of \(R\).  Ordered
initial-segment containment, as defined in
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}, preserves all relevant vertices and
exact displayed edge entries.  Hence \noderef{TreeIncidentEdge} makes every
edge incident with the smaller tree incident with \(T_n\), and the union
definition in \noderef{TreeMissingColors} makes its tree-missing-color set
a subset of that of \(T_n\).  The two clauses of
\noderef{StableColoring} therefore restrict directly.  The sixth lower-rung
hypothesis, applied to \(R\), now states that
\(\sigma_{n-1}\) is a mod-coloring of \(R\).  In the universal clause of
\noderef{ModColoring}, take the strict extension to be the exact \(T_n\)
and use the copied closure (or the copied exact connecting edge followed by
that closure) as the eligibility witness.  We obtain a
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate for
\((\sigma_{n-1},T_n)\) relative to \(R\).

It remains only to ensure that the certified replacement series itself,
not merely its displayed ETT, ends at the exact tree \(T_n\).  Keep all
its data through tree index \(n-1\) and replace its last output tree by the
copied closure \(T_n\).  At the last certified slot retain the same
constructor and all of its coloring, choice, and history witnesses.  In the
SE and RE cases, equality of the singleton connecting-edge sets fixes the
exact connecting edge; choose the copied one-edge adjoined seed and its
exact-adjoin certificate of \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}, then use the
copied closure from that seed, whose successive steps are formed and
composed as in \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}.  In the PE case no
connecting-edge seed is needed, so only the terminal closure field is
replaced.  All earlier certificates are unchanged.  Rebuild the displayed
ETT according to \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} with this normalized
series and the reflexive final-prefix relation at \(T_n\), supplied by
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}.  This produces a corresponding
certificate whose replacement series has exact final tree \(T_n\).  Its
final-color equation is the certificate's defining equation, hence its
final coloring is exactly \(\sigma_{n-1}\).  Since the normalization
changes none of the ordered ladder trees, raw stored sets, connecting-edge
sets, kinds, or colorings, all properness, one-uncoloredness, and
positive-stage stability fields of the corresponding certificate remain
valid.  Finally apply
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} to the lower mod-coloring fact, this
normalized certificate, and the maximum property of \(R\).  It gives the
maximum property of the replacement ETT, completing the \(n>1\) branch.

If \(n=1\), a positive-rung restricted ETT does not exist.  Retain the
original one-edge seed and use \(\sigma_0=\sigma_{n-1}\) as the replacement
input coloring.  The graph, criticality, chromatic-index, and degree fields
of the original \noderef{KTriple} are unchanged, while properness and the
exact one-uncolored-edge condition proved above supply its two coloring
fields.  Use the copied closure from the seed to exact \(T_1=T_n\) in the
initial constructor of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}; set the initial raw
stored and connecting-edge sets to empty and every initial optional field
to absent, as that constructor requires.  By the definition
\noderef{TashkinovSeries}, this single initial certificate is an actual
zero-rung certified series.  Its final tree is the copied exact \(T_n\),
and its sole, hence final, coloring is \(\sigma_0=\sigma_{n-1}\).  This is
the stated direct \(n=1\) branch and finishes the proof.
\end{proof}
