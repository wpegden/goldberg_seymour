\begin{lemma}
Deleting every edge identity lowers the chromatic index of $G^{\circ}$ if
and only if deleting every edge identity lowers the chromatic index of $G$.
Thus criticality in the sense of \noderef{Critical} is equivalent for $G$
and its incident core.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix the original edge identity $e$.  Deleting $e$ is restriction to
$E\setminus\{e\}$ in both graphs.  Applying the
restricted equality in \noderef{IncidentCoreChromaticIndex} to this edge
set, and its full equality to the undeleted graphs, gives
\[
 \chi'(G^{\circ}-e)=\chi'(G-e)
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \chi'(G^{\circ})=\chi'(G).
\]
Consequently the strict inequality on the core side holds exactly when the
strict inequality on the original side holds.  Quantifying this equivalence
over all $e$ and using \noderef{Critical} proves the claimed equivalence of
criticality.
\end{proof}
