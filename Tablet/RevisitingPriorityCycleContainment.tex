\begin{helper}[Revisiting priority contains earlier PE cycles]
In a certified Tashkinov series, let \(t\) be a PE slot with colors
\(\gamma_t,\delta_t\), and let \(n>t\) be a later PE slot.  Suppose that
for every vertex \(v\) of the preceding tree at \(t\), apart from the
chosen support, the full \((\gamma_t,\delta_t)\)-component under the
current coloring at \(t\) is a cycle.  Then there is a least slot
\(\epsilon\), with \(t<\epsilon\le n\), whose kind is not RE; every
strictly intermediate slot is RE; and every vertex-node of every one of
these cycles lies in the preceding tree at \(\epsilon\).
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Because slot \(n\) is PE, it is not RE, so the finite set of non-RE
slots strictly after \(t\) is nonempty.  Choose its least member
\(\epsilon\).  Then \(t<\epsilon\le n\), its kind is not RE, and
minimality makes every strictly intermediate slot RE.  At an RE slot,
the current-coloring field of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} identifies
the new coloring with the preceding one.  Composing these equalities
over the finite interval gives
\(\varphi_t=\varphi_{\epsilon-1}\).

Fix \(v\) in the tree at \(t\), distinct from the chosen support, and
choose the exact \noderef{TwoColorCycle} whose support is its full
component, as supplied by \noderef{TwoColorComponentCycle}.  Suppose a
vertex-node \(w\) on this cycle were outside the preceding tree at
\(\epsilon\).  The ordered-extension fields in the intervening
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} certificates put \(v\) inside that tree.
Use the coloring equality above to regard the same exact cycle as a
cycle under \(\varphi_{\epsilon-1}\).  Traverse it from \(v\) toward
\(w\), stop at the first outside vertex, and call the immediately
preceding exact edge \(f\), with inside endpoint \(a\) and outside
endpoint \(b\).  By \noderef{BoundaryEdges}, \(f\) is an exact boundary
identity of the tree at \(\epsilon\).  Reversing the initial segment
from \(f\) back to \(v\) gives a simple return path on the cycle, all of
whose vertex-nodes lie in the current tree by the choice of the first
outside vertex.

These objects fill every field of
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate} at index \(\epsilon-1\).
Take its earlier slot to be the positive slot \(t\); the strict index
inequality supplies positivity and the required bound.  The given PE,
gamma, and delta equations are its earlier-slot fields.  Minimality of
\(\epsilon\) supplies every intervening-RE field.  The cycle meets the
earlier tree at \(v\) and leaves the current tree at \(w\).  The edge
\(f\), endpoints \(a,b\), return vertex \(v\), and reversed initial
segment supply respectively the boundary, endpoint, meeting, and three
return-path clauses.

Now inspect the certified constructor at slot \(\epsilon\) in
\noderef{TashkinovSeries}.  It is not initial because \(\epsilon>t\).
Its kind is not RE, so it cannot be the RE constructor.  Both remaining
SE and PE constructors of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} assert that no
revisiting certificate at the preceding index is inhabited, contradicting
the certificate just constructed.  Thus no such outside vertex \(w\)
exists.  The argument applies to every indicated \(v\), proving the
claimed containment.
\end{proof}
