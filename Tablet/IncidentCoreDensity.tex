\begin{lemma}
The density ceiling of the incident core is at most that of the original
graph:
\[
 \lceil\Gamma(G^{\circ})\rceil\leq\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a finite core vertex set $U$ that is eligible in the sense of
\noderef{EligibleOddSet}; thus $|U|\geq3$ and $|U|$ is odd.  Let $\bar U$
be its image in $V(G)$.  By \noderef{IncidentCoreInternalEdges},
$|\bar U|=|U|$ and the internal-edge finsets agree identity by identity.
Consequently $\bar U$ is also eligible and
\[
 \frac{2|E_{G^{\circ}}(U)|}{|U|-1}
 =\frac{2|E_G(\bar U)|}{|\bar U|-1}.
\]
By \noderef{DensityValue}, this says that every density value contributed
to the maximum for $G^{\circ}$ is also contributed to the maximum for $G$.
The additional value $0$ in \noderef{Density} is common to both maxima.
Therefore $\Gamma(G^{\circ})\leq\Gamma(G)$.  The least-natural-upper-bound
operation in \noderef{DensityCeil} is monotone: every natural upper bound of
$\Gamma(G)$ is also an upper bound of the smaller rational number
$\Gamma(G^{\circ})$.  Taking the least such bounds yields the displayed
inequality.
\end{proof}
