\begin{definition}
Fix normal globally maximum data $N$ for $(T,e_0,\varphi)$, and let its
height be $p$.  A \emph{balanced candidate} for $N$ is a Tashkinov
tree-sequence $S$ for the same pair $(e_0,\varphi)$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $|V(S)|$ is odd;
\item the exact tree-sequence \noderef{NormalTripleTrunk}$(N)$ is an
entry-by-entry initial segment of $S$ in the sense of
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment};
\item every color in $\noderef{TreeUsedColors}(\varphi,S)$ belongs to
$\noderef{TreeUsedColors}(\varphi,T)$; and
\item whenever $p<2j<S.\operatorname{length}+1$, the two exact edge
identities of $S$ in zero-based positions $2j-2$ and $2j-1$ have the same
$\varphi$-color.
\end{enumerate}
The Tashkinov condition in the first sentence has its exact meaning from
\noderef{TashkinovTree}; in particular it is part of the candidate data,
not a consequence merely of having the same vertex set as $T$.
\end{definition}
