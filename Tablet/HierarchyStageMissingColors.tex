\begin{definition}
For a hierarchy, write
\[
 M_{n,0}=\overline{\varphi_n}(T_n),\qquad
 M_{n,j}=\overline{\varphi_n}(T_{n,j})\quad(1\leq j\leq q).
\]
These are the stage-missing color sets from which the corresponding
$\Gamma$-pairs are selected in condition (i) of a good hierarchy.
\end{definition}
