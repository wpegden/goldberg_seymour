\begin{definition}
For an extended Tashkinov tree \(T\) with rung number \(n\), certified by
a Tashkinov series whose stored colorings are
\(\varphi_0,\varphi_1,\ldots,\varphi_n\), the coloring sequence of \(T\)
is the indexed family
\((\varphi_0,\varphi_1,\ldots,\varphi_n)\) itself.
\end{definition}
