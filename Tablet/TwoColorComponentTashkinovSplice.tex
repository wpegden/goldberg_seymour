\begin{lemma}[Splicing an exact two-color component onto a tree]
Let \(G\) be a finite loopless multigraph with exact edge type \(E\), let
\(S\) be an ordered tree-sequence rooted at \(e_0\), and let \(\mu\) be a
proper partial \(k\)-edge-coloring.  Let \(\alpha\ne\beta\) both belong to
\(\overline\mu(S)\).  Suppose that \(y\notin V(S)\) and that the
\((\alpha,\beta)\)-component through the vertex-node \(y\) contains a
vertex-node belonging to \(V(S)\).

Then there are a vertex \(u\in V(S)\), whose vertex-node belongs to that
same \((\alpha,\beta)\)-component, and a strict ordered extension
\(S'\) of \(S\) such that \(S'\) is obtained from \(S\) by a
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}, its last displayed vertex is
\(y\), and every new exact edge and new vertex-node belongs to the
\noderef{TwoColorComponent} through \(y\).  Every new edge has color
\(\alpha\) or \(\beta\), and every new vertex lies outside \(V(S)\).
Moreover, if the new entries have indices \(|S|\le r<|S'|\), then
\[
 \operatorname{ends}(S'.\operatorname{edge}(r))=
 \begin{cases}
   \{u,S'.\operatorname{vertex}(r+1)\},&r=|S|,\\
   \{S'.\operatorname{vertex}(r),S'.\operatorname{vertex}(r+1)\},
       &r>|S|.
 \end{cases}                                                \tag{1}
\]
Thus the appended entries are the reversed segment from the first
intersection with \(S\) to \(y\), and (1) retains the identity of every
parallel edge rather than retaining only its endpoint pair.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{TwoColorComponentNearestSegment}.  It gives a positive
integer \(m\), an injective vertex list
\(v_0,\ldots,v_m\), and an injective exact-edge list
\(f_0,\ldots,f_{m-1}\).  Its conclusions say that \(v_0\in V(S)\), that
the vertex-node \(v_0\) belongs to the component through \(y\), that
\(v_m=y\), that every later vertex is outside \(V(S)\), and that all the
vertex, edge, color, endpoint, and old-edge-disjointness conditions of
\noderef{TashkinovAlternatingSegmentAppend} hold.

Apply that append lemma using the two assumed tree-missing colors
\(\alpha\) and \(\beta\).  It returns a strict exact extension \(S'\),
the finite Tashkinov augmentation sequence from \(S\) to \(S'\), the last
vertex identity \(S'.\operatorname{vertex}(|S'|)=v_m=y\), and all the
component, outside-tree, color, and endpoint conclusions in (1).  Taking
\(u=v_0\) supplies both \(u\in V(S)\) and the newly stated membership of
its vertex-node in the component, so every conclusion follows.
\end{proof}
