\begin{lemma}[The first PE hierarchy stage properly extends the core]
If a good hierarchy has terminal kind PE and its terminal tree $T$ is
closed under $\varphi_n$ for every color missing on $T$, then
\[
                     T_{n,1}\ne T_n\vee R_n.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $C=T_n\vee R_n=T_{n,0}^{*}$ and
$\Gamma^0=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,0}}\Gamma^0_\eta$.  Suppose that
$T_{n,1}=C$.  Clause (iv) of \noderef{GoodHierarchy} says that $C$ is
not $(\Gamma^0)^{-}$-closed.

Suppose first that the divider count $q$ is positive.  Clause (v) of
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} at $j=1$ says that
$T_{n,1}$ is
$(\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,1}}\Gamma^0_\eta)^{-}$-closed.  Equality of
$T_{n,1}$ and $C$ also identifies the starred stages
$T_{n,1}^{*}=T_{n,1}$ and $T_{n,0}^{*}=C$ from
\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}.  The two subtractions in
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} therefore have the same stored set and
the same missing-color set, so $D_{n,1}=D_{n,0}$.
The reserved unions are therefore equal, contradicting clause (iv).

Suppose now that $q=0$.  The terminal-stage field of
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} gives $T_{n,1}=T$.  Hence the assumed
equality yields $C=T$.  By hypothesis, $T$ is closed for every color in
$\overline{\varphi_n}(T)$.  Every color in
$\overline{\varphi_n}(T)-\Gamma^0$ lies in that set, so expanding
\noderef{TreeColorSetClosed} and \noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}
shows that $T$, and therefore $C$, is $(\Gamma^0)^{-}$-closed.  This
again contradicts clause (iv).  Both possible divider-count cases are
impossible, proving the asserted strictness.
\end{proof}
