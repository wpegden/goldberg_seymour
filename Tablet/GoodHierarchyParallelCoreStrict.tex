\begin{lemma}[The first PE hierarchy stage properly extends the core]
If a good hierarchy has terminal kind PE, then
\[
                     T_{n,1}\ne T_n\vee R_n.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $C=T_n\vee R_n=T_{n,0}^{*}$ and suppose that $T_{n,1}=C$.
Clause (iv) of \noderef{GoodHierarchy} says that $C$ is not
$(\Gamma^0)^{-}$-closed.

If the hierarchy has a divider, clause (v) at $j=1$ says that
$T_{n,1}$ is
$(\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,1}}\Gamma^0_\eta)^{-}$-closed.  Equality of
$T_{n,1}$ and $C$ gives equality of their missing-color sets, so
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} gives $D_{n,1}=D_{n,0}$.
The reserved unions are therefore equal, contradicting clause (iv).

If there is no divider, $T_{n,1}$ is the terminal tree $T$.  The PE
constructor in \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} makes $T$ a closure under
$\varphi_n$, hence it is closed for every missing color.  Under the
assumed equality the core $C$ is therefore, in particular,
$(\Gamma^0)^{-}$-closed, again contradicting clause (iv).  Both cases
are impossible, proving (5.8).
\end{proof}
