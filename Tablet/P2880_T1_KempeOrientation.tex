\begin{lemma}[Orientation of an unordered Kempe pair]
For a loopless multigraph, a partial edge coloring \(\mu\), and distinct
colors \(\alpha,\beta\), reversing their order leaves the
\noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph}, every \noderef{TwoColorComponent},
every \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}, and the
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} predicate unchanged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The incidence adjacency predicate uses the symmetric disjunction that
an edge has color \(\alpha\) or color \(\beta\). Commuting that
disjunction proves equality of graphs and therefore of their connected
components, by \noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph} and
\noderef{TwoColorComponent}. Outside the component both swap formulas
fix the edge. Inside it an old \(\alpha\)-edge becomes \(\beta\),
an old \(\beta\)-edge becomes \(\alpha\), and every other value
is fixed; distinctness makes these cases exclusive. Thus
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} is unchanged. The endpoint and
neighbor-set conditions in \noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} concern the
same graph and component, and color distinctness is symmetric.
\end{proof}
