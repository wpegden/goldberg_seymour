\begin{definition}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite multigraph, let $\varphi$ be a partial
$k$-edge-coloring, and let $X\subseteq V$.  The set $X$ is closed with
respect to $\varphi$ if no color missing at any vertex of $X$ is assigned by
$\varphi$ to any boundary-edge identity in $\partial_G(X)$.
\end{definition}
