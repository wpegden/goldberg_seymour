\begin{definition}
Two good-hierarchy witnesses are the \emph{same good hierarchy} when
their underlying hierarchy presentations have the same stages and
dividers in the sense of \noderef{SameHierarchyStages}, and their
dependent Gamma families are equal after transporting across the common
divider count.  In particular, the PE closure is the same when it is
part of the starred base, while an unused non-PE closure field is not
constrained.  Thus “remains good with the same Gamma sets” has a literal
formal meaning without adding non-paper data.
\end{definition}
