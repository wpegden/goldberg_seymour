\begin{definition}
Two good-hierarchy witnesses are the \emph{same good hierarchy} when
their underlying hierarchy presentations have the same stages and
dividers in the sense of \noderef{SameHierarchyStages}.  Transport each
stage index $j$ across the resulting equality of divider counts.  At
every transported pair of stages, the corresponding sets $D_{n,j}$
from \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} must be equal, and
$\Gamma^j_\eta$ must be equal for every color $\eta$ in this common
set.  No equality is imposed on values of the total Lean Gamma functions
at colors outside $D_{n,j}$, because those values are not Gamma sets in
the paper.  In particular, the PE closure is the same when it is part of
the starred base, while unused closure fields in the common non-PE case
are not constrained.  Thus “remains good with the same Gamma sets” means
exactly equality on all and only the paper-defined Gamma indices.
\end{definition}
