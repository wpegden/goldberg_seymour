\begin{lemma}[Initial reserved pairs in the non-PE cases]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung ETT with rung number $n$, terminal
ladder tree $T_n$, generating coloring $\varphi_n$, and terminal extension
kind different from PE.  Fix a \noderef{HierarchyBase} $B$ and a
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} $H$ over $B$, and put
\[
 M=\noderef{TreeMissingColors}(\varphi_n,T_n),\qquad
 D=D^H_{n,0}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,0).
\]
If $|M|\geq2n+11$, then there is a total family of finite color sets
$\Gamma_\eta$, constrained at every $\eta\in D$, such that
\[
 |\Gamma_\eta|=2,\qquad \Gamma_\eta\subseteq M,
 \qquad
 \Gamma_\eta\cap\Gamma_\theta=\varnothing
 \quad(\eta\ne\theta\text{ in }D).
\]
The family is total only to match the Lean representation; the conclusions
impose conditions exactly at the paper indices $\eta\in D$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The stagewise estimate \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsCardinality} gives
$|D|\leq n$.  Put $d=|D|$.  The assumed large-branch bound gives
\[
                         |M|\geq2n+11\geq2d.                  \tag{1}
\]
Choose any $2d$-element subset $R$ of $M$; it exists by (1).  Enumerate
the $d$ elements of $D$ and the $2d$ elements of $R$.  For the $i$-th
member $\eta$ of $D$, let $\Gamma_\eta$ be the two colors in positions
$2i$ and $2i+1$ of the enumeration of $R$, and put
$\Gamma_\eta=\varnothing$ when $\eta\notin D$.

Every indexed pair has exactly two elements.  Distinct indices receive
disjoint two-position blocks, so their pairs are disjoint.  Every assigned
color belongs to $R\subseteq M$.  These are precisely the three asserted
properties.
\end{proof}
