\begin{theorem}[Goldberg--Seymour]
Every multigraph $G$ satisfies
\[
  \chi'(G)\le
  \max\{\Delta(G)+1,\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph.  If
$\chi'(G)\le\Delta(G)+1$, then the desired inequality follows because
$\Delta(G)+1$ is one of the two entries in the displayed maximum.

Otherwise the integrality of the chromatic index gives
$\chi'(G)\ge\Delta(G)+2$.  By
\noderef{GeneralHighIndexElementary}, $G$ is elementary, so
$\chi'(G)=\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil$.  The latter number is the other entry in
the displayed maximum, and the desired inequality follows in this case as
well.  The two cases exhaust all natural-number values of $\chi'(G)$.
\end{proof}
