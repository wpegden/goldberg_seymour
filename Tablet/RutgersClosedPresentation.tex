\begin{definition}
Fix an original positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree \(\mathcal E\).
A \emph{Rutgers closed presentation relative to \(\mathcal E\)} consists
of a replacement coloring \(\sigma\), a displayed tree \(U\), and an
actual \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate from
\(\mathcal E\) directly to the replacement ETT displayed on \(U\).  The
replacement ETT is required to satisfy \noderef{MaximumProperty}.

Write \(n\) for its rung number and \(T_n^\sigma\) for its terminal
ladder tree.  The presentation must additionally retain exactly one of
the following kind-sensitive terminal routes under \(\sigma\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item its terminal kind is PE and
  \(\noderef{TashkinovClosure}(\sigma,T_n^\sigma,U)\) holds;
\item its terminal kind is SE or RE, and there are an exact edge identity
  \(f\) and a seed \(A\) such that its terminal connecting-edge set is
  exactly \(\{f\}\),
  \(\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}(T_n^\sigma,f,A)\), and
  \(\noderef{TashkinovClosure}(\sigma,A,U)\) hold.
\end{enumerate}
The alternatives are exclusive because the recorded terminal kind cannot
simultaneously be PE and SE or RE.  In particular, the route records both
the exact non-PE connecting edge and closedness of the displayed tree; a
mere initial-segment or correspondence certificate is not a route.
\end{definition}
