\begin{helper}[Initial good hierarchy for Algorithm 5.5]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung ETT with generating coloring
$\varphi_n$, terminal ladder tree $T_n$, and a
\noderef{HierarchyBase} $B$.
Let $J$ carry a
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} $\mathcal E_J$, a copied
base $B_J$, and a zero-divider \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} $K_J$ such
that
\[
 B_J.\mathit{core}=B.\mathit{core},\qquad
 B_J.\mathit{closure}=B.\mathit{closure},\qquad K_J(0)=T_n.
\]
Assume that the exact seed alternative holds: in PE,
$J=B.\mathit{core}$; in SE or RE, there is an individual edge $f_n$ with
$F_n=\{f_n\}$ and
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}$(T_n,f_n,J)$.  Assume
$|\overline\varphi_n(T_n)|\geq 2n+11$.  In PE assume also that some
color missing on $B.\mathit{core}$ is not closed there, every color of
\[
 \overline\varphi_n(T_n)\cap
 \overline\varphi_n(B.\mathit{closure})
\]
is closed on $B.\mathit{core}$, and this intersection has at least four
colors.  Then $K_J$ has a \noderef{GoodHierarchy} witness
$\mathcal G_J$ for which the sole stage-missing set is exactly
$\overline\varphi_n(T_n)$.  Moreover, in PE, if
\[
 R=\bigcup_{\eta\in\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(K_J,0)}
      \mathcal G_J.\Gamma^0_\eta,
\]
then $B.\mathit{core}$ is not $R^-$-closed and at least four colors of
$\overline\varphi_n(T_n)\cap
\overline\varphi_n(B.\mathit{closure})$ lie outside $R$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Put $D=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(K_J,0)$ and
$M=\noderef{TreeMissingColors}(\varphi_n,T_n)$.  The corresponding-ETT
certificate identifies the replacement terminal ladder tree, rung number,
stored colors, connecting-edge set, and terminal extension kind with those
of $\mathcal E$.

First suppose the common terminal kind is PE.  Transport the three PE
assumptions through the stated core and closure equalities and transport
the large missing-color bound through the exact ladder-tree equality.
The hypotheses of \noderef{GoodHierarchyInitialPairsPE} now hold for
$K_J$.  It supplies sets $\Gamma_\eta$ for $\eta\in D$ such that every
$\Gamma_\eta$ has two elements and is contained in $M$, distinct stored
colors receive disjoint pairs,
\[
 B_J.\mathit{core}\text{ is not }
   \Bigl(\bigcup_{\eta\in D}\Gamma_\eta\Bigr)^-\text{-closed},
\]
and at least four colors of the common missing-color intersection remain
outside that union.

Now suppose the common terminal kind is not PE.  The terminal index is
positive, so the certificate supplied by \noderef{TashkinovSeries} is a
positive constructor of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}; the kind equality
excludes PE and leaves SE or RE.  After the same ladder-tree transport,
\noderef{GoodHierarchyInitialPairsNonPE} supplies two-element subsets
$\Gamma_\eta\subseteq M$ which are pairwise disjoint for distinct
$\eta\in D$.

It remains to verify segment avoidance in the non-PE cases.  Consider SE.
The certified terminal SE constructor identifies the unique connecting
edge with the edge $f_n$ of its
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}; its selected coloring is exactly
$\varphi_n$, and $\varphi_n(f_n)=\delta_n$.  The preceding coloring is
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetClosed} on $T_n$, while the selected coloring is
\noderef{StableColoring} there.  Both colorings are proper and have the same unique
uncolored edge.  Symmetry in \noderef{sc2} reverses this stability
relation.  If a $\varphi_n$-colored boundary edge of $T_n$ had a color
$c\in M$, choose a vertex of $T_n$ missing $c$.  The missing-set clause
of stability makes that vertex miss $c$ under the preceding coloring,
and the controlled incident-edge clause makes the boundary edge retain
color $c$ under the preceding coloring, contradicting its closedness.
Thus $T_n$ is closed under $\varphi_n$, and the boundary edge $f_n$ shows
$\delta_n\notin M$.

In RE, the terminal constructor says that the current coloring is the
preceding closed coloring.  Its exact edge $f_n$ is a boundary edge on
the recorded \noderef{TwoColorCycle}; hence its current color is one of
the two cycle colors.  Closedness implies directly that this edge color
is not in $M$.  Therefore, in either non-PE case, the only displayed edge
of $J-T_n$ has a color outside $M$.

Define the sole Gamma family of $K_J$ by the selected sets
$\Gamma_\eta$.  Pair cardinality and disjointness are already proved.  By
the zero-divider hypothesis and the equality $K_J(0)=T_n$,
\noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors}$(K_J,0)=M$.  In PE,
\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}$(K_J,0)=B_J.\mathit{core}=J$, so the
half-open edge interval defining
\noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}$(K_J,0,\eta)$ is empty.  In SE or RE,
the starred stage is $T_n$ and the exact seed is $T_n+f_n$; hence every
segment color is the color of $f_n$, which lies outside $M$ and therefore
outside every $\Gamma_\eta$.  This proves the avoidance field.

There are no positive divider indices, so the unique-refresh and internal
minus-closed fields have empty domains.  In PE the parallel-base field is
exactly the non-minus-closed conclusion and four-color reserve supplied
by \noderef{GoodHierarchyInitialPairsPE}; outside PE its antecedent is
false.  The equality of the sole stage-missing set with $M$ was established
above, and the base equalities rewrite the PE union and reserve back to
$B.\mathit{core}$ and $B.\mathit{closure}$.  These clauses prove every
part of the conclusion.
\end{proof}
