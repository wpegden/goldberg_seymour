\begin{lemma}[Case 2, observation (22)]
Let \(v_n\in V(T_n)\), let
\(u\in V(T'_j)\setminus V(T_n)\), and let \(S'\) be the support-color
set.  Suppose the Case 2 hypothesis holds in its vertexwise form
\[
 \overline{\varrho}_1(v)\cap S'=\varnothing
 \quad\text{for every }v\in V(T'_j)\setminus\{v_n\}.            \tag{C2}
\]
If \(\beta\in\overline{\varrho}_1(u)\), then
\[
                         \beta\notin S'.                       \tag{22}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since \(u\notin V(T_n)\) but \(v_n\in V(T_n)\), we have \(u\ne v_n\).
Together with \(u\in V(T'_j)\), this puts \(u\) in the erased vertex set
to which (C2) applies.  If \(\beta\in S'\), its assumed membership in
\(\noderef{MissingColors}(G,\varrho_1,u)\) would put \(\beta\) in the
intersection in (C2), contradicting its emptiness.  Hence
\(\beta\notin S'\), as asserted.
\end{proof}
