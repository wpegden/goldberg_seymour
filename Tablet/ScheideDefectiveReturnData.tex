\begin{definition}[Last-return data for a defective replacement chain]
Fix a defective color $\delta$, put
$X=\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(T)$,
$i=\gammaIndex(\delta)$, and let $P'$ be the replacement path recorded by
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup}.  A
\emph{Scheide defective return datum} consists of three positions
$r,r+1,r+2$ on $P'$, vertices $v_1,u_1$, an exact edge identity $f_1$,
and a block index $j$, with the following properties.

The nodes of $P'$ at those positions are respectively
$v_1,f_1,u_1$; $v_1\in X$, $v_1\ne v^0_\delta$, and every vertex-node
strictly after position $r$ lies outside $X$.  In particular
$u_1\notin X$.  The vertex $v_1$ is in
\noderef{ScheideExitVertices}$(T,\varphi)$ and in the base image $Y$.
Moreover,
\[
 \varphi(f_1)=\delta,\qquad
 \operatorname{ends}(f_1)=\{v_1,u_1\},\qquad
 \gamma_j\in\overline\varphi(v_1),\qquad j\ne i.
\]
Finally the four colors
$\alpha _0,\gamma_i,\gamma_j,\delta$ are pairwise distinct.  Here all
positions, vertices, edge identities, and color inequalities are stored as
part of the datum, as are the exact position equalities and the assertion
that every later vertex-node is outside $X$.
\end{definition}
