\begin{lemma}[Certified Tashkinov series have boundedly many iterations]
Let \(V\) and \(E\) be finite types with decidable equality, let
\(G=(V,E)\) be a loopless multigraph, let \(k,r\in\mathbb N\), let
\(e_0\in E\), and let \(\varphi\) be a partial \(k\)-edge-coloring.  If
\(\mathcal S\) is a certified Tashkinov series constructed from
\((G,e_0,\varphi)\) with \(r\) noninitial iterations, then
\[
                         r+2\leq |V|.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{TashkinovSeriesData}, the tree entries of \(\mathcal S\) are
indexed as
\[
                         T_0,T_1,\ldots,T_{r+1}.
\]
By \noderef{TashkinovSeries}, the series has a
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} certificate at every index from \(0\) through
\(r\).

We first prove that
\[
                         |T_{i+1}|<|T_{i+2}|                 \tag{1}
\]
for every integer \(i\) with \(0\leq i<r\).  Inspect the certificate at
index \(i+1\).  The initial constructor of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} certifies only index \(0\), so it cannot be
this certificate because \(i+1>0\).  The only remaining constructors are
SE, PE, and RE.  Each of those three constructors includes the strict
extension field
\(\lvert T_{i+1}\rvert<\lvert T_{i+2}\rvert\), proving (1).

We next show by induction on \(j\), for \(0\leq j\leq r\), that
\[
                         |T_1|+j\leq |T_{j+1}|.              \tag{2}
\]
For \(j=0\), both sides of (2) are \(|T_1|\), so the claim is equality.
Suppose \(j<r\) and (2) holds at \(j\).  Formula (1) gives
\(|T_{j+1}|<|T_{j+2}|\), hence
\(|T_{j+1}|+1\leq |T_{j+2}|\).  Adding one to the induction hypothesis
and composing the two inequalities yields
\[
                 |T_1|+(j+1)\leq |T_{j+1}|+1\leq |T_{j+2}|,
\]
which is (2) at \(j+1\).  Taking \(j=r\) in (2) gives
\[
                         |T_1|+r\leq |T_{r+1}|.              \tag{3}
\]

Every \noderef{TreeSequence} has positive length, so \(|T_1|\geq1\).
The same definition gives an injective vertex map from the
\(|T_{r+1}|+1\) displayed vertex positions of \(T_{r+1}\) into the finite
type \(V\).  The finite-set cardinality bound for an injection therefore
gives
\[
                         |T_{r+1}|+1\leq |V|.                \tag{4}
\]
Combining positivity, (3), and (4), we obtain
\[
 r+2\leq |T_1|+r+1\leq |T_{r+1}|+1\leq |V|,
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
