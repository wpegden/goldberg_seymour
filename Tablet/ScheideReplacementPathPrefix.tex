\begin{helper}[Confinement of the replacement-path prefix]
Let $C$ be a \noderef{ScheideReducedConfiguration} for
$(G,k,e_0,\varphi,T)$, let $D$ be its
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup}, let $R$ assign
\noderef{ScheideReplacementData} to each defective color, and let $L$ be
a \noderef{ScheideLastExitPath} certificate for $\alpha$.  Fix a
defective color $\delta$, put
$T_\delta=(R\delta).\mathit{replacementTree}$, and assume that the
labeled $(D.\alpha_0,\alpha)$ \noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph}s for $\varphi$ and
$\varphi_\delta$ agree.  If $L.\mathit{lastInside}\in U_\delta$ and
$\alpha$ is not defective for $T_\delta$ under $\varphi_\delta$, then
every vertex-node of $L.path$ from $y_0$ through
$L.\mathit{lastInside}$ belongs to $V(T_\delta)$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Write $v=L.\mathit{lastInside}$, $g=L.\mathit{crossing}$, and
$X=\noderef{ScheideEnlargedVertexSet}(R)$.  The exact vertex-set field of
\noderef{ScheideReplacementData} and the assumed membership give
$v\in V(T_\delta)$, and also give $V(T_\delta)\subseteq X$.  By
\noderef{ScheideReplacementMaximum} and
\noderef{ScheideGlobalMaximumOrder}, $V(T_\delta)$ is
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetClosed} under $\varphi_\delta$.

The base-position fields of
\noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration} put $y_0$ in
$V(T_\delta)$.  The formula in
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSwitchEffect}, together with the fact that
$D.\alpha_0$ differs from the switched block color, shows that
$D.\alpha_0$ remains missing at $y_0$.  The certificate
\noderef{ScheideLastExitPath} records $v,g$, and the next vertex
consecutively on $L.path$, with the next vertex outside $X$.  Hence $g$
leaves $V(T_\delta)$.  By the assumed graph equality it has one of the
two colors $D.\alpha_0,\alpha$ under $\varphi_\delta$.  Closedness and the
missing color $D.\alpha_0$ exclude the first possibility, so $g$ is an
$\alpha$-colored boundary edge of $V(T_\delta)$.

Suppose the path left $V(T_\delta)$ before reaching $v$, and take its
first such departure.  The departure edge again has one of the two path
colors, and closedness again excludes $D.\alpha_0$, so it has color
$\alpha$.  It is distinct from $g$: the first departure occurs before
the occurrence at $v$, and $L.pathSimple$ from
\noderef{ScheideLastExitPath} forbids repetition of its edge-node.  Thus
there are at least two distinct $\alpha$-colored boundary edges of
$V(T_\delta)$.  Expanding \noderef{DefectiveColor} and
\noderef{DefectiveColors}, this says that $\alpha$ is defective, contrary
to the hypothesis.  Therefore no such earlier departure occurs, and all
vertex-nodes through $v$ lie in $V(T_\delta)$.
\end{proof}
