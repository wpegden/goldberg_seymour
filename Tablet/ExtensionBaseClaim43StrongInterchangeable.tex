\begin{lemma}[Claim 4.3(b4): terminal-class pairs survive restoration]
Let \(q\) be the terminal PE slot with supporting vertex \(v_n\), and
let \(\mathcal R\) satisfy
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant}.  At the output coloring
\(\varrho_1\), every exact pair \(\gamma_s,\delta_s\) with
\[
             s\in L(v_n)\setminus\{q\}
\]
is \(T_s\)-strongly interchangeable.  Here \(L(v_n)\) is the exact
support class from \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact range of the slots in
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace} is
\[
 L^*=L\setminus L(v_n)
\]
by \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices}.  Hence a slot
\(s\in L(v_n)\) is absent from the trace.  Support-class membership also
implies active-set membership.  Since \(s\ne q\), all hypotheses of the
final preservation clause in
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant} hold with the exact
support \(v_n\) and the exact gamma/delta fields stated here.  That
clause gives precisely the asserted strong interchangeability under the
output coloring.  This proves (b4), including the empty-\(L^*\) case.
\end{proof}
