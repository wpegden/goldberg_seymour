\begin{lemma}[Correspondence preserves the computed terminal stored set]
Let $\mathcal C$ be a corresponding replacement of a positive-rung ETT,
possibly on a different exact terminal tree. The computed terminal stored
set of its replacement ETT equals the computed terminal stored set of
the original ETT, each evaluated at its own terminal rung index.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the rung and series-presentation fields of
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} to identify the terminal
indices and to compute with its displayed replacement series. At every
index the raw stored sets agree. Their unions over indices up to the
common terminal rung therefore agree. Since the rung is positive by
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}, there is a positive slot with successor
index equal to this terminal rung. Ladder agreement at that slot gives
the same exact terminal ladder tree. Replacement stability at that slot
gives equality of missing sets at each vertex of the old terminal ladder,
by \noderef{StableColoring}. Taking their union according to
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} identifies the missing sets of that ladder
under the two respective terminal series colorings. The definition
\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors} subtracts exactly these equal missing sets
from the equal unions of raw stored sets. Both nonzero-index branches
thus have equal values. This proves equality of the computed sets, not
just agreement of the raw arrays.
\end{proof}
