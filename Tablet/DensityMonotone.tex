\begin{lemma}
For every edge restriction $G|F$,
$\lceil\Gamma(G|F)\rceil\le\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $U$ be any eligible odd vertex set.  By
\noderef{RestrictEdges}, forgetting the subtype witness injects the internal
edges $E_{G|F}(U)$ into $E_G(U)$ and preserves their endpoint pairs.
Consequently $|E_{G|F}(U)|\le |E_G(U)|$.  The common denominator
$|U|-1$ is positive, so the density value of $U$ in $G|F$ is no larger than
its value in $G$.  Taking the maximum over the same eligible vertex sets in
\noderef{Density} yields $\Gamma(G|F)\le\Gamma(G)$.  Monotonicity of the
natural ceiling from \noderef{DensityCeil} gives the asserted inequality.
\end{proof}
