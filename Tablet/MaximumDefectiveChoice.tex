\begin{definition}
Let (T) be an ordered tree-sequence, let (C\subseteq[k]), and let
\(\varphi\) be a partial coloring.  An admissible maximum-defective choice
consists of a proper coloring \(\pi\) leaving exactly (e_0) uncolored and
stable with respect to ((T,C,\varphi)), together with a defective vertex
(v) of (T) under \(\pi\).  It is required that for every other
admissible stable coloring \(\rho\) and every defective vertex (w) of
(T) under \(\rho\), either (w=v) or (w\prec_T v).

The certificate also selects an individual defective boundary edge (f)
incident with (v), its other endpoint (u\notin V(T)), and its color
\(\delta\), with the exact endpoint and coloring equations
\(\operatorname{ends}(f)=\{v,u\}\) and \(\pi(f)=\delta\).  This is the
maximum-defective selection, corresponding coloring, and connecting data
chosen in the non-RE branch of Algorithm 3.1.
\end{definition}
