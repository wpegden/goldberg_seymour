\begin{definition}
An \emph{extension-base restoration trace} for a terminal coloring
\(\sigma_n\) is the following finite certificate for Algorithm (A).
It consists of a natural number \(r\), certified slots
\(t_0,\ldots,t_{r-1}\), their exact supporting vertices and exact
gamma/delta colors, and colorings
\(\sigma^0,\ldots,\sigma^r\), such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\sigma^0=\sigma_n\), and the set of the distinct slot identities
      \(t_a\) is exactly
      \(L^*=\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices}\);
\item \(t_0>t_1>\cdots>t_{r-1}\);
\item each stored support, gamma, and delta is equal to the corresponding
      field of that exact certified PE slot; and
\item
\[
 \sigma^{a+1}=\sigma^a/
 P_{v_{t_a}}(\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\sigma^a)
\]
      for every \(a<r\), where the right side is the component switch
      \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} rooted at the exact vertex node
      \(v_{t_a}\).
\end{enumerate}
The certificate therefore records every intermediate coloring and keeps
parallel edge identities intact throughout the construction.
\end{definition}
