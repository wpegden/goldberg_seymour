\begin{definition}
The measure of a \(\noderef{TchangeCounterexample}\) is
\[
 |P_1|+|P_2|+|P_3|,
\]
where path length is the incidence-graph walk length.  This differs by
the fixed factor two from the number of exact multigraph edge-nodes used
in the paper and therefore induces the same strict order.
