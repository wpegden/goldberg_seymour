\begin{definition}
For a tree-sequence $T$, a partial coloring $\varphi$, and colors
$\alpha,\beta$, the outside-$T$ swap
$\varphi/(G-T,\alpha,\beta)$ fixes every edge identity incident with $T$
and interchanges $\alpha$ and $\beta$ on every edge identity not incident
with $T$.  All other coloring values, including the uncolored value, are
fixed.
\end{definition}
