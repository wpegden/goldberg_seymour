\begin{definition}
Let $\varphi_n$ be the ETT generating coloring, let $\sigma$ be the
coloring of its hierarchy base, and let $T=T_{n,q+1}$ be the terminal
tree.  Define
\[
 D_{n,q+1}=D_n^{\varphi}-\overline\sigma(T),
 \qquad
 D_n^{\varphi}=\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}.
\]
By the terminal-ladder missing-color compatibility in
\noderef{HierarchyBase} and the inclusion $T_n\subseteq T$, this is
equivalently the union of the raw stored sets $S_h$ through the terminal
rung with $\overline\sigma(T)$ removed.  Thus the raw stored union and
the subtraction use one mathematically compatible terminal coloring.
This is the terminal stored-color set used in the last clause of
\textup{LEM\_Stable}.
\end{definition}
