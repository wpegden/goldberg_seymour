\begin{helper}[Terminal series- or revisiting-extension ordering case]
Let \(T\) be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree generated by
\[
 \mathcal T=\{(T_i,\varphi_{i-1},S_{i-1},F_{i-1},\Theta_{i-1}):
                   1\leq i\leq n+1\},
\]
and suppose that \(T\) has the maximum property. Keep the graph \(G\),
palette size \(k\), and distinguished uncolored edge \(e_0\) fixed, but
allow the certified input coloring to vary arbitrarily. Assume explicitly
the following six assertions for every positive-rung ETT \(T'\) on these
fixed data, with arbitrary certified input coloring \(\varphi'_0\), maximum
property, and rung number \(s<n\), writing \(\varphi'_s\) for its generating
coloring and \(\mathcal T'\) for its certified series:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(V(T')\) is elementary with respect to \(\varphi'_s\).
\item Under every proper \((T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely \(e_0\) uncolored, \(T'_{s+1}\) is interchangeable for
any pair of colors of which at least one is missing on \(T'_{s+1}\).
\item For each PE slot \(a\leq s\) with supporting vertex \(u_a\) and
\(m(u_a)=b\), every proper \((T'_a,D'_a,\varphi'_a)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely \(e_0\) uncolored is
\((T'(u_a)-u_a,D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable. When \(b>1\), it is
also \((T'_{b-1},D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable. Moreover, the stored
color pairs at two distinct PE slots with distinct supporting vertices
and the same first-appearance index are disjoint.
\item If the terminal slot is PE with supporting vertex \(u_s\) and
stored colors \(\gamma'_s,\delta'_s\), then under every proper
\((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving precisely \(e_0\)
uncolored, the \((\gamma'_s,\delta'_s)\)-component through \(u_s\)
contains exactly the one vertex \(u_s\) of \(T'_s\).
\item If \(\tau\) is any proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable
coloring leaving precisely \(e_0\) uncolored, \(d\) is defective on
\(T'_s\) under \(\tau\), and \(x\) is a nonminimum vertex of
\(I[\partial_{\tau,d}(T'_s)]\), then
\(x\preceq_{T'}u_a\) for every PE supporting vertex or SE extension
vertex \(u_a\) with \(m(x)\leq a\leq s\).
\item Every proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving
precisely \(e_0\) uncolored is a \(\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s\) coloring.
\end{enumerate}
Assume that the terminal positive slot of \(\mathcal T\) is SE or RE.

Write \(T_n\) for the terminal pre-extension tree. Let \(\sigma_n\) be
any proper \((T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)\)-stable coloring leaving precisely
\(e_0\) uncolored, let \(d\) be defective on \(T_n\) under \(\sigma_n\),
and let \(v\) be a nonminimum vertex of
\(I[\partial_{\sigma_n,d}(T_n)]\). If \(v_i\) is the exact chosen
vertex at a PE or SE slot \(i\leq n\), and \(m(v)\leq i\), then
\[
                              v\preceq_T v_i .
\]
Here the weak order means equality or strict precedence in the exact
vertex order of the displayed final tree, and RE chosen vertices are not
included among the applicable vertices.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Specializing the lower-rung package to the current certified input gives
the premise of \noderef{ExtensionRules}; hence current assertion (iii)
holds with its stability and stored-pair clauses. The generalized package
also supplies current assertion (iv) through \noderef{ExtensionBase};
that assertion is vacuous in the present non-PE terminal case but fixes
the same induction interface as the PE case.

Fix \(\sigma_n,d,v,i,v_i\) and put \(X=V(T_n)\). By
\noderef{BoundaryColorVertex}, choose exact \(d\)-colored boundary
identities \(a,b\), with \(a\) incident with \(v\) and \(b\) incident
with some \(w\prec_{T_n}v\). They are distinct: by
\noderef{BoundaryEdges} each has a unique inside endpoint, while
\noderef{TreeVertexOrder} gives \(w\ne v\). Their exact boundary and
color facts are those of \noderef{BoundaryColorEdges}.

We first recover stability relative to the old terminal reference.
The first inclusion of \noderef{HkuMissingStoredMonotone} is
\[
 \overline\varphi_{n-1}(T_n)\cup D_{n-1}
       \subseteq \overline\varphi_n(T_n)\cup D_n.               \tag{1}
\]
The vertex clause of terminal \noderef{StableColoring} identifies the
missing sets of \(\sigma_n\) and \(\varphi_n\) on \(T_n\), and its edge
clause together with (1) lets us reduce the control set from \(D_n\) to
\(D_{n-1}\). Thus \(\sigma_n\) is
\((T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_n)\)-stable.

Inspect the actual terminal constructor in
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}. In the SE constructor,
\(\varphi_n\) is the selected coloring of
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}; its selected-stability field makes
\(\varphi_n\) \((T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})\)-stable. In the RE
constructor the certified equality is exactly
\(\varphi_n=\varphi_{n-1}\), so this relation is reflexive. Transitivity
\noderef{sc2} therefore gives in either case
\[
       \sigma_n\text{ is }
       (T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})\text{-stable}.               \tag{2}
\]

Suppose first that \(i=n\). The applicability hypothesis says the
terminal kind is PE or SE. Together with the present assumption that it
is SE or RE and the distinctness of the three constructors, this forces
SE; the terminal RE possibility is contradictory. In the SE case the
chosen-vertex equation identifies \(v_i\) with the vertex of the exact
terminal \noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}. The color \(d\), edge \(a\),
and its inside endpoint \(v\) make \(v\) a defective vertex by
\noderef{DefectiveEdge} and \noderef{DefectiveVertex}. Applying the
maximality field to \(\sigma_n\) using (2) gives
\(v=v_i\) or \(v\prec_{T_n}v_i\). The terminal ladder tree is an ordered
initial segment of \(T\) by \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}; the exact
entry equalities in \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} transport the
strict alternative to \(T\). Hence \(v\preceq_Tv_i\).

It remains to assume \(i<n\). The bound \(m(v)\leq i\), the minimum in
\noderef{ETTFirstAppearanceIndex}, and the nested stage trees in
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} place \(v\) in \(T_{n-1}\). Because that
tree is an ordered initial segment of \(T_n\), vertex injectivity in
\noderef{TreeSequence} also places every earlier displayed vertex there;
in particular \(w\in V(T_{n-1})\) and
\(w\prec_{T_{n-1}}v\). The outside endpoints of the exact identities
\(a,b\) lie outside \(X\), hence outside \(V(T_{n-1})\). Their unchanged
endpoint and color equations, their distinctness, and
\noderef{DefectiveColor} show that \(d\) is defective on \(T_{n-1}\)
under \(\sigma_n\), with \(v\) a nonminimum boundary vertex.

Since \(1\leq i<n\), the rung \(n-1\) is positive. Apply
\noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at that rung. It returns an actual
positive-rung ETT on the original input, with maximum property, whose
displayed tree is exactly the old \(T_n\), whose generating coloring is
\(\varphi_{n-1}\), and whose retained trees, raw arrays, stored sets,
kinds, chosen vertices, and step certificates agree pointwise with the
original series. Relation (2) restricts to
\((T_{n-1},D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})\)-stability: vertex missingness
restricts, and any edge color controlled on the smaller tree has the
same missing-vertex witness in \(T_n\), so the edge clause of
\noderef{StableColoring} restricts.

The first-appearance index of \(v\) is unchanged by the restriction.
Indeed its least stage is at most \(i<n\), and every candidate stage
through \(i\) is identified pointwise by
\noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction}. We may therefore apply lower-rung
assertion (v) to the restricted ETT, the coloring \(\sigma_n\), the same
exact edges \(a,b\), the same applicable slot \(i\), and the transported
first-appearance inequality. It gives
\(v=v_i\) or \(v\prec_{T_n}v_i\). The ordered inclusion of \(T_n\) in
\(T\) transports the latter relation, proving \(v\preceq_Tv_i\).
\end{proof}
