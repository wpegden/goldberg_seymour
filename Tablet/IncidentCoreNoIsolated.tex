\begin{lemma}
Every vertex of the incident core $G^{\circ}$ is incident with an edge of
$G^{\circ}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $x\in V^{\circ}(G)$.  By the definition
\noderef{IncidentVertex}, there is an original edge identity $e$ incident
with the underlying vertex of $x$ in $G$.  Incidence transport
\noderef{IncidentCoreIncidence} says that the same identity $e$ is incident
with $x$ in $G^{\circ}$.  Thus $x$ is not isolated.
\end{proof}
