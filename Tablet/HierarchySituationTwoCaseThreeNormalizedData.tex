\begin{definition}
A \emph{normalized Situation 7.2 Case 3 datum} is a
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoCaseThreeData} satisfying the full condition
(20).  Let \(H\) be its good hierarchy, let \(q\) be the last divider index,
put
\[
 A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)=T_{n,q}^{*},\qquad
 B=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,\ell-2)=T(y_{p-2}),
\]
let \(D_{n,q}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,q)\), and let \(D_n\)
be the raw terminal ladder stored set
\(\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}(\mathit{ett},n)\).  Then
\[
 \alpha\in\overline\varphi_n(B)
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \varphi_n(T.\mathit{edge}(t))\ne\alpha
       \quad(|A|\leq t<|T|).                                  \tag{20a}
\]
Writing
\(\Gamma^q=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,q}}\Gamma^q_\eta\), exactly one of the
following three alternatives is certified:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(q>0\) and
      \(\alpha\notin D_{n,q}\cup\Gamma^q\);
\item \(q=0\) and
      \(\alpha\notin D_n\cup\Gamma^0\), with the exclusion from the raw
      \(D_n\), not merely from \(D_{n,0}\);
\item \(\alpha\in D_{n,q}\) and its entire reserved pair
      \(\Gamma^q_\alpha\) is disjoint from the colors on every exact edge
      of the full displayed tail \(T-A\).
\end{enumerate}
Together with the inherited two missing-vertex witnesses, these clauses are
precisely the branch-neutral normalization (20), before deciding whether
\(\theta\) is missing at \(y_{p-1}\).
\end{definition}
