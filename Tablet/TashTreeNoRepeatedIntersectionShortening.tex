\begin{helper}[Intersecting component shortens the no-repetition case]
Let \((\varphi,T)\) be a minimum bad pair with
\(2<q:=\rho(T)<n:=|E(T)|\).  Suppose distinct colors
\(\varepsilon,\beta\) are missing at \(y_m,y_i\), respectively, with
\(m,i\le q-2\), and \(\beta\) is also missing at \(y_n\).  If the
\((\varepsilon,\beta)\)-component rooted at \(y_n\) meets a displayed
vertex of \(T_{q-1}\), then a smaller bad pair exists, a contradiction.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let \(Q\) be the stated component.  Unfold
\noderef{TashTreeMinimumBadPair} and take its bad-pair conjunct.  By
\noderef{TashTreeBadPair}, this supplies a \(k\)-triple and the
Tashkinov-tree condition for the full sequence \(T\).  By
\noderef{KTriple}, the coloring \(\varphi\) is proper, as required by
each application of \noderef{TwoColorComponentTashkinovSplice}.

We first record that each prefix used below is itself a Tashkinov tree.
Let \(p\) be either \(q-2\) or \(q-1\), and put
\(S=T_p\) using \noderef{TreeSequencePrefix}.  Fix a formal edge index
\(r\) of \(S\) with \(1\le r\).  The full-tree condition from
\noderef{TashkinovTree} gives a color of the exact edge in position
\(r\) and a missing-color witness at a vertex in a position
\(w\le r\).  The edge and vertex identities in
\noderef{TreeSequencePrefix} identify these entries of \(S\) with the
same entries of \(T\).  Thus the same color and the same earlier vertex
witness the Tashkinov condition for this edge of \(S\).  Since this
holds for every \(r\), \(S\) is a Tashkinov tree.  This argument applies
separately to both \(T_{q-2}\) and \(T_{q-1}\).

First suppose that \(Q\) meets the smaller exact prefix \(T_{q-2}\),
and set \(S=T_{q-2}\).  The inequalities \(m,i\le q-2\), together with
the entry identities in \noderef{TreeSequencePrefix}, put \(y_m,y_i\)
in the vertex set \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} of \(S\).  Hence
\(\varepsilon,\beta\), respectively, belong to
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} of \(S\).  Every vertex of \(S\) has an
original displayed index at most \(q-2<n\); injectivity of the vertex
map in \noderef{TreeSequence} therefore shows that the final vertex
\(y_n\) is outside \(V(S)\).

All premises of \noderef{TwoColorComponentTashkinovSplice} now hold:
properness was obtained above, the colors are distinct and tree-missing,
\(y_n\) is outside, and this subcase supplies the component
intersection.  The result is a strict extension \(S'\) which has \(S\)
as an exact initial segment in the sense of
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}, ends at \(y_n\), and comes with a
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}.  Since \(S\) was proved
Tashkinov, \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequencePreservesTashkinovTree}
makes \(S'\) Tashkinov.

The initial-segment vertex identities retain \(y_i\) in \(S'\), while
the last-vertex identity makes \(y_n\) the final vertex of \(S'\).
These vertices are distinct by \noderef{TreeSequence}, and both belong
to \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} of \(S'\).  Both miss \(\beta\), so
the disjointness condition in \noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}
fails.  The unchanged \(k\)-triple, the Tashkinov property just proved,
and this non-elementariness are exactly
\noderef{TashTreeBadPair}; hence \((\varphi,S')\) is bad.

It remains to compare ranks.  Here \(|E(S)|=q-2\).  For every formal
edge position \(r\ge q-1=(q-2)+1\), one has
\(r>|E(S)|\), so the later-edge equation returned by the splice says
that the exact edge in position \(r\) of \(S'\) joins its vertices in
positions \(r,r+1\).  Moreover \(q>2\) gives \(1\le q-1\), and strict
extension gives \(q-1\le |E(S')|\).  These endpoint equations and bounds
are precisely \noderef{TashTreeSuffixPath} at start \(q-1\).  By the
least-start definition \noderef{TashTreePathIndex},
\(\rho(S')\le q-1<q=\rho(T)\).  Thus
\noderef{TashTreeRankLess} holds, contradicting the universal
minimality clause of \noderef{TashTreeMinimumBadPair}.

It remains to suppose that \(Q\) avoids every displayed vertex of
\(T_{q-2}\) but meets \(T_{q-1}\), and now set \(S=T_{q-1}\).  The
same prefix, vertex-set, and injectivity arguments put the two early
missing-color witnesses in \(S\) and put \(y_n\) outside it.  Hence the
properness established above permits another application of
\noderef{TwoColorComponentTashkinovSplice}.  Its attachment vertex
\(u\) lies in \(Q\cap V(T_{q-1})\).  By
\noderef{TreeSequencePrefix} and \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}, the
vertices of \(T_{q-1}\) are exactly
\(y_0,\ldots,y_{q-1}\), whereas those of \(T_{q-2}\) are exactly
\(y_0,\ldots,y_{q-2}\).  Because \(Q\) avoids the latter set and the
displayed vertices are distinct by \noderef{TreeSequence}, necessarily
\(u=y_{q-1}\).

The prefix \(S\) is Tashkinov by the restriction argument above, so the
returned augmentation sequence and
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequencePreservesTashkinovTree} make the
extension \(S'\) Tashkinov.  Now \(|E(S)|=q-1\).  At the first new
formal edge position \(r=q-1\), the special first-edge equation from
the splice joins \(u=y_{q-1}\) to the first new vertex; the
initial-segment equality in \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}
identifies \(y_{q-1}\) with the vertex of \(S'\) in position \(r\).
At every later position the splice gives the ordinary consecutive
endpoint equation.  Since \(q>2\) gives \(1\le q-1\), and strict
extension gives \(q-1\le |E(S')|\), these equations again establish
\noderef{TashTreeSuffixPath} with start \(q-1\).  Thus
\noderef{TashTreePathIndex} gives \(\rho(S')\le q-1<q\).

Finally, the initial-segment and last-vertex identities retain the two
distinct missing-\(\beta\) vertices \(y_i,y_n\).  By
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} and
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}, \(V(S')\) is not elementary.
Together with the unchanged \(k\)-triple and the Tashkinov property,
this makes \((\varphi,S')\) bad by \noderef{TashTreeBadPair}.  The path
index inequality gives \noderef{TashTreeRankLess}, again contradicting
\noderef{TashTreeMinimumBadPair}.  The two prefix subcases exhaust the
asserted meeting with \(T_{q-1}\).
\end{proof}
