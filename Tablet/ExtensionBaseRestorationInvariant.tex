\begin{definition}
Let
\(\mathcal R=(t_a,v_{t_a},\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\sigma^a)\)
be an \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace}, and let \(q\) be the
terminal PE index.  The trace satisfies the \emph{extension-base
restoration invariant} when every intermediate coloring is proper and
leaves exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, and the following assertion holds at
each step \(a\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(t_a\in L^*\), the terminal support precedes \(v_{t_a}\), and
      \(T_j\) is an ordered initial segment of \(T_{t_a}\), which
      contains the exact support \(v_{t_a}\);
\item \(\delta_{t_a}\) is missing at \(v_{t_a}\) under \(\sigma^a\);
\item the component
      \(P_{v_{t_a}}(\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\sigma^a)\) is a path and
      its intersection with \(V(T_{t_a})\) is exactly
      \(\{v_{t_a}\}\); and
\item after switching this component, every active nonterminal pair
      whose exact slot has not yet been processed remains
      \(T_s\)-strongly interchangeable.
\end{enumerate}
The final clause is also required at the output coloring when the trace
has no steps: every active nonterminal pair whose slot is absent from
the trace is strongly interchangeable there.  This is precisely the
substantive invariant (6) of Algorithm (A), including its empty-trace
boundary case.
\end{definition}
