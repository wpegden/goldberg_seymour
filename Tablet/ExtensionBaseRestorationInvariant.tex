\begin{definition}
Let \(T\) be the final displayed ETT, let \(v_*\in V(T)\) be the
distinguished vertex used to form
\(L^*=\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices}\), and let
\(j_{\mathrm{slot}}\) and \(q\) be arbitrary noninitial slots of this
ETT.  For any such slot \(s\), write \(\mathcal T[s]\) for the stored
tree at its positive displayed stage \(s+1\).  Thus
\(\mathcal T[j_{\mathrm{slot}}]\), with no first-appearance assumption,
is the reference tree in every initial-segment clause below.  The slot
\(q\), with no terminality assumption, is exactly the slot exempted by
\(s\ne q\) when the active pairs are quantified below.

Let
\(\mathcal R=(t_a,v_{t_a},\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\sigma^a)\)
be an \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace} of length \(\ell\), with
initial coloring \(\sigma^0=\sigma_n\).  The trace satisfies the
\emph{extension-base restoration invariant} when every
\(\sigma^a\), \(0\leq a\leq\ell\), is proper and leaves exactly
\(e_0\) uncolored, and the following conditions hold for every step
\(a<\ell\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(t_a\in L^*\), one has \(v_*\prec_T v_{t_a}\), the ordered tree
      \(\mathcal T[j_{\mathrm{slot}}]\) is an initial segment of
      \(\mathcal T[t_a]\), and
      \(v_{t_a}\in V(\mathcal T[t_a])\);
\item \(\delta_{t_a}\) is missing at \(v_{t_a}\) under \(\sigma^a\);
\item the component
      \(P_{v_{t_a}}(\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\sigma^a)\) is a path,
      contains an actual edge node,
      hence satisfies \noderef{TwoColorNontrivialPathComponent}, and its
      vertex-node intersection with \(V(\mathcal T[t_a])\) is
      exactly \(\{v_{t_a}\}\); and
\item under the post-switch coloring \(\sigma^{a+1}\), every active
      slot \(s\ne q\) which has not occurred among
      \(t_0,\ldots,t_a\), with exact certified support \(v_s\) and
      exact certified colors \(\gamma_s,\delta_s\), has
      \(\gamma_s,\delta_s\) strongly interchangeable at
      \(\mathcal T[s]\).
\end{enumerate}
In addition, at the output \(\sigma^\ell\) of every trace, every active
slot \(s\ne q\) which is absent from the entire list
\(t_0,\ldots,t_{\ell-1}\), with its exact certified support and colors,
has its color pair strongly interchangeable at \(\mathcal T[s]\).
This final absent-slot clause is required for all \(\ell\); when
\(\ell=0\), it is simply the zero-step boundary case of that same
requirement.

This is the generic restoration predicate abstracted from invariant
(6).  It does not assert \(q+1=n\), does not call \(q\) terminal, and
does not identify \(j_{\mathrm{slot}}\) with the first appearance of
\(v_*\); those extra facts belong only to the certified Algorithm (A)
specialization.
\end{definition}
