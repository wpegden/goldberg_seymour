\begin{definition}[Initial-segment data of a Tashkinov series]
Let \(\mathcal D\) be \noderef{TashkinovSeriesData} with \(n\) rounds, and
let \(m\leq n\).  The data
\(\operatorname{TashkinovSeriesDataPrefix}(m,\mathcal D)\) have \(m\)
rounds and are obtained through the canonical order-preserving inclusions
\[
 \iota_T:\operatorname{Fin}(m+2)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Fin}(n+2),
 \qquad
 \iota_D:\operatorname{Fin}(m+1)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Fin}(n+1).
\]
Their tree entry at \(a\) is \(\mathcal D.\mathit{tree}(\iota_T(a))\).
Each of their coloring, raw stored-color, exact connecting-edge, extension-kind,
chosen-vertex, outside-vertex, delta, and gamma entries at \(a\) is the
corresponding entry of \(\mathcal D\) at \(\iota_D(a)\).  In particular, the
construction retains individual edge identities, not only their endpoints.
\end{definition}
