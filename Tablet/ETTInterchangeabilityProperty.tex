\begin{definition}
Let \(T\) be an extended Tashkinov tree with rung number \(n\), series
\(\mathcal T\), final stored-color set \(D_n\), and generating coloring
\(\varphi_n\).  The tree has the interchangeability property when it is
the closed final stage itself, namely \(T=T_{n+1}\), and the following
condition holds universally.

For every proper coloring \(\sigma_n\) leaving exactly the distinguished
edge uncolored and stable for \((T,D_n,\varphi_n)\), and every pair of
colors \(\alpha,\beta\) for which at least one of \(\alpha,\beta\) is
missing at a vertex of \(T\) under \(\sigma_n\), the colors
\(\alpha,\beta\) are \(T\)-interchangeable under \(\sigma_n\).
\end{definition}
