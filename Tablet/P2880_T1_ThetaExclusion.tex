\begin{lemma}[Every early-tail missing color is outside the last Gamma union]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph, palette, distinguished edge, input,
exact displayed tree \(T\), generating coloring \(\mu\), ETT, base,
hierarchy counterexample \(C\), and a
\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext} for these data.
Put \(H=C.\mathit{hierarchy}\), \(q=H.\mathit{dividerCount}\), and
\(\ell=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailLength}(C)\).
If \(1\le j<\ell\) and \(Y=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,j)\),
then every color missing at the last vertex of \(Y\) is outside
\((\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,q)).\mathit{biUnion}
(C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^q)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)\), and let \(Z\) be the
full penultimate prefix. Apply
\noderef{HierarchyCounterexamplePenultimateElementary} to all six
fields of the stated context. It makes \(Z\) elementary under \(\mu\).
The hierarchy and \noderef{HierarchyBase} give
\(H.\mathit{stage}(q)\preccurlyeq A\preccurlyeq T\):
at positive divider count the first two trees are equal; at zero
dividers they are the terminal ladder and the core, related by the
left segment of the PE \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} or by equality
in the nonparallel case. By \noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix},
\(|Y|=|A|+j\) and \(|Z|=|T|-1\); since \(1\le j<\ell\),
\(A\prec Y\preccurlyeq Z\). All these are exact prefix relations.
The last vertex of \(Y\) is not in \(A\): otherwise its equality
with a displayed vertex of \(A\), transported into \(Y\), would
contradict injectivity of \(Y\)'s vertex map and \(|A|<|Y|\).
Suppose a color \(\theta\) missing at that last vertex belongs to the
last Gamma union. Choose its owner in the last stored set. The avoidance
field of \noderef{GoodHierarchy} puts \(\theta\) in
\noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors} at that index. Expanding the
latter definition and the hierarchy's base-stage equation gives a
\(\theta\)-missing vertex in \(H.\mathit{stage}(q)\), hence in
\(A\). \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset} puts
both this vertex and the last vertex of \(Y\) in \(Z\).
They are distinct by the preceding observation, contradicting
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}. Thus \(\theta\) is outside
the last Gamma union.
\end{proof}
