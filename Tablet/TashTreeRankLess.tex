\begin{definition}
For tree-sequences \(S,T\), say that \(S\) has smaller Tashkinov rank than
\(T\) if
\[
  (\rho(S),|E(S)|)<_{\mathrm{lex}}(\rho(T),|E(T)|),
\]
where \(\rho\) is \noderef{TashTreePathIndex}.  Explicitly, either
\(\rho(S)<\rho(T)\), or the path indices are equal and \(S\) has fewer
displayed edge identities.
\end{definition}
