\begin{definition}
Two hierarchy presentations on the same terminal ordered tree have the
\emph{same stages and dividers} when their starred base trees are equal,
their divider counts are equal, and, after that dependent index
equality, their full ordered stage families and divider-position families
are equal.  If the terminal kind in either presentation is PE, their
chosen closure trees are also required to be equal.  For a non-PE
presentation the closure field in \noderef{HierarchyBase} is unused, so
two presentations are not distinguished by arbitrary values of that
field.  Every required tree-sequence equality preserves the complete
vertex order and each displayed edge identity.
\end{definition}
