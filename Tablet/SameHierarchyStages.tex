\begin{definition}
Two hierarchy presentations on the same terminal ordered tree have the
\emph{same stages and dividers} when their starred base trees and chosen
closure trees are equal, their divider counts are equal, and, after that
dependent index equality, their full stage families and divider-position
families are equal.  The equalities are equalities of tree-sequences and
therefore preserve the complete vertex order and each displayed edge
identity.
\end{definition}
