\begin{definition}
Two hierarchy presentations on the same terminal ordered tree have the
\emph{same stages and dividers} when their starred base trees are equal,
their divider counts are equal, and, after that dependent index
equality, their full ordered stage families and divider-position families
are equal.  Their terminal extension kinds must agree on whether they
are PE.  In that common PE case their chosen closure trees are also
required to be equal.  In the common non-PE case the closure fields in
\noderef{HierarchyBase} are unused, so the presentations are not
distinguished by arbitrary values of those fields.  In particular, a
PE presentation can never be identified with a non-PE presentation.
Every required tree-sequence equality preserves the complete vertex
order and each displayed edge identity.
\end{definition}
