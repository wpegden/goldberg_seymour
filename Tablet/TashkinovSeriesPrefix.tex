\begin{lemma}[Every initial data segment is a certified series]
Let \(\mathcal S\) be a certified \noderef{TashkinovSeries} with \(n\) rounds
and let \(m\leq n\).  There exists a certified series \(\mathcal S^{\leq m}\)
with \(m\) rounds whose data are exactly
\(\noderef{TashkinovSeriesDataPrefix}(m,\mathcal S.\mathit{data})\).
At every \(a\in\operatorname{Fin}(m+1)\), its certificate is heterogeneously
equal to the original certificate at the canonical included index.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the prefix data as the data field.  For each retained index \(a\), the
original series supplies its certificate at the included index, and
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStepPrefix} supplies a corresponding certificate for
the prefix data together with the required heterogeneous equality.  Choose
one such prefix certificate at every index.  These choices form the
certification field of a \noderef{TashkinovSeries}.  The data equality is
reflexive by construction, and the chosen certificate's heterogeneous
equality is exactly the one supplied at that index.
\end{proof}
