\begin{lemma}[\textup{LEM\_Stable}: recoloring chains outside a good hierarchy]
Let $T$ be a positive-rung ETT satisfying maximum property and carrying a
good hierarchy under its generating coloring $\varphi_n$.  Let
$\sigma_n$ be obtained by recoloring finitely many
$(\alpha,\beta)$-chains fully contained in $G-V(T)$.  Keep $G,k,e_0$
fixed but allow the certified input coloring to vary.  Assume that for
every positive-rung ETT $T'$ with arbitrary certified input coloring,
maximum property, and rung number $s<n$, the following six assertions
hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V(T')$ is elementary under its generating coloring
$\varphi'_s$.
\item Under every proper $(T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)$-stable coloring
leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored, $T'_{s+1}$ is interchangeable for any
two colors of which at least one is missing on it.
\item At every PE slot, stability descends to the prefix before the
support and to the ladder stage indexed by the support's first
appearance; stored pairs at distinct PE slots with distinct supports
and equal first-appearance indices are disjoint.
\item At a terminal PE, the stored two-color component through its
support meets the terminal ladder tree only at that support.
\item Every nonminimum tree endpoint of a defective boundary color
precedes each later PE support or SE extension vertex permitted by its
first-appearance index.
\item Every proper terminal stable coloring leaving exactly $e_0$
uncolored is a $\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s$ coloring.
\end{enumerate}
Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\sigma_n$ is proper, leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored, is
$(T,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable, and is $(T,\varphi_n)$-invariant;
\item the same terminal tree carries a corresponding ETT certificate
under $\sigma_n$ and satisfies maximum property;
\item the same ordered stages and dividers form a good hierarchy under
$\sigma_n$, with the same $D_{n,j}$ and the same $\Gamma^j_\eta$ for
every $\eta\in D_{n,j}$; and
\item if $T$ is
$(\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,q+1}}\Gamma^q_\eta)^{-}$-closed under
$\varphi_n$, it is so under $\sigma_n$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{HierarchyOutsideKempeStable}.  It gives every assertion in
(i), and in the PE case it also gives
$(T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stability.  Since every edge incident
with $T$ and every missing-color set on $T$ is fixed, the original
terminal construction from $T_n$ remains the identical Tashkinov
augmentation construction under $\sigma_n$: in a PE the seed is $T_n$,
and in an SE or RE the same individual connecting edge is adjoined before
the same augmentation tail.  Restricting the stable relation from $T$
to $T_n$ gives $(T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stability.  Apply \noderef{Td} to
the lower-rung package, maximum property, properness, and this restricted
stability.  It gives that $\sigma_n$ is a
$\varphi_n\bmod T_n$ coloring.  The eligibility alternative in
\noderef{ModColoring} therefore applies to the displayed tree $T$.
It supplies an actual
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate under
$\sigma_n$.  Now \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport}, using the given
maximum property, proves (ii).

We next transport the hierarchy base.  In the PE case, the hypotheses
just established meet \noderef{HierarchySplitter}; it uses
\noderef{CombinedStableClosurePreserved} to retain the same ordered
$R_n$, the same union $T_n\vee R_n$, and all stages and dividers; its
stable vertex clause also supplies the terminal-ladder missing-color
equality required by \noderef{HierarchyBase}.  In the non-PE cases the
starred base is $T_n$, and the outside recoloring fixes its vertexwise
missing-color sets.  Thus a compatible base exists there without
constraining the unused closure field.  The corresponding-ETT certificate
identifies every ladder tree, raw stored set, extension kind, and support
datum.  Copying all stages and divider positions therefore yields a
hierarchy for the corresponding ETT satisfying the paper-extensional
relation \noderef{SameHierarchyStages}.

Apply \noderef{OutsideKempeGoodHierarchy} to these two corresponding
presentations.  Its hypotheses have just been verified: the stage data
are identical, the raw stored sets agree by correspondence, and the
recoloring is outside $T$.  It supplies a good hierarchy under
$\sigma_n$ with the same Gamma family, proving (iii).  Here this means
the paper-extensional relation \noderef{SameGoodHierarchy}:
corresponding stages have equal $D_{n,j}$ sets and their Gamma values
agree on every color of that common set.  The total Lean functions may
differ away from these sets, where the paper defines no Gamma set.

For (iv), set
$C=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,q+1}}\Gamma^q_\eta$, where
$D_{n,q+1}$ is \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}.  Its stored
union and terminal subtraction are compatible by the hierarchy-base
missing-color invariant.  The two
colorings agree on every edge incident with $T$ and have identical
missing-color sets at every vertex of $T$.  Hence
$\overline{\sigma_n}(T)-C=\overline{\varphi_n}(T)-C$, and for each color
in this common set the two exact colored boundary-edge sets are equal.
Expanding \noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed} transports the assumed
minus-closedness from $\varphi_n$ to $\sigma_n$, completing the proof.
\end{proof}
