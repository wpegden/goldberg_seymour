\begin{definition}
Let $T$ be a tree-sequence, let $\varphi$ be a partial $k$-edge-coloring,
and let $C\subseteq[k]$.  The tree $T$ is \emph{$C$-closed} under
$\varphi$ if
\[
 \partial_{\varphi,\alpha}(T)=\varnothing
 \qquad\text{for every }\alpha\in C.
\]
Thus the definition concerns every exact boundary-edge identity of each
color in $C$.
\end{definition}
