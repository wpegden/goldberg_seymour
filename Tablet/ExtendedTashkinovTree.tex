\begin{definition}
Let a finite certified Tashkinov series from ((G,e_0,\varphi)) contain at
least one noninitial iteration, and write its last output tuple as
\((T_{n+1},\varphi_n,S_n,F_n,\Theta_n)\).  A tree-sequence (T), together
with a displayed coloring \(\psi\), is an extended Tashkinov tree (ETT)
constructed from this series if
\[
                    T_n\subset T\subseteq T_{n+1}
\]
as ordered tree-sequences and \(\psi=\varphi_n\).  The first containment is
strict, expressed by an ordered initial-segment relation plus a strict
length inequality; the second is an ordered initial-segment relation.
Thus the certificate retains the entire generating series and its generating
coloring, and cannot be satisfied merely by inclusions between vertex sets.
\end{definition}
