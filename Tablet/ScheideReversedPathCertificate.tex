\begin{definition}[Reversed Kempe-path certificate]
Fix a loopless multigraph $G$, an edge $e_0$, a partial $k$-edge-coloring
$\varphi$, a tree-sequence $T$ with first
vertices $y_0,y_1$, colors $\alpha,\beta$, and an incidence walk $P$ from
$y_0$ to $y_1$.  A reversed Kempe-path certificate consists of a
tree-sequence $U$ and an individual edge identity $q$ such that, writing
$m=\operatorname{length}(U)$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $2\leq m=\operatorname{length}(P)/2$ and $U$ is a Tashkinov tree;
\item the first two vertices of $U$ are $y_0,y_1$;
\item $q$ joins the last vertex of $U$ to its first vertex;
\item the vertex-nodes in the support of $P$ are exactly the vertex entries
of $U$;
\item the edge-nodes in the support of $P$ are exactly $q$ together with
the individual edge entries of $U$ having zero-based index at least one;
and
\item every edge of $U$ having zero-based index at least one joins the
vertex at that index to the next vertex.
\end{enumerate}
All references to edges in this certificate are to edge identities, so
parallel edges with equal endpoint pairs remain distinct.
\end{definition}
