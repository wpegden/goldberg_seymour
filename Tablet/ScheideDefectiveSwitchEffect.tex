\begin{lemma}[Scheide exact defective-color switch (6)]
For a defective color $\delta$, switch
$\gamma(\delta)$ and $\delta$ on the full incidence component through
$v^0_\delta$, obtaining $\varphi_\delta$.  Then
$\varphi_\delta$ is proper and leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored,
$f^0_\delta$ receives color $\gamma(\delta)$, and
\[
 \overline{\varphi_\delta}(v^0_\delta)=
 (\overline\varphi(v^0_\delta)\setminus\{\gamma(\delta)\})
 \cup\{\delta\}.
\]
Every other edge identity incident with $V(T)$ is fixed, every other tree
vertex has the same missing colors, and every vertex-node outside the
switched component has the same missing colors.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The colors are distinct: $\gamma(\delta)$ is missing at
$v^0_\delta$, whereas closedness and defectiveness make $\delta$ present
there.  The coloring in the statement is exactly the component swap of
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}.  Since the original coloring is proper by
the globally maximum field of \noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup},
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapProper} makes $\varphi_\delta$ proper.
Every edge-node of the component has one of the two colored values, so the
uncolored identity $e_0$ is not changed; all other identities remain
colored.  Thus the same edge is uniquely uncolored.

The recorded $f^0_\delta$ lies on the component and has color $\delta$,
so it receives $\gamma(\delta)$.  At $v^0_\delta$, properness gives one
incident $\delta$-edge and no incident $\gamma(\delta)$-edge before the
switch.  The switch exchanges precisely those two present/missing statuses,
which gives the displayed erase-and-insert identity of finite missing-color
sets.

By \noderef{ScheideDefectiveTailExternal}, no other vertex of $V(T)$ lies
on the switched component.  Hence no edge of the component can be incident
with such a vertex.  At $v^0_\delta$, the only incident component edge is
$f^0_\delta$, because the vertex-node is an end of the path.  Therefore
every other edge identity incident with $V(T)$ is fixed, and every other
tree vertex has exactly its old incident color values and hence its old
missing set.  Finally the definition of
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} fixes every edge-node outside the selected
component.  If a vertex-node is outside it, all of its incident edge-nodes
are outside it as well, so its present and missing colors are unchanged.
These observations prove every asserted clause.
\end{proof}
