\begin{lemma}[The exact seed for Algorithm 5.5]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung ETT with rung number $n$, terminal
ladder tree $T_n$, final series output $T_{n+1}$, generating coloring
$\varphi_n$, and maximum property.  Fix a \noderef{HierarchyBase} $B$.
Assume that, when the terminal kind is PE,
\[
 |T_n|<|B.\mathit{core}|,
 \qquad
 \noderef{TashkinovClosure}
   (\varphi_n,B.\mathit{core},T_{n+1})                         \tag{1}
\]
holds.  Thus (1) is the paper's special choice that $T_{n+1}$ is a
closure of $T_n\vee R_n$.

There is an exact seed $J$ carrying a
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} $\mathcal E_J$ for
$(\varphi_n,T_n)$ with maximum property, such that $J$ is an exact ordered
initial segment of $T_{n+1}$.  Moreover, $\mathcal E_J$ has a hierarchy
base $B_J$ with the same core, closure, and numerical support position as
$B$, and it has a zero-divider hierarchy $K_J$ whose two stages are exactly
$T_n$ and $J$.  The seed is determined by the terminal kind as follows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In PE, $J=B.\mathit{core}=T_n\vee R_n$.
\item In SE or RE, there is an individual edge identity $f_n$ such that
  the terminal connecting-edge set is exactly $\{f_n\}$,
  \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}$(T_n,f_n,J)$ holds, and there is a
  $\varphi_n$-\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} from $J$ to
  $T_{n+1}$.
\end{enumerate}
No endpoint pair is substituted for the individual identity $f_n$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $Z=T_{n+1}$.  Inspect the certificate at the positive terminal index
of the \noderef{TashkinovSeries} carried by $\mathcal E$.  Positivity of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} rules out the initial constructor there,
so \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} gives exactly the PE, SE, or RE cases.

Suppose first that the constructor is PE.  Set $J=B.\mathit{core}$.
The PE clause of \noderef{HierarchyBase} supplies an
\noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} presentation of this core whose left tree is
$T_n$; its left-segment field therefore gives the exact ordered inclusion
$T_n\preccurlyeq J$ in the sense of
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}.  The first hypothesis in (1) makes this inclusion
strict.  The augmentation-sequence part of the closure in (1) makes
$J\preccurlyeq Z$: every \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation} contains an
exact ordered initial-segment field, and these fields compose along the
reflexive-transitive sequence.

In the SE case, the constructor supplies an individual edge $f_n$, an
ordered tree $J$, the exact equality $F_n=\{f_n\}$, the relation
$\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}(T_n,f_n,J)$, and a closure from $J$ to
$Z$ under the current coloring.  The generating-color equation of
$\mathcal E$ identifies that current coloring with $\varphi_n$.  The
adjoin relation gives $T_n\preccurlyeq J$ and
$|J|=|T_n|+1$, while the closure gives both the asserted augmentation
sequence $J\leadsto Z$ and $J\preccurlyeq Z$.  The RE constructor supplies
the same four pieces of exact seed data, so the identical argument applies.
This proves the seed alternatives, including their strictness above
$T_n$.

For any of the three choices of $J$, define a new ETT on $J$ by retaining
the original rung number and the entire original certified series.  Use
the just-proved exact strict inclusion $T_n\prec J$, the exact inclusion
$J\preccurlyeq Z$, and the original generating-color equation as its
three terminal fields.  This is an actual
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}, denoted $\mathcal E_J$.

It is corresponding to the original ETT by taking its replacement input
and replacement series to be the original input and series.  Rung,
ladder-tree, raw stored-set, connecting-edge-set, and extension-kind
agreement are then literal equalities.  The required properness and
exact-one-uncolored assertions follow at index zero from the certified
\noderef{KTriple} in the initial constructor.  At every positive index
they come from the SE, PE, or RE constructor at that index, using the
selected-coloring fields of \noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice} in SE.  The positive-stage
\noderef{StableColoring} fields are reflexive: every controlled edge value
and every missing-color set is equal to itself.  These are all fields of
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}.  Since
$\mathcal E_J$ has exactly the original series, both clauses of
\noderef{MaximumProperty} are unchanged, so its maximum property is the
given one.

Define $B_J$ by copying the core, closure, support position, and every
proof field of $B$.  The replacement series is literally the original
series, so the copied fields have the required dependent types.  In
particular the three advertised data equalities are literal.

Finally define $K_J$ with divider count zero, stage zero $T_n$, and stage
one $J$.  Its base-stage and terminal-stage fields are the displayed
equalities.  The only nestedness and strictness fields are precisely
$T_n\preccurlyeq J$ and $|T_n|<|J|$, already proved.  There are no divider
positions.  For the core-inside-first field, in PE use $J=B_J.\mathit{core}$;
in SE or RE use the nonparallel clause of \noderef{HierarchyBase}, which
identifies $B_J.\mathit{core}$ with $T_n$, followed by the exact adjoin
inclusion.  Thus $K_J$ is the asserted zero-divider
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}, completing the proof.
\end{proof}
