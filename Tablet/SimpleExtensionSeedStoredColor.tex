\begin{lemma}[The seed color of a simple extension is stored]
Let a certified step of a Tashkinov series be an SE step at a positive
index $j$.  If its connecting-edge set is the singleton containing the
individual edge $f$ and the current coloring gives $f$ the color
$\beta$, then $\beta$ belongs to the computed stored-color set $D_j$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the SE constructor of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  Its
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice} identifies $f$ with the selected
defective edge, identifies the current coloring with the selected stable
coloring, and gives $f$ the selected color $\delta$.  The singleton
connecting-edge and color equations therefore give $\beta=\delta$.
The constructor also puts $\delta$ in the raw stored set at index $j$.

It remains to show that $\delta$ is not removed when
\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors} subtracts the current tree-missing
colors.  Suppose instead that $δ$ is missing at a vertex $w$ of the
preceding ladder tree.  The vertexwise clause of
\noderef{StableColoring} says that it is also missing at $w$ under the
preceding coloring.  At the selected inside vertex $v$, however, the
edge $f$ has current color $\delta$, so $\delta$ is present there; the
same vertexwise clause says that $\delta$ is not missing at $v$ under
the preceding coloring.  Hence some individual edge $g$ incident with
$v$ has preceding color $\delta$.

Since $\delta$ is tree-missing under the preceding coloring, the
protected-edge clause of \noderef{StableColoring} fixes $g$, so $g$ also
has current color $\delta$.  The current coloring is proper by
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}; the edges $g$ and $f$ are adjacent at
$v$ and both currently have color $\delta$, so they are the same
individual edge.  Thus $f$ had preceding color $\delta$.  But $f$ is a
boundary edge of the preceding ladder tree and that tree is closed under
the preceding coloring, contradicting that $\delta$ is missing at $w$.
Therefore $\delta$ is not current-tree-missing.  It survives the
subtraction in \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}, proving
$\beta=\delta\in D_j$.
\end{proof}
