\begin{definition}
Let \(C\) be a \(\noderef{TchangeCounterexample}\).  A
\emph{\(T\)-change Kempe-closure datum} for \(C\) consists of a coloring
\(\sigma\) and tree-sequences \(S,S_1,S_2,U\) with the following
properties.  The coloring \(\sigma\) is obtained from \(C.\varphi\) by
swapping \(C.a,C.b\) on the two-color component through \(C.b_3\).
The tree \(S\) is an initial segment of \(C.T\), ends at \(C.b_3\), and
contains \(C.b_1,C.b_2\); it is Tashkinov under \(\sigma\).  There are
successive Tashkinov augmentations \(S\to S_1\to S_2\), and \(U\) is a
\(\noderef{TashkinovClosure}\) of \(S_2\).

Moreover, \((G,e_0,\sigma)\) is a \(k\)-triple, \(U\) is a closed
Tashkinov tree whose vertex set is elementary, \(C.b\) is missing at
\(C.b_3\), all three \(C.b_i\) lie in \(U\), no \(C.a_i\) lies in \(U\),
and both ends of the exact edge \(C.f_1\) lie in \(U\).  Finally there
are three \((C.a,C.b)\)-exit walks under \(\sigma\), with the same ordered
endpoints, supports, and lengths as the three walks of \(C\).
\end{definition}
