\begin{definition}
Let $G$ be a finite multigraph, let $e_0$ be an edge identity, let
$\varphi$ be a partial $k$-edge-coloring, and let
$T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_n,y_n)$ be a tree-sequence with $e_1=e_0$.
The sequence $T$ is a Tashkinov tree with respect to $e_0$ and $\varphi$
if, for every $2\leq j\leq n$, the edge $e_j$ is colored by some color
which is missing at an earlier vertex $y_i$ with $0\leq i<j$.
\end{definition}
