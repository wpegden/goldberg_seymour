\begin{lemma}[One-divider good-hierarchy refresh]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph $G$, palette $[k]$, root edge $e_0$,
and positive-rung ETT $\mathcal E$ with input coloring $\varphi_{\rm in}$,
displayed tree $T$, and generating coloring $\varphi$.
Let $U,W$ carry corresponding certificates $C_U,C_W$ relative to this
same original ETT and coloring. Let their bases $B_U,B_W$ have equal
cores and equal closures. Let $H$ be a hierarchy on $C_U$ over $B_U$,
with $d$ dividers and good witness $\mathcal G$, and suppose $V(U)$
is elementary under $\varphi$. Put
\[
 A=H.\mathit{starred}(d),\quad
 D^+=\noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}(H),\quad
 R=\bigcup_{\theta\in D^+}\mathcal G.\Gamma^d_\theta.
\]
Suppose $\eta\in D^+$, $\beta\in\mathcal G.\Gamma^d_\eta$,
$\varphi(f)=\beta$, and $W=U+f$ as an exact one-edge ordered
tree-sequence. Suppose $\gamma_1\ne\gamma_2$ are missing on
$V(U)-V(A)$, their pair is disjoint from $R$, and $U$ is $R^-$-closed.

Then there is a hierarchy $H_W$ over the supplied certificate $C_W$
and base $B_W$, with $d+1$ dividers, and a good witness $\mathcal G_W$.
Under the canonical inclusions of finite indices it copies every old
stage $0,\ldots,d+1$, every old divider position, every indexed stored
set $0,\ldots,d$, and every old total Gamma family. Its new preceding
and starred stage are exactly $U$, its terminal stage is exactly $W$,
and its new divider position is $|U|$. Its new last indexed stored set
is exactly $D^+$, and its last Gamma family satisfies
\[
 \mathcal G_W.\Gamma^{d+1}_\eta=\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2\},\qquad
 \mathcal G_W.\Gamma^{d+1}_\theta=\mathcal G.\Gamma^d_\theta
       \quad(\theta\in D^+,\ \theta\ne\eta).
\]
All stage equalities are equalities of complete ordered edge-valued trees.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $N=d+1$ and $P=\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2\}$. Both corresponding
certificates have the same fixed original, not one another, as their
reference. The rungAgreement and seriesPresentation fields of
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} let us identify their
rungs with the original $n$. Their ladderTreesAgree fields identify
their terminal ladder trees because $n\ge1$. Their extensionKindsAgree
fields identify all kinds. Their storedColorsAgree fields identify
every raw stored set. Their generating-color equations identify both
terminal colorings with $\varphi$, and the terminal ladder trees agree
as just checked. Thus their computed terminal stored sets agree by
\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}: both the raw union and the subtracted
terminal-ladder missing set agree. These are the data equalities used below.

Define $H_W$ with divider count $N$, stage $H.\mathit{stage}(s)$ for
$0\le s\le d+1$, and stage $W$ for $s=d+2$.
Define its divider positions by copying the old positions numerically
for indices $0,\ldots,d-1$ and assigning $|U|$ to index $d$.
We verify every field of \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}.
The baseStage equation copies from $H$ and the terminal ladder
agreement above. Its terminalStage equation holds by definition.
Old nesting and strictness copy from $H$. The last nesting and
strictness follow from \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}:
$U\preccurlyeq W$ and $|W|=|U|+1$.
The old divider positions are valid indices in $W$ because their
values are at most $|U|<|W|$. The new one is also a valid index.
Old positivity and increasingness copy. The new positivity is
$|U|\ge1$, by \noderef{TreeSequence}. Every old divider has value
strictly less than $|U|$: its dividerStage identifies its value with
the length of old stage $i+1$, and successive strict fields increase
that length through the terminal stage $U$. Thus increasingness also
holds against the new last divider.
For old dividerStage equations, exact prefix agreement $U\preccurlyeq W$
makes the prefixes of $U$ and $W$ of the same old divider length equal,
entry by entry, by \noderef{TreeSequencePrefix}. For the new equation
the prefix of $W$ of length $|U|$ is exactly $U$, with identical vertex
and edge arrays. Finally coreInsideFirst copies using the equal cores.
This constructs an actual hierarchy over the supplied $C_W$ and $B_W$.
In particular all the asserted stage and divider equalities hold.

By \noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}, all old starred stages agree
(at index zero use the equality of cores), and the new last starred
stage is $U$. The equal computed raw stored set and the definition
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} consequently give all old indexed
stored-set equalities. At the new last index this definition gives
\[
 D_n-\overline\varphi(U)
       =\noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}(H)=D^+ .       \tag{1}
\]
It is the old terminal set, not $D_{n,d}$.
By \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColorsSubsetLast},
$D^+\subseteq D_{n,d}$.

Define the total Gamma family of $H_W$ by copying all old levels
$0,\ldots,d$. At level $d+1$, assign $P$ to $\eta$ and
$\mathcal G.\Gamma^d_\theta$ to every $\theta\ne\eta$, including
colors outside $D^+$. We verify every field of \noderef{GoodHierarchy}.

For twoColors, every old level copies, and at the new level the pair
$P$ has cardinality two since its members differ. Every other owner
in $D^+$ belongs to $D_{n,d}$, so its copied pair has cardinality
two by the old field. For disjointPairs, old levels copy; distinct
copied pairs at the new level are disjoint by the old field. The pair
$P$ is disjoint from each copied pair for an owner in $D^+$ because
that pair is a subset of $R$ and $P$ is disjoint from $R$.

Here is the exact cutoff check needed for old avoidance. For every
old level $j\le d$, put $B_j=H.\mathit{stage}(j+1)$.
The nested fields imply $B_j\preccurlyeq U$ and
$|B_j|\le|U|$. Apply \noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPositionPrefixCutoff}
to the exact prefix $U\preccurlyeq W$, color $\theta$, and cutoff
length $|B_j|$. It gives
\[
 \min(m_U(\theta),|B_j|)=\min(m_W(\theta),|B_j|).            \tag{2}
\]
This theorem uses the totalized first-missing position: if $\theta$
has a missing witness on $U$, exact prefix equality identifies the
least witness on $W$ with that on $U$. If it has no witness on $U$,
its old fallback is $|U|$, and its new first witness, if any, is after
$|U|$; otherwise its new fallback is $|W|$. All these positions have
the same minimum $|B_j|$. Thus (2) also covers the no-witness case.
Together with equal starred lengths and equal edge arrays of $B_j$,
(2) gives equality of the old segment-color sets by
\noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}. The old stage-missing sets are
also equal by \noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors}, using terminal
ladder agreement at zero and the copied preceding stage at positive
indices. Hence the old avoidance fields copy exactly.

At the new level, \noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors} is
$\overline\varphi(U)$ and the segment runs from the starred tree $U$
inside $W$. Its only possible displayed edge is $f$, of color $\beta$.
The two colors in $P$ are missing on $U$ and are not $\beta$:
$\beta\in R$ by the supplied owner $\eta\in D^+$, while
$P\cap R=\varnothing$. Thus $P$ avoids this segment.
For $\theta\in D^+$ with $\theta\ne\eta$,
\noderef{GoodHierarchyReservedDisjoint} says its copied pair is
missing on $A$. The starred tree $A$ is an exact prefix of $U$:
when $d=0$ use coreInsideFirst and terminalStage, and when $d>0$
use the last nested field and terminalStage. Its missing witnesses
therefore lie in $U$ by
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset}.
Old pair disjointness between $\theta$ and $\eta$ excludes $\beta$
from the copied pair. Hence this pair also lies in the new stage's
missing set and avoids its segment. New avoidance is proved for
every owner in (1).

For uniqueRefresh, every old divider copies: its indexed stored set,
both Gamma levels, preceding stage, and starred stage are unchanged,
so its entire existential-uniqueness predicate is unchanged.
At the new divider, $\eta$ is an owner by (1), its pair $P$ is missing
on $V(U)-V(A)$ by hypothesis, and every other owner retains the old
last pair by construction. It remains to prove uniqueness.
Let $\theta\in D^+$ with $\theta\ne\eta$. Its copied pair has
cardinality two, so select a member $c$. By
\noderef{GoodHierarchyReservedDisjoint}, $c$ is missing at some
$a\in V(A)$. If that copied pair were contained in the difference-missing
set, $c$ would also be missing at some $u\in V(U)-V(A)$, by
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} and the biunion definition.
The prefix relation puts $a$ in $V(U)$, and $u\ne a$.
This contradicts the supplied elementarity of $U$ in
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}. Thus no other owner satisfies
the difference-missing clause of the refresh predicate, proving its
unique owner without assuming that distinct colors have distinct
first missing vertices.

For parallelBase, the two terminal kinds agree with the same original
kind. Their terminal ladder trees, cores, closures, level-zero stored
sets, and level-zero Gamma families also agree. Both conjuncts of the
old parallelBase field therefore transport literally to $H_W$.
There is no additional assumption about the support position here;
this field depends only on the just-listed equalities.

Finally each old minusClosed field copies. At the new divider its
preceding tree is $U$, its indexed owner set is $D^+$ by (1), and
the preceding Gamma family is the old last family. Its required
minus-closedness is exactly the supplied $R^-$-closedness of $U$.
All fields of the good witness have now been verified. Its definition
gives the claimed old Gamma equalities and the single new replacement,
and the preceding construction gives every remaining exact equality.
\end{proof}
