\begin{lemma}[An exact segment with the full vertex set is the full tree]
Let $A$ and $B$ be tree-sequences for the same finite loopless
multigraph and distinguished first edge.  Suppose that $A$ is an exact
ordered \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} of $B$ and that
\[
 \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(A)=
 \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(B).
\]
Then $A=B$ as full ordered edge-valued tree-sequences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $|S|$ for the number of edge entries in a tree-sequence $S$.
By the data in \noderef{TreeSequence}, its vertex map has domain
$\{0,\ldots,|S|\}$ and is injective.  Consequently the definition of
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} gives
\[
             |V(S)|=|S|+1.                                      \tag{1}
\]
Apply (1) to $A$ and $B$.  The assumed equality $V(A)=V(B)$ gives
$|A|+1=|B|+1$, and hence $|A|=|B|$.

Now expand \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}.  Since the two lengths
are equal, every vertex index of $B$ lies in the range on which the
initial-segment vertex equality applies, and every edge index of $B$
lies in the range on which the edge equality applies.  Thus the ordered
vertex maps of $A$ and $B$ agree at every index, and so do their
ordered edge maps.  The remaining fields in \noderef{TreeSequence}
assert positivity, injectivity, the distinguished first edge, and
attachment; all are propositions, so proof irrelevance identifies these
fields once the lengths and the two maps have been identified.  Therefore
the complete tree-sequence structures $A$ and $B$ are equal.
\end{proof}
