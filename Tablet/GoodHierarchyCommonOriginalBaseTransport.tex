\begin{helper}[Transporting a hierarchy base between common-original presentations]
Let two corresponding ETT certificates on ordered trees $J$ and $U$ have
the same original positive-rung ETT and the same final coloring $\sigma$.
Every hierarchy base $B_J$ on the replacement ETT over $J$ transports to
a hierarchy base $B_U$ on the replacement ETT over $U$ whose core, closure,
and numerical supporting position equal those of $B_J$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal E$, $\mathcal E_J$, and $\mathcal E_U$ for the original
ETT and the two replacement ETTs.  We first construct an auxiliary
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate from
$\mathcal E_J$ to $\mathcal E_U$.  The two given certificates identify
the rung number, every positive ladder tree, every raw stored set, every
connecting-edge set, and every extension kind with the corresponding data
of $\mathcal E$.  Transporting their indices through the two rung
equalities and composing through the common original data therefore gives
all these agreement fields from $\mathcal E_J$ to $\mathcal E_U$.  The
series-presentation and final-generating-color fields similarly give the
required presentation and the final coloring $\sigma$.

It remains to establish the replacement-stability field.  Fix a positive
rung $i$.  Let $\varphi_i$, $\psi_i$, and $\chi_i$ be the colorings at
that rung in $\mathcal E$, $\mathcal E_J$, and $\mathcal E_U$.  The two
given correspondence certificates say that $\psi_i$ and $\chi_i$ are
\noderef{StableColoring}s relative to $\varphi_i$ on the same original
ladder tree and protected set.  They also give properness and the
exactly-one-uncolored property for $\psi_i$ and $\chi_i$.

For $\varphi_i$, inspect the certificate supplied by
\noderef{TashkinovSeries}.  Since $i$ is positive, the
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} certificate is SE, PE, or RE.  In the SE
case, properness and the one-uncolored property are fields of its selected
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}; the PE case records both properties for
the current coloring; and the RE case identifies the current coloring
with the preceding proper, one-uncolored coloring.  Thus all three
colorings are elements of the subtype used by \noderef{sc2}.

The symmetric clause of \noderef{sc2} reverses the stability relation
from $\varphi_i$ to $\psi_i$, and its transitive clause composes that
relation with the one from $\varphi_i$ to $\chi_i$.  This gives stability
from $\psi_i$ to $\chi_i$ on the original ladder tree and protected set.
To identify the protected set computed in $\mathcal E_J$, unfold
\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}.  The raw stored entries agree rung by
rung with those of $\mathcal E$, while the vertexwise missing-set clause
of the stable relation for $\psi_i$ identifies the tree-missing sets on
the common ladder tree.  Hence the computed stored sets agree.  Transport
across the ladder-tree and stored-set equalities gives exactly the
replacement-stability field relative to $\mathcal E_J$.

Doing this at every positive rung completes the auxiliary correspondence
certificate from $\mathcal E_J$ to $\mathcal E_U$.  Apply
\noderef{HierarchyBaseCorrespondingTransport} to that certificate and
$B_J$.  Its conclusion supplies a base on $\mathcal E_U$ with the same
core, closure, and numerical supporting position.  In the PE case the
cited transport theorem compares the exact chosen boundary-edge identities
and their inside endpoints, so no uniqueness of an unspecified support
vertex is being assumed.
\end{proof}
