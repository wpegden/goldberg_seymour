\begin{lemma}[Step 1, Subcase 2.1: adjoining the two retained edges]
Let $\mu$ be a mod-coloring of a positive-rung maximum ETT $\mathcal E$,
and let
$C$ carry a corresponding ETT $\mathcal E_C$ with maximum property, an
actual hierarchy base, and a good hierarchy $K_C$.  Assume explicitly
that the terminal PE augmentation sequence to $C$, or the exact terminal
SE/RE seed followed by its augmentation sequence to $C$, is available.
Assume the replacement large missing-color estimate and suppose colors
$\gamma,\beta$ are both absent from the last indexed Gamma union of
$K_C$.

Let $b_1,b_2,b_3\in V(C)$, with $\beta$ missing at $b_3$.  Let $f_1,f_2$
be retained individual $\beta$-colored boundary-edge identities of $C$,
incident with $b_1,b_2$ respectively, and suppose their outside endpoints
are distinct.  Then there are exact tree-sequences
\[
                         J_1=C+f_1,\qquad J=J_1+f_2,
\]
a corresponding ETT $\mathcal E_J$ under $\mu$, an actual hierarchy
base, and a good hierarchy $K_J$ on $J$.  The replacement has maximum
property and the same divider count and large estimate as the $C$-level
package; $\gamma,\beta$ remain absent from its last indexed Gamma union.
Both endpoints of each exact identity $f_1,f_2$, and all three vertices
$b_1,b_2,b_3$, belong to $V(J)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand \noderef{BoundaryColorEdges} and \noderef{BoundaryEdges}.  For
$i=1,2$, the exact identity $f_i$ has one endpoint in $V(C)$ and one
endpoint $u_i$ outside it, and $\mu(f_i)=\beta$.  Since $b_i\in V(C)$ is
incident with $f_i$, looplessness and the unordered endpoint equation
identify its inside endpoint with $b_i$ and its outside endpoint with
$u_i$.  By hypothesis $u_1\ne u_2$.

Append the vertex $u_1$ along the individual edge identity $f_1$ to form
$J_1$, then append $u_2$ along $f_2$ to form $J$.  The second append is
legal because $u_2\notin V(C)$ and $u_2\ne u_1$, whereas
$V(J_1)=V(C)\cup\{u_1\}$.  In both cases the new edge has one old and one
new endpoint, and injectivity follows from the boundary property and the
distinctness just proved.  Thus every field of
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge} holds and
\[
                         J_1=C+f_1,\qquad J=J_1+f_2.           \tag{1}
\]

The color $\beta$ is missing at $b_3\in V(C)$, hence belongs to
\noderef{TreeMissingColors} of $C$ and of $J_1$.  The two new exact
identities have color $\beta$.  Combining these facts with (1) verifies
every field of
\[
   \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}(\mu,C,J_1),\qquad
   \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}(\mu,J_1,J).                \tag{2}
\]
Append the two steps (2) to the stated $C$-level route using the
reflexive-transitive closure in
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}.  In the PE case this gives a
route from the last ladder tree $T_n$ to $J$.  In the SE/RE case it gives
a route from the same exact seed $T_n+f_n$ to $J$, retaining both the
individual identity $f_n$ and the equality $F_n=\{f_n\}$.  Since the
corresponding ETT on $C$ strictly extends $T_n$, so does $J$.  These are
exactly the eligibility alternatives in \noderef{ModColoring}.

Apply the universal clause of the given mod-coloring to $J$.  It supplies
an actual \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate
$\mathcal C_J$ under $\mu$.  The rung is the same as that of the original
ETT, and \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} transfers the original
maximum property
to its replacement ETT $\mathcal E_J$.

We rebuild its hierarchy base from the actual terminal constructor.  If
the terminal kind is not PE, both corresponding ETTs have the common last
ladder tree $T_n$ as core; copy the support position and unused closure
field from the $C$-level base.  The missing-color compatibility is
unchanged because both bases use the same coloring $\mu$ and exact ladder
tree.

Suppose the terminal kind is PE.  Extract separately the terminal PE
constructors of $\mathcal E_C$ and $\mathcal E_J$.  Their connecting-edge
sets each agree, by their respective
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificates, with the same
exact connecting-edge set of the original ETT.  The singleton equations
in \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} therefore identify the two PE edge
identities.  The endpoint equations in
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}, the inside assertions in
\noderef{DefectiveVertex}, and the outside-end assertions orient the
common unordered pair exactly as in the $C$-level construction.  Hence
the actual support vertex and its positive position agree.

Copy the core and closure from the $C$-level base.  Its PE field already
says that this closure is a genuine \noderef{TashkinovClosure} under the
same coloring $\mu$, and its \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} witness is
coloring-free.  Thus no new prefix assertion about the closure is used:
the copied closure certificate, the support identity just proved, and the
ordered union verify every PE field of a base $B_J$ for $\mathcal E_J$.

Define $K_J$ using all nonterminal stages and divider positions of $K_C$
and replacing only the terminal stage $C$ by $J$.  The two exact adjoin
steps give $C\prec J$, so all nesting and strictness fields hold and the
divider count is unchanged.  The last ladder tree, raw stored sets,
starred stages, and coloring are unchanged.  Expanding
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} therefore identifies every indexed
stored set.  Use the same Gamma family.  Pair cardinality, pairwise
disjointness, unique refresh, internal minus-closedness, and the rebuilt
parallel-base clause all copy from the given
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} on $K_C$.

For avoidance, expand the totalized first-missing position and
\noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}.  A first missing position already in
$C$ is unchanged, since $C$ is an exact prefix of $J$.  If a color had no
missing vertex in $C$, adjoining $u_1,u_2$ can only move its totalized
first-missing position beyond the end of $C$.  The only displayed edges
new in that enlarged segment are $f_1,f_2$, both colored $\beta$.  By
hypothesis $\beta$ is in none of the indexed last Gamma pairs.  Hence no
Gamma pair acquires a forbidden segment color, and every avoidance clause
remains true.  The copied data therefore form a genuine good hierarchy
$\mathcal G_J$.

The indexed stored sets and Gamma pairs were copied, so the given
exclusions of $\gamma$ and $\beta$ transfer directly.  Both
$\mathcal E_C$ and $\mathcal E_J$ correspond to the same original ETT;
their rung numbers and exact last ladder trees agree, and their displayed
generating coloring is the same $\mu$.  The large estimate for
$\mathcal E_C$ is consequently the required estimate for
$\mathcal E_J$.

Finally, (1) includes every vertex of $C$ in $J$ and adds the two outside
endpoints $u_1,u_2$.  Each endpoint of $f_1$ or $f_2$ is therefore in
$V(J)$, as are $b_1,b_2,b_3$.  This proves all asserted conclusions.
\end{proof}
