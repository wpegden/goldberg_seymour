\begin{theorem}[Appending one certified Tashkinov-series tuple]
Let \(\mathcal S\) be a certified Tashkinov series with \(r\) noninitial
iterations.  Supply one new terminal tree and all eight final non-tree values,
and let \(A^+\) be the exact data append of \(\mathcal S\)'s data.  If the
last index of \(A^+\) has an Algorithm 3.1 step certificate, then there exists
a certified Tashkinov series \(\mathcal S^+\) with \(r+1\) noninitial
iterations whose data are exactly \(A^+\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Define \(\mathcal S^+\)'s data to be the supplied exact append.  It remains to
certify every index of \(\operatorname{Fin}(r+2)\).  Perform last-index case
analysis.  In the last case, use the supplied final certificate.  In a
cast-successor case \(j^+\), the old series supplies its certificate at
\(j\), and \(\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStepAppend}\) transports that certificate
to \(j^+\).  These two cases exhaust the enlarged finite index type, so they
define the certification field required by \(\noderef{TashkinovSeries}\).
The data field was defined to be the exact append, making the concluding data
equality reflexive.
\end{proof}
