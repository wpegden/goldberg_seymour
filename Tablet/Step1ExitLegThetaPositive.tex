\begin{lemma}[The positive-divider predecessor color]
Let $M$ be a Step 1 exit-leg minimum whose selected hierarchy has
positive divider count $d$.  Then there is a color $\theta$, different
from the two exit-leg colors, which is missing and singleton-closed on
the last starred stage and singleton-closed on the selected terminal
tree.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $K$ be the selected hierarchy, put
$B_-=K.\mathit{stage}(d-1)$ and
$A=K.\mathit{stage}(d)$.  The latter is the last starred stage by
\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}.  The `baseStage', `nested', and
`terminalStage' clauses of \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}, composed as
relations from \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}, give exact ordered
initial-segment inclusions from the terminal ladder tree $X$ through
$B_-$ and $A$ to the selected terminal tree $U$.  Since an exact initial
segment retains every displayed vertex, \noderef{TreeMissingColors}
gives
\[
 \overline\psi(X)\subseteq\overline\psi(B_-)
 \subseteq\overline\psi(A)\subseteq\overline\psi(U).       \tag{1}
\]
There is also an exact initial-segment inclusion from the preceding
starred stage $K.\mathit{starred}(d-1)$ to $A$: by
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} it is the `nested' inclusion when $d>1$
and the `coreInsideFirst' inclusion when $d=1$.  Thus its missing-color
set is contained in that of $A$ by \noderef{TreeMissingColors}.
Subtracting these nested missing sets from the common raw stored set in
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} gives
$D^K_{n,d}\subseteq D^K_{n,d-1}$.

Apply `twoColors' and `disjointPairs' from
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} at index $d-1$ to the indices in
$D^K_{n,d}$.  Together with
\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsCardinality} and the `rounds_eq' field of
\noderef{Step1ExitLegPresentation}, this bounds
\[
 \left|\bigcup_{\eta\in D^K_{n,d}}\Gamma^{d-1}_\eta\right|
 \leq 2n.                                                    \tag{2}
\]
The `large' field of the selected
\noderef{Step1ExitLegPresentation} gives at least $2n+11$ colors missing
on $X$.
Using (1), (2), and deleting the two exit-leg colors, choose
\[
 \theta\in\overline\psi(B_-)
 \setminus\left(\{a,b\}\cup
   \bigcup_{\eta\in D^K_{n,d}}\Gamma^{d-1}_\eta\right).
\]
Then $\theta$ is missing on $A$.  The last `minusClosed' field of
\noderef{GoodHierarchy}, unfolded via
\noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}, closes it on $A$.

It remains to exclude $\theta$ from the terminal Gamma union, whose
superscript is $d$.  The terminal stored set is contained in
$D^K_{n,d}$: this follows by subtracting the larger terminal-tree missing
set in \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}.  Let $\eta_g$ be the
unique refreshed index supplied by `uniqueRefresh'.  For every
$\eta\ne\eta_g$, the Gamma pair at level $d$ equals the pair at level
$d-1$, so the choice of $\theta$ excludes it.  Every color in the
refreshed pair at $\eta_g$ is missing at a vertex of
$V(A)\setminus V(K.\mathit{starred}(d-1))$.  On the other hand,
$\theta$ is missing at a vertex of $B_-$, and every vertex of $B_-$ lies
in that preceding starred stage.  The latter is equality for $d-1>0$;
for $d=1$, \noderef{HierarchyStarredStage} makes the preceding starred
stage either $X$ itself or the chosen ordered union containing $X$; the
vertex-union clause of \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} gives the latter
containment.  Thus the old and refreshed missing witnesses are distinct.
Both belong to $U$ by the exact hierarchy inclusions above.  If $\theta$
belonged to the refreshed pair, these would be two distinct vertices of
the selected terminal tree missing $\theta$.  The `elementary' field of
\noderef{Step1ExitLegMinimum}, interpreted by
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}, forbids one color from being
missing at two distinct vertices of that set.  Hence $\theta$ avoids the
full terminal Gamma union.  It is missing on $U$ by (1), and the
`terminalMinusClosed' field of
\noderef{Step1ExitLegPresentation}, unfolded with
\noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}, therefore closes $\theta$ on $U$.
\end{proof}
