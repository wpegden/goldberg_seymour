\begin{lemma}
Two loopless multigraph structures on the same vertex and edge types are
equal if their endpoint maps are equal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{LooplessMultigraph}, the only data field is the endpoint map; the
remaining field is a proof that this map is loopless.  Equality of the data
fields is assumed, and proof irrelevance identifies the two looplessness
proofs.  Hence the two structures are equal.
\end{proof}
