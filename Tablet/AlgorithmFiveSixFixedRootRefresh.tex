\begin{theorem}[One fixed-root Algorithm 5.6 refresh]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph $G$ with individual edge type $E$, a
palette $[k]$, and a distinguished edge $e_0$.  Let $\mathcal E$ be a
positive-rung ETT of rung $n$, generating coloring $\varphi$, maximum
property, terminal ladder tree $T_n$, and hierarchy base $B$.  Assume
$\Delta(G)+1\leq k$, $11\leq t(G)$, and a
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} at bound $n$.  Assume also that, for
every arbitrary-input maximum ETT $\mathcal L$ of rung $n$ carrying a
base and a good hierarchy, its terminal ladder tree $L_n$ satisfies
\[
                 2n+11\leq |\overline\psi(L_n)|,
\]
where $\psi$ is the generating coloring of $\mathcal L$.

Let $U$ carry a corresponding ETT certificate $C_U$ relative to the
fixed original $\mathcal E$ and the same coloring $\varphi$, maximum
property for $C_U.\mathit{replacementETT}$, and a base $B_U$ whose core,
closure, and numerical support position equal those of $B$.  Let $H$ be
a hierarchy on $U$ over $B_U$, with $d$ dividers and good witness
$\mathcal G$.  Put
\[
 q=\operatorname{last}(d),\qquad
 A=H.\mathit{starred}(q),\qquad
 D^+=\noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}(H),\qquad
 \Gamma^- =\bigcup_{\theta\in D^+}\mathcal G.\Gamma^q_\theta.
\]
Assume that $U$ is $(\Gamma^-)^-$-closed but is not fully closed under
$\varphi$.

Finally retain an exact anchor tree $J$ and an augmentation sequence
$J\longrightarrow U$.  The anchor data are one of the following two
alternatives.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The terminal kind of $\mathcal E$ is PE, $J=B.\mathit{core}$,
and there is an exact $\varphi$-augmentation sequence
$T_n\longrightarrow J$; in this alternative
$|B.\mathit{core}|<|U|$.
\item The terminal kind is SE or RE, and there is an individual edge
$f_n$ such that the original connecting-edge set is exactly $\{f_n\}$
and $J=T_n+f_n$ as an exact one-edge tree-sequence; in these alternatives
$|T_n|<|U|$.
\end{enumerate}

Then there exist colors
$\alpha,\beta,\eta,\gamma_1,\gamma_2\in[k]$, an exact edge $f$, and a
tree-sequence $W$ with all of the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Both $V(A)$ and $V(U)$ are elementary under $\varphi$,
$|A|<|U|$, and both vertex sets have odd cardinality.  Moreover
$\alpha\in\overline\varphi(U)-\Gamma^-$ and $U$ is
$\alpha$-closed.
\item The color $\beta$ is missing on $U$, belongs to $\Gamma^-$, and
has a nonempty exact colored boundary.  The color $\eta$ belongs to
$D^+$, $\beta\in\mathcal G.\Gamma^q_\eta$, and $\eta$ is the unique
member of $D^+$ whose old pair contains $\beta$.  The chosen edge $f$
belongs to that exact $\beta$-boundary and satisfies
$\varphi(f)=\beta$.
\item The tree $W$ is the exact one-edge extension $U+f$, and
$U\longrightarrow W$ is a Tashkinov augmentation under $\varphi$.
The colors $\gamma_1\ne\gamma_2$ are each missing at a vertex of
$V(U)-V(A)$, the pair $\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2\}$ is disjoint from
$\Gamma^-$, and in particular it does not contain $\beta$.
\item The tree $W$ carries a corresponding certificate $C_W$ relative
to the fixed original $\mathcal E$, maximum property, and a base $B_W$
whose core, closure, and numerical support position equal those of $B$.
There is a hierarchy $H_W$ on $W$ with $d+1$ dividers and a good witness
$\mathcal G_W$.  Under the canonical inclusions of finite indices,
all stages $0,\ldots,d+1$, all old divider positions, all indexed stored
sets $0,\ldots,d$, and every old Gamma pair agree exactly with those of
$H$ and $\mathcal G$.  The new preceding stage and starred stage are
$U$, the new terminal stage is $W$, and the new divider position is
$|U|$.  At the new last indexed level the stored set is exactly $D^+$,
\[
 \mathcal G_W.\Gamma^{d+1}_\eta=\{\gamma_1,\gamma_2\},\qquad
 \mathcal G_W.\Gamma^{d+1}_\theta=
     \mathcal G.\Gamma^q_\theta
       \quad(\theta\in D^+,\ \theta\ne\eta).
\]
There is an exact augmentation sequence $J\longrightarrow W$.
\item There are a terminal tree $V$, a corresponding certificate $C_V$
relative to the same fixed original $\mathcal E$, maximum property, and
a base $B_V$ whose core, closure, and numerical support position equal
those of $B$, such that $|U|<|V|$ and $J\longrightarrow V$ is an exact
augmentation sequence.  The tree $V$ carries a hierarchy $K$ with
$d+1$ dividers and a good witness $\mathcal G_V$.  Its old stages,
divider positions, indexed stored sets, and Gamma pairs agree with those
of $H$ exactly as for $H_W$; its new preceding and starred stage are
$U$, its terminal stage is $V$, its new divider position is $|U|$, its
new indexed stored set is $D^+$, and its last Gamma family has exactly
the displayed replacement at $\eta$.  Finally
\[
 V\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\theta\in
   \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}(K)}
   \mathcal G_V.\Gamma^{d+1}_\theta\right)^-\text{-closed under }\varphi.
\]
\end{enumerate}
Every equality of stages above is equality of complete ordered
edge-valued tree-sequences.  In particular, $D^+$ is the old terminal
stored set, not the old last indexed stored set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{P2940_T1_StarredPrefixPreparation} to the fixed original
ETT, the supplied induction context, maximum witnesses, degree and
large-order bounds, uniform missing bound, corresponding certificate
$C_U$, bases, good hierarchy, core equality, and the two retained strict
anchor inequalities. It gives properness and exact one-uncoloredness of
$\varphi$, elementarity of $V(A)$ and $V(U)$, the exact prefix
$A\preccurlyeq U$, strictness $|A|<|U|$, oddness of $|V(A)|$, and
$|\Gamma^-|<|\overline\varphi(U)|$.

By \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColorsSubsetLast},
$D^+$ lies in the old last indexed stored set.
\noderef{GoodHierarchyReservedDisjoint} therefore puts every pair
indexed by $D^+$ inside $\overline\varphi(A)$. The disjointPairs field of
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} gives pairwise disjointness for these owners.
Apply \noderef{P2940_T1_NestedOddMissingPair} with the independently
supplied nested trees $A,U$, family indexed by $D^+$, the preparation
facts, the degree bound, the assumed minus-closedness, and the failure
of full closedness. It returns the five colors, exact edge $f$, and
one-edge tree $W$, proving all assertions in items 1--3, including
oddness of $V(U)$, uniqueness of the boundary owner, and disjointness
of the fresh pair from $\Gamma^-$.

We construct the certificates with the fixed original as their root.
The generating-color equation in \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}
identifies $\varphi$ with its actual terminal series coloring.
Reflexivity in \noderef{sc2}, using the properness and exact
one-uncoloredness just obtained, gives stability of $\varphi$ on
$T_n$ relative to that coloring and its computed stored set.
The currentSix field of \noderef{TechnicalInductionContext}, applied
to the original ETT and original maximum witness, gives
$\noderef{ModColoring}(\mathcal E,\varphi)$.

The augmentation $U\longrightarrow W$ supplied above is a singleton
chain in \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}. Concatenating it
with the retained route gives $J\longrightarrow W$.
The anchor hypotheses give $T_n\preccurlyeq J$: in PE this follows from
the retained augmentation route and
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment}; in SE/RE it is
the prefix clause of \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge} for the exact
stored connecting seed. Thus $T_n\preccurlyeq W$, by composing exact
vertex and edge entries in \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment}.
Also $|T_n|\leq|J|\leq|U|<|W|$, where the last inequality is the
one-edge length equation. In PE concatenate the retained
$T_n\longrightarrow J$ route with $J\longrightarrow W$.
In SE/RE retain precisely the given individual edge $f_n$, the
connecting-edge equality $\{f_n\}$, the exact seed $J=T_n+f_n$, and
the route $J\longrightarrow W$. These are respectively the two
eligibility alternatives of \noderef{ModColoring}.
Apply that predicate to obtain $C_W$ directly relative to $\mathcal E$.
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} gives its maximum property.
\noderef{HierarchyBaseCorrespondingTransport}, applied to the original
base $B$, returns $B_W$ with exactly the three required equalities.

Now apply \noderef{P2940_T1_OneDividerRefresh} to the supplied
$C_U,H,\mathcal G$ and the just constructed $C_W,B_W$. The equalities
of cores and closures relative to $B$ compose to the equalities
between $B_W$ and $B_U$ required there. Its remaining inputs are the
elementarity of $U$, the selected owner and old pair membership, the
exact edge color and one-edge extension, the distinct fresh colors and
their difference-missing memberships, pair disjointness, and the
assumed terminal minus-closedness. It returns $H_W$ with one additional
divider and its good witness, with all stage, divider, indexed
stored-set, total-family, and refreshed-owner equalities in item 4.
Its new last indexed stored set is exactly $D^+$.

For Step 2 apply \noderef{GoodHierarchyRestrictedAugmentation} to
the original ETT and its mod-coloring and maximum witnesses, with
$W,C_W,B_W,H_W,\mathcal G_W$. It returns a chain
$W\longrightarrow V$, a corresponding certificate $C_V$ already
relative to the same fixed original, maximum property, a base $B_V$,
a hierarchy $K$ of the same divider count as $H_W$, and a good witness.
It supplies equality of every nonterminal stage, starred stage,
indexed stored set, divider value, and total Gamma family with the
corresponding data of $H_W$, and the requested terminal
minus-closedness under its own terminal stored set.

Compose these equalities with those returned by the refresh helper.
The finite index casts all preserve numerical values. In particular,
the last index of $K$ maps to the last index of $H_W$, so their new
preceding stages and starred stages are $U$, their new indexed stored
sets are $D^+$, and their final Gamma families have exactly the same
replacement at $\eta$. The old stage indices $0,\ldots,d+1$,
old stored and Gamma indices $0,\ldots,d$, and old divider indices
$0,\ldots,d-1$ also preserve their values under both inclusions.
The new divider index $d$ therefore retains value $|U|$.
The terminalStage field of \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} makes the
new terminal stage of $K$ exactly $V$. Base core, closure, and
numerical support equalities compose back to $B$.
Finally concatenate $J\longrightarrow W$ with $W\longrightarrow V$
to obtain the retained-anchor route to $V$.
By \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment},
$|W|\leq|V|$, so the exact one-edge equation gives $|U|<|V|$.
This proves every clause of item 5 and completes the conclusion.
\end{proof}
