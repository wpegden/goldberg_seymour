\begin{definition}
For fixed \(G,k,e\), a bad pair \((\varphi,T)\) consists of a
\(k\)-triple \((G,e,\varphi)\) as in \noderef{KTriple} and a Tashkinov
tree \(T\) as in \noderef{TashkinovTree} whose vertex set
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} is not elementary in the sense of
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}.
\end{definition}
