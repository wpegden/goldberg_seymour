\begin{lemma}
Let $U$ be a finite set of vertices of $G^{\circ}$, and let
$\bar U\subseteq V(G)$ be its image under the injective map forgetting the
incidence witness.  Then $|\bar U|=|U|$, and, as finite sets of the original
edge identities,
\[
 E_{G^{\circ}}(U)=E_G(\bar U).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The forgetting map on \noderef{IncidentVertex} is injective because two
subtype elements with the same underlying vertex are equal.  Its image on
$U$ therefore has cardinality $|U|$.

Fix an edge identity $e$.  Suppose first that $e$ is internal to $U$ in the
sense of \noderef{InternalEdges}.  If an original vertex $v$ is incident
with $e$, then $(v,e)$ is an incident-core vertex and
\noderef{IncidentCoreIncidence} makes it incident with $e$ in the core.
Internality puts $(v,e)$ in $U$, so $v$ lies in $\bar U$.  Thus every
original endpoint of $e$ lies in $\bar U$, and $e\in E_G(\bar U)$.

Conversely, suppose $e\in E_G(\bar U)$ and let $x$ be a core vertex
incident with $e$.  By \noderef{IncidentCoreIncidence}, the underlying
vertex of $x$ is an original endpoint of $e$, hence belongs to $\bar U$.
It is therefore the underlying vertex of some $y\in U$.  Injectivity of the
forgetting map gives $x=y$, so $x\in U$.  Every core endpoint of $e$ lies in
$U$, proving $e\in E_{G^{\circ}}(U)$.  Membership is equivalent for each
individual $e\in E$, so the two internal-edge finsets are equal without
collapsing parallel identities.
\end{proof}
