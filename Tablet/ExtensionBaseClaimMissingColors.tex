\begin{lemma}[Claim 4.1(a3)--(a4): missing-color transport]
Use the active set \(L\) and support classes
\(L(w)\) from \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}.  Put
\(j=m(v_n)\leq n\),
and let \(\sigma_n\) be a proper coloring leaving exactly \(e_0\)
uncolored and \((T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)\)-stable.

First, if \(v\in V(T_j)\) is not the supporting vertex of any active
slot, then
\[
                    \overline\sigma_n(v)
                    =\overline\varphi_{j-1}(v).                \tag{1}
\]

Second, let the nonempty class (L(w)) have exact increasing enumeration
\(i_0<\cdots<i_p\), and assume this is also the exact enumeration through
round (n) of all PEs supported at (w).  Put
\[
 \mathcal S_w=\bigcup_{a=0}^{p}S_{i_a},qquad
 \gamma_0=\gamma_{i_0},\qquad \delta_p=\delta_{i_p}.
\]
Then
\[
 \overline\sigma_n(w)\cap\mathcal S_w=\{\delta_p\},            \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 \overline\varphi_{j-1}(w)
   =(\overline\sigma_n(w)\setminus\{\delta_p\})
       \cup\{\gamma_0\}.                                     \tag{3}
\]
These are exactly Claim 4.1(a3) and (a4).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The vertex clause of \noderef{StableColoring} gives
\[
                  \overline\sigma_n(x)=\overline\varphi_n(x) \tag{4}
\]
for every (x\in V(T_n)).  The certified ordered initial segments put
\(V(T_j)\), and all support vertices considered below, inside \(V(T_n)\).

Let (v\in V(T_j)) satisfy the first hypothesis.  No PE between stages
(j) and (n) is supported at (v): a PE there has index at least (j),
and (v\in V(T_j)) gives (m(v)\leq j), so such an index would belong
to the active set \noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}.  Apply
\noderef{HkuUnsupportedVertexColors} to the full interval.  It yields
\(\overline\varphi_{j-1}(v)=\overline\varphi_n(v)\), which combines
with (4) to prove (1).

For the class (L(w)), apply \noderef{SupportingPEStoredIntersection}
to its exact enumeration, with terminal bound (n).  It gives
\[
                  \overline\varphi_n(w)\cap\mathcal S_w
                  =\{\delta_p\}.                              \tag{5}
\]
Equation (4) at (w) changes the left side of (5) to
\(\overline\sigma_n(w)\cap\mathcal S_w\), proving (2).

Apply \noderef{SupportingPEMissingTransport} to the same enumeration.
Its endpoint identity is
\[
 \overline\varphi_{i_0-1}(w)
   =(\overline\varphi_{i_p}(w)\setminus\{\delta_p\})
      \cup\{\gamma_0\}.                                     \tag{6}
\]
Exactness of the enumeration says there is no PE supported at (w)
between (j) and (i_0-1), nor between (i_p+1) and (n).
Two applications of \noderef{HkuUnsupportedVertexColors} therefore give
\[
 \overline\varphi_{j-1}(w)=\overline\varphi_{i_0-1}(w),
 \qquad
 \overline\varphi_{i_p}(w)=\overline\varphi_n(w).             \tag{7}
\]
The range equality with \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass} ensures in
particular that (i_0\geq j), so both applications use the indicated
ordered intervals without an index shift.  Substitute (7) and then (4)
into (6).  The result is exactly (3), completing (a4) and the proof.
\end{proof}
