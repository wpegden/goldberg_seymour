\begin{lemma}
For every finite multigraph $G$, a proper $\chi'(G)$-edge-coloring exists,
and if a proper $k$-edge-coloring exists then $\chi'(G)\le k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The existence assertion \noderef{ColoringExists} makes the set of feasible
color counts nonempty.  By the least-number definition in
\noderef{ChromaticIndex}, its least member is feasible, and it is no larger
than every other feasible member.  These are exactly the two asserted
properties.
\end{proof}
