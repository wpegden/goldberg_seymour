\begin{helper}[The two block-deleted recolorings are Tashkinov]
Fix a defective color $\delta$ and a
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData} $R$.  Put
$i=\gammaIndex(\delta)$, $j=R.j$, $\gamma=\gamma_i$, and
$\eta=\gamma_j$.  Let $\varphi_2$ be obtained by interchanging
$\alpha_0,\gamma$ outside $T$ and then switching the full terminal
component through $v^0_\delta$.  Let $\psi_2$ be the coloring in
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSecondTerminalComponent}.

There are tree-sequences $S_\gamma,S_\eta$ such that
\[
 \noderef{TashkinovTree}(G,e_0,\varphi_2,S_\gamma),\qquad
 V(S_\gamma)=(V(T)\setminus U_\gamma)\cup\{R.u_1\},
\]
\[
 \noderef{TashkinovTree}(G,e_0,\psi_2,S_\eta),\qquad
 V(S_\eta)=(V(T)\setminus U_\eta)\cup\{u^0_\delta\}.
\]
Moreover $\varphi_2(R.f_1)=\delta$ and
$\psi_2(f^0_\delta)=\delta$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Write $X=\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(T)$.  Apply
\noderef{ScheideSelectedBlockDeletion} first at $i$ and then at $j$.
It gives exact tree-sequences $T^-_\gamma,T^-_\eta$, retaining the base
and every other block in their old relative order, with
\[
 V(T^-_\gamma)=X\setminus U_\gamma,\qquad
 V(T^-_\eta)=X\setminus U_\eta.
 \tag{1}
\]
Its edge classification says that every retained edge is either a base
edge or belongs to a block whose index differs from the deleted one, and
that every such edge is the same exact edge identity as an edge of $T$.
The data in \noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup} put $v^0_\delta$ in the exit
set, so
\noderef{ScheideStructuralExitsInBase} puts it in the base; the return
datum \noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData} puts $R.v_1$ in the base.
Consequently the base-retention clause of
\noderef{ScheideSelectedBlockDeletion} puts both vertices in each
deleted sequence, before every retained block vertex.

The old Tashkinov witnesses used below are available because the
\noderef{GlobalMaximumTriple} stored in
\noderef{ScheideReducedConfiguration}, through its inherited
\noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration}, contains
\noderef{TashkinovTree}$(G,e_0,\varphi,T)$.

For the first recoloring, invoke
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveTerminalComponent}.  The outside swap fixes
every edge incident with $T$, and its switched component meets $X$ only
at $v^0_\delta$ and contains no displayed tree-edge node.  Hence every
edge retained in $T^-_\gamma$ has its original value under
$\varphi_2$.  At every vertex of $X$ other than $v^0_\delta$ the
component switch preserves the whole missing-color set; at
$v^0_\delta$ it turns the known missing color $\gamma$ into a missing
color $\delta$.  These claims follow edge-by-edge from the exact
component assertion and
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapMissingBehavior}, using the
$\gamma$-missing datum at $v^0_\delta$ from
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup}.

We verify the Tashkinov witnesses edge by edge.  If a retained nonfirst
edge is a base edge, its old Tashkinov certificate supplies a missing
witness at an earlier base vertex.  The base-avoidance field of
\noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration} says that its color is not
$\gamma$.  If the retained edge belongs to a block $r\ne i$, the block
color and base-witness fields of that same node say that its color is
$\gamma_r$ and that $\gamma_r$ is missing at a base vertex; injectivity
of the block colors gives $\gamma_r\ne\gamma$.  In either case the color
is used by a nonfirst displayed edge of $T$, so
\noderef{ScheideGlobalMaximumUnused}, interpreted through
\noderef{TreeUsedColors}, says that it is not the defective color
$\delta$.  The unaffected-color clause of
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapMissingBehavior} therefore preserves the
chosen missing witness.  The exact-edge clause of
\noderef{ScheideSelectedBlockDeletion} and the no-displayed-edge clause
of \noderef{ScheideDefectiveTerminalComponent} preserve the retained
edge's color.  Hence every nonfirst edge of $T^-_\gamma$ satisfies the
definition of \noderef{TashkinovTree} under $\varphi_2$.

The exact edge $R.f_1$ is fixed by the outside swap because it is
incident with $R.v_1\in X$; this membership, the endpoint equation
$\operatorname{ends}(R.f_1)=\{R.v_1,R.u_1\}$, and the old color
$\varphi(R.f_1)=\delta$ are all fields of
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData}.  If its edge-node belonged to the
switched component, adjacency at $R.v_1$ would put that vertex-node in
the component.  The singleton tree-vertex conclusion of
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveTerminalComponent} would then give
$R.v_1=v^0_\delta$, contrary to the inequality in
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData}.  Thus
$\varphi_2(R.f_1)=\delta$.

The same return datum \noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData} says that
$R.u_1\notin X$.  Since $R.v_1$ is retained in $T^-_\gamma$, equation
(1) and the exact endpoint equation put $R.f_1$ in
\noderef{BoundaryEdges}$(G,V(T^-_\gamma))$.  The setup datum
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup} says that $\gamma$ was missing at the
retained earlier vertex $v^0_\delta$; the component missing-color
interchange just proved makes $\delta$ missing there.  Thus
\noderef{TreeSequenceBoundaryEdgeAppendExists} appends
$R.f_1,R.u_1$ to give $S_\gamma$.  Unfolding the resulting
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}, old edge obligations transport through
the initial-segment maps, while the unique new last-edge obligation uses
the displayed color $\delta$ and this earlier missing witness.  Therefore
$S_\gamma$ is Tashkinov.

For the vertex set, the same initial-segment maps include
$V(T^-_\gamma)$ in $V(S_\gamma)$, and the attachment equation for the
new edge puts $R.u_1$ in $V(S_\gamma)$.  By the injective vertex-map
clause of \noderef{TreeSequence}, a tree-sequence of length $m$ has
$m+1$ vertices; the adjoin has length one larger, and
$R.u_1\notin V(T^-_\gamma)$.  The inclusion and equal cardinalities
therefore give
$V(S_\gamma)=V(T^-_\gamma)\cup\{R.u_1\}$, which with (1) is the first
asserted equality.

The second construction is symmetric but uses the independently proved
component in \noderef{ScheideDefectiveSecondTerminalComponent}.  The
outside swap fixes all tree-incident identities, and the switched
component meets $X$ only at $R.v_1$ and contains no displayed tree edge.
Thus all edges retained in $T^-_\eta$ keep their original values.  A
retained nonfirst base-edge color avoids $\eta$ by the base-avoidance
field of \noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration}.  A retained block
edge has color $\gamma_r\ne\eta$ by the block-color and injectivity
fields of that node, and its recorded base missing witness is retained.
As above \noderef{ScheideGlobalMaximumUnused} makes either retained
edge color distinct from $\delta$, so the unaffected-color clause of
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapMissingBehavior} preserves the missing
witness.  The exact-edge classification from
\noderef{ScheideSelectedBlockDeletion} now proves, edge by edge, that
$T^-_\eta$ is Tashkinov under $\psi_2$.

The same helper gives $\psi_2(f^0_\delta)=\delta$ and makes $\delta$
missing at the retained base vertex $R.v_1$; the latter base membership
is a field of \noderef{ScheideDefectiveReturnData}.  The exact attachment
$\operatorname{ends}(f^0_\delta)=\{v^0_\delta,u^0_\delta\}$, its old
color $\delta$, the exit membership of $v^0_\delta$, and
$u^0_\delta\notin X$ are fields of
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup}.  The exit membership and
\noderef{ScheideStructuralExitsInBase} retain $v^0_\delta$ in
$T^-_\eta$.  Hence the endpoint equation puts $f^0_\delta$ directly in
\noderef{BoundaryEdges}$(G,V(T^-_\eta))$.  Append it using
\noderef{TreeSequenceBoundaryEdgeAppendExists}.  The identical
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge} argument transports every old
Tashkinov obligation and uses the $\delta$-missing witness at $R.v_1$
for the new last edge.  It also gives
$V(S_\eta)=V(T^-_\eta)\cup\{u^0_\delta\}$ by the same inclusion and
cardinality calculation.  Together with (1), these are the second
Tashkinov certificate and the second exact vertex-set equality, and the
two displayed color equalities have already been established.
\end{proof}
