\begin{helper}[Corresponding maximum ETT at an augmented endpoint]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung ETT with maximum property, let
$\sigma$ be a mod-coloring for $\mathcal E$, and suppose that $J$ carries
a \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate relative to
$\mathcal E$ and $\sigma$.  If there is a
$\sigma$-\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} from $J$ to $U$, then
$U$ carries a corresponding ETT relative to the same original
$\mathcal E$ and $\sigma$, and that replacement ETT has maximum property.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let $T_n$ be the common last ladder tree.  The given corresponding
certificate and \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} give exact ordered initial
segments $T_n\preccurlyeq J\preccurlyeq Z$, where $Z$ is the final tree of
the replacement series, and $|T_n|<|J|$.  The augmentation sequence from
$J$ to $U$ gives $J\preccurlyeq U$ by induction on
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}; hence $T_n\preccurlyeq U$ and
$|T_n|<|U|$.

Inspect the certified last step of the replacement series.  Positivity of
the rung excludes the initial constructor of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  In the PE case its closure supplies a
$\sigma$-augmentation sequence $T_n\leadsto Z$.  Applying
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} to
$T_n\preccurlyeq J\preccurlyeq Z$ yields $T_n\leadsto J$, and appending
the given $J\leadsto U$ route yields $T_n\leadsto U$.

In the SE or RE case, the last-step constructor supplies the individual
connecting edge $f_n$, its exact one-edge seed $S=T_n+f_n$, and a closure
route $S\leadsto Z$.  It also gives $|S|=|T_n|+1\leq |J|$.  Both $S$ and
$J$ are exact prefixes of $Z$: the former follows along the closure route,
and the latter is part of the corresponding ETT.  Comparing their entries
through $|S|$ gives $S\preccurlyeq J$.  Restriction now yields
$S\leadsto J$, which appends to $J\leadsto U$.  The singleton
connecting-edge equation in the SE or RE constructor and connecting-edge
agreement in \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} identify the
same exact edge $f_n$ on the original series.

Thus, in every possible last-step case, all hypotheses of the universal
extension clause in \noderef{ModColoring} hold for $U$: the exact prefix
and strict length conditions were proved above, and the appropriate PE or
SE/RE augmentation route has just been constructed.  That clause supplies
a corresponding ETT on $U$.  Finally
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport}, applied to the original maximum
property and this corresponding certificate, gives maximum property for
the replacement ETT.
\end{proof}
