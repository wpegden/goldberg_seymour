\begin{helper}[Corresponding maximum ETT at an augmented endpoint]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung ETT with maximum property, let
$\sigma$ be a mod-coloring for $\mathcal E$, and suppose that $J$ carries
a \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate relative to
$\mathcal E$ and $\sigma$.  If there is a
$\sigma$-\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} from $J$ to $U$, then
$U$ carries a corresponding ETT relative to the same original
$\mathcal E$ and $\sigma$, and that replacement ETT has maximum property.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let $T_n$ be the common last ladder tree and let $Z$ be the final tree of
the replacement series carried by the certificate on $J$.  The ladder-tree
agreement in \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}, followed by the
lower and upper exact-prefix fields of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}, gives
\[
 T_n\preccurlyeq J\preccurlyeq Z,
 \qquad |T_n|<|J|.                                      \tag{1}
\]
By \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment}, the given
$J\leadsto U$ sequence gives the exact ordered prefix
$J\preccurlyeq U$.  Composing the length inequalities and the entrywise
vertex and edge equalities in \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} with
(1) yields
\[
 T_n\preccurlyeq U,
 \qquad |T_n|<|U|.                                      \tag{2}
\]

The certified-step field of \noderef{TashkinovSeries} supplies a
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} certificate at the last replacement-series
index.  Positivity of the rung makes that index positive, so its certificate
cannot be the initial constructor.  In the PE constructor, the displayed
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} has seed $T_n$ and endpoint $Z$; its route field
is therefore a $\sigma$-\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}
$T_n\leadsto Z$.  Applying
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} to that route and the two
prefixes in (1) gives $T_n\leadsto J$.  Since an augmentation sequence is
the reflexive-transitive closure of the one-step relation, its transitivity
appends the given $J\leadsto U$ route and gives
$T_n\leadsto U$.                                         \tag{3}

In the SE or RE case, the last-step constructor supplies the individual
connecting edge $f_n$, its exact one-edge seed $S=T_n+f_n$, and a closure
route $S\leadsto Z$.  The exact seed relation is
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}$(T_n,f_n,S)$, so
$|S|=|T_n|+1$ and $T_n\preccurlyeq S$.  The strict inequality in (1)
therefore gives $|S|\leq |J|$.  The closure route is the route field of
\noderef{TashkinovClosure}, and
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment} makes $S$ an exact
prefix of $Z$.  Since $J\preccurlyeq Z$ as well, compare the two prefixes
entry by entry through $|S|$: the defining clauses of
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} identify each entry of $S$ with the
same entry of $Z$ and then with the corresponding entry of $J$.  Together
with $|S|\leq|J|$, this proves $S\preccurlyeq J$.  Restriction gives
$S\leadsto J$, and transitivity of
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} appends $J\leadsto U$ to obtain
\[
                         S\leadsto U.                  \tag{4}
\]
The singleton connecting-edge equation in the SE or RE constructor and
connecting-edge agreement in
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} identify this exact edge
$f_n$ with the original series' connecting edge; this is equality of edge
identities, not merely equality of endpoint pairs.

Thus, in every possible last-step case, all hypotheses of the universal
extension clause in \noderef{ModColoring} hold for $U$: the exact prefix
and strict length conditions are (2), while (3) is the PE route and (4),
together with the exact one-edge seed, is the SE/RE route.  That clause
supplies a corresponding ETT on $U$.  Finally
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport}, applied to the original maximum
property and this corresponding certificate, gives maximum property for
the replacement ETT.
\end{proof}
