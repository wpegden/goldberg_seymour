\begin{lemma}
If $\varphi$ is a proper partial $k$-edge-coloring of $G$ and $F\subseteq
E(G)$, then its restriction $e\mapsto\varphi(e)$ to the retained edge
identities is a proper partial $k$-edge-coloring of $G|F$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $e$ and $f$ be distinct adjacent edges of $G|F$ that are both colored.
By \noderef{RestrictEdges}, their underlying edge identities are distinct
edges of $G$ with unchanged endpoint pairs.  Thus their adjacency in $G|F$
implies their adjacency in $G$.  The properness condition of
\noderef{IsProperEdgeColoring} for $\varphi$ in $G$ now says that their
colors differ, which is precisely properness of the restricted coloring.
\end{proof}
