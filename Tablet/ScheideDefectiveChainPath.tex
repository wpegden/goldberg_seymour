\begin{definition}
For a tree $T$, colors $\alpha,\gamma$, and $u\in V(T)$, a
\emph{full defective-chain path} from $u$ to $z$ is a simple path in the
$(\alpha,\gamma)$ incidence graph which starts at the vertex-node $u$,
ends at a vertex-node $z\notin V(T)$, and whose support is the entire
two-color component containing $u$.  Its incidence support includes the
individual edge-node of every multigraph edge on the chain.
\end{definition}
