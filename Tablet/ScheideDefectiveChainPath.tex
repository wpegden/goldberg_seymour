\begin{definition}
Let $T$ be a tree-sequence, let $\varphi$ be a partial edge-coloring, and
let $P$ be an $(\alpha,\gamma)$-incidence walk from the vertex-node $u$ to
the vertex-node $z$.  The predicate that $P$ is a \emph{full
defective-chain path} for $(T,\varphi,\alpha,\gamma,u,z)$ means that
$u\in V(T)$, the walk $P$ is simple, $z\notin V(T)$, and the support of
$P$ is exactly the full $(\alpha,\gamma)$-component containing $u$.  The
incidence support includes the individual edge-node of every multigraph
edge identity on the chain.
\end{definition}
