\begin{definition}
For a tree-sequence $T$ and partial coloring $\varphi$, a vertex $v$ is a
\emph{Scheide exit vertex} if there are a tree vertex $u$, a color
$\gamma$ missing at $u$, a defective color $\delta$, and the full
$(\delta,\gamma)$ incidence-chain from $u$ to an outside end such that
$v$ is the last vertex of $V(T)$ on that chain in the order starting at
$u$.  The witness contains the entire incidence path, and therefore every
individual parallel-edge identity on the chain.
\end{definition}
