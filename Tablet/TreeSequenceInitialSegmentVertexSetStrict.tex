\begin{lemma}[A longer initial-segment extension strictly grows the vertex set]
Let $A$ and $B$ be tree-sequences for the same finite loopless multigraph
and the same distinguished first edge.  Suppose that $A$ is an exact
ordered \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} of $B$ and that
$|A|<|B|$, where $|T|$ denotes the number of edge entries of $T$.
Then
\[
  \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(A)
  \subsetneq \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(B).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The inclusion $V(A)\subseteq V(B)$ follows from
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset}.

It remains to prove that equality is impossible.  By
\noderef{TreeSequence}, the vertex map of a tree-sequence $T$ has domain
$\{0,\ldots,|T|\}$ and is injective.  Hence the definition of
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} gives
\[
                         |V(T)|=|T|+1.                         \tag{1}
\]
If $V(A)=V(B)$, then taking finite cardinalities and applying (1) to both
trees gives $|A|+1=|B|+1$, hence $|A|=|B|$.  This contradicts the assumed
strict inequality $|A|<|B|$.  Thus the inclusion is strict.
\end{proof}
