\begin{lemma}[Step 1, Subcase 2.1: the third end is outside the starred predecessor]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph retaining individual parallel-edge
identities, let $e_0$ be distinguished, and let $[k]$ be the color set.
Let $T$ be a positive-rung certified ETT with generating coloring
$\varphi$, maximum property, properness, and exactly $e_0$ uncolored.
Assume the complete six-part lower-rung package.  Let $H$ be a good
hierarchy over a hierarchy base, with $d<q_0$ dividers.  Assume uniformly
that every same-rung maximum ETT built from an arbitrary certified input
and generating coloring and having a good hierarchy with fewer than
$q_0$ dividers is elementary.  Assume the large bound
$|\overline\varphi(T_n)|\geq2n+11$, the separate terminal certificate
\[
 T\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\eta\in D^H_{n,d+1}}\Gamma^d_\eta\right)^-
 \text{-closed under }\varphi,
\]
and elementarity of $V(T)$.  Put $A=H.\mathit{starred}(d)$.

Let $\alpha\ne\beta$, with $\alpha$ missing and singleton-closed on $T$
and $\beta$ not missing on $T$.  Let $P_1,P_2,P_3$ be pairwise
support-disjoint exact exit paths for $\alpha,\beta$.  Their outside ends
$a_1,a_2,a_3$ and tree ends $b_1,b_2,b_3$ are pairwise distinct and
$b_1\prec_T b_2\prec_T b_3$.  Retain, for every $j$, the exact
$\beta$-colored boundary-edge identity $f_j$ on $P_j$ incident with
$b_j$.  Suppose $b_3\notin V(A)$.

Assume the following universal secondary-minimality property, ranging
over arbitrary certified replacement inputs rather than only over paths
on the current display.  Let $\sigma$ be any proper coloring leaving
exactly $e_0$ uncolored and stable from $\varphi$ on the final ladder
tree.  Let $T'$ be the exact terminal tree of any corresponding
same-rung ETT under $\sigma$, with maximum property, a hierarchy base,
a good hierarchy $K$ with exactly $d$ dividers, its own large estimate,
and its separate terminal minus-closed certificate.  For distinct colors
$a,b$, suppose $a$ is missing and singleton-closed on $T'$ and two exact
support-disjoint $(a,b)$ exit legs have distinct outside ends.  Such a
replacement is forbidden if either
\begin{enumerate}
\item $b$ is missing on $T'$, so its secondary coordinate is zero; or
\item $b$ is not missing and there is a third exact exit leg, pairwise
support-disjoint from the first two and with a third outside end, such
that the total of the three exact walk lengths is strictly less than
$|P_1|+|P_2|+|P_3|$.
\end{enumerate}
Then the displayed Subcase 2.1 data are contradictory.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{Step1CaseTwoOutsidePrefixTwoEdges} to the displayed data.
It returns the exact recoloring, three transformed exit paths, corresponding
maximum ETT, and the good hierarchy on the prefix through $b_3$ with the
individual edges $f_1,f_2$ adjoined.  Feed that complete witness package,
together with the stated full replacement-family no-better hypothesis, to
\noderef{Step1CaseTwoOutsideRestrictedAugmentation}.  That lemma performs
the finite restricted augmentation and produces the forbidden shorter
presentation, hence gives the required contradiction.
\end{proof}
