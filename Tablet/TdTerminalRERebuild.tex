\begin{helper}[Terminal RE reconstruction]
Let (T) be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree generated by
\[
 \mathcal T=\{(T_i,\varphi_{i-1},S_{i-1},F_{i-1},\Theta_{i-1}):
                   1\leq i\leq n+1\},
\]
and suppose that (T) has the maximum property.  Keep the graph (G),
palette size (k), and distinguished uncolored edge (e_0) fixed, but
allow the certified input coloring to vary arbitrarily.  Assume explicitly
the following six assertions for every positive-rung ETT (T') on these
fixed data, with arbitrary certified input coloring \(\varphi'_0\), maximum
property, and rung number (s<n), writing \(\varphi'_s\) for its generating
coloring and \(\mathcal T'\) for its certified series:
\begin{enumerate}
\item (V(T')) is elementary with respect to \(\varphi'_s\).
\item Under every proper \((T'_{s+1},D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (T'_{s+1}) is interchangeable for
any pair of colors of which at least one is missing on (T'_{s+1}).
\item For each PE slot (a\leq s) with supporting vertex (u_a) and
(m(u_a)=b), every proper \((T'_a,D'_a,\varphi'_a)\)-stable coloring
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored is
\((T'(u_a)-u_a,D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  When (b>1), it is
also \((T'_{b-1},D'_{b-1},\varphi'_{b-1})\)-stable.  Moreover, the stored
color pairs at two distinct PE slots with distinct supporting vertices
and the same first-appearance index are disjoint.
\item If the terminal slot is PE with supporting vertex (u_s) and
stored colors \(\gamma'_s,\delta'_s\), then under every proper
\((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving precisely (e_0)
uncolored, the \((\gamma'_s,\delta'_s)\)-component through (u_s)
contains exactly the one vertex (u_s) of (T'_s).
\item If \(\tau\) is any proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable
coloring leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored, (d) is defective on
(T'_s) under \(\tau\), and (x) is a nonminimum vertex of
\(I[\partial_{\tau,d}(T'_s)]\), then
(x\preceq_{T'}u_a) for every PE supporting vertex or SE extension
vertex (u_a) with (m(x)\leq a\leq s).
\item Every proper \((T'_s,D'_s,\varphi'_s)\)-stable coloring leaving
precisely (e_0) uncolored is a \(\varphi'_s\bmod T'_s\) coloring.
\end{enumerate}

Assume in addition that the terminal extension kind is RE.  Then every proper \((T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)\)-stable coloring \(\sigma_n\)
leaving precisely (e_0) uncolored is a
\(\varphi_n\bmod T_n\) coloring in the sense of
\(\noderef{ModColoring}\).  In particular, the conclusion is universal
over every eligible ordered tree-sequence strictly extending (T_n),
and not merely over one selected closure.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix the terminal stable coloring and an arbitrary eligible strict extension.  Apply
\noderef{TdReplacementPrefixExact}.  Its two branches are exhaustive: when the
rung number is one it reconstructs the initial certified closure directly, and
when it is larger it restricts to rung n-1, invokes the lower mod-coloring
assertion, and normalizes the last closure so that the replacement prefix ends
at the exact ordered tree T_n.  Thus we have a replacement prefix with the
same ladder trees, raw stored sets, exact connecting-edge sets, and kinds, and
with the transported preterminal coloring.

Let h be the earlier PE slot supplied by the terminal
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate}.  At h, apply
\noderef{TdEarlierPEWitnessIdentification} to the old and replacement PE
constructors.  Equality of their singleton connecting-edge sets fixes the
individual support edge.  Its endpoint equation, together with the facts that
the support endpoint is in the common tree and the other endpoint is outside,
fixes the orientation.  Equality of the two stored pairs is then oriented by
the post-switch missing/present status, yielding equality of delta and gamma.
This is done separately at h; no equality of nondeterministic witnesses is
read from the corresponding-ETT structure.

The certificate says every positive slot strictly between h and n is RE.
Their current-coloring equations are literal equalities, so the original and
replacement PE exit components persist to the two preterminal colorings.
The return path of the old certificate uses only the two stored colors and
edges incident with T_n.  Stability therefore copies every exact path edge,
including the connecting edge, into the replacement coloring.  Its full
two-color component meets the earlier tree at the return vertex, which differs
from the PE support because the PE delta is missing there.  It is consequently
distinct from the persisted PE exit component.

If the copied component were a path component, these would be two distinct
path components meeting T_n while one of their colors is missing there.
The lower-rung interchangeability assertion, applied to the replacement
prefix with transported maximum property, contradicts
\noderef{TreeInterchangeable}.  The degree-at-most-two classification
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSpanningPath} therefore supplies a full cycle.
The cycle crosses the boundary of T_n twice.  Closedness excludes the missing
delta from both crossings, so both have color gamma; hence T_n is not strongly
closed.

Using this cycle, the unchanged exact connecting edge and endpoints, and the
copied return path gives every field of a replacement
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate}.  Append the RE constructor to the
exact replacement prefix, and use the composed augmentation route from its
one-edge seed through the arbitrary strict extension to a closure.  The final
series agrees with the original in every ladder tree, stored set, exact
connecting-edge set, and kind, and its positive colorings are stable relative
to the originals.  It therefore yields the required
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} for the arbitrary extension.
Properness and exact one-edge uncoloredness were hypotheses, so all clauses of
\noderef{ModColoring} hold.
\end{proof}
