\begin{helper}[No revisiting extension after positive-prefix replacement]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph $G$ with individual edge identities, a
palette $[k]$, and an edge $e_0$.  Let $\mathcal E$ be an
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} with input coloring $\varphi_0$, displayed
tree $T$, certified series $\mathcal S$, generating coloring $\varphi$,
rung number $n>1$, and \noderef{MaximumProperty}.  Write
\[
 A_i=\mathcal S.\mathit{tree}(i)\quad(0\leq i\leq n+1),
 \qquad \varphi_i=\mathcal S.\mathit{coloring}(i)\quad(0\leq i\leq n),
 \qquad r=n-1.
\]
Write $S_i,F_i,\Theta_i$ for its raw stored sets, exact connecting-edge
sets, and extension kinds, and put
$D_i=\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}(\mathcal S,i)$.
Assume the following two assertions for every positive-rung ETT
$\mathcal L$ with rung $s<n$, arbitrary input and generating colorings
on the fixed $G,k,e_0$, and maximum property.  Denote its series by
$\mathcal S_{\mathcal L}$, its last output tree by
$U=\mathcal S_{\mathcal L}.\mathit{tree}(s+1)$, its terminal ladder tree
by $B=\mathcal S_{\mathcal L}.\mathit{tree}(s)$, its final series coloring
by $\psi_s$, and its computed stored set by $D_s^{\mathcal L}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every proper $(U,D_s^{\mathcal L},\psi_s)$-stable coloring $\tau$
leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored makes $U$ interchangeable for each pair
$\alpha,\beta$ with at least one color missing on $U$.
\item If the terminal kind of $\mathcal L$ is PE and its chosen vertex,
gamma, and delta fields are respectively $v,\gamma,\delta$, then every
proper $(B,D_s^{\mathcal L},\psi_s)$-stable coloring $\tau$ leaving exactly
$e_0$ uncolored satisfies
\[
 w\in V(B)\ \Longrightarrow
 \bigl(w\in P_v(\gamma,\delta,\tau)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ w=v\bigr).
\]
Here $P_v$ denotes the full incidence-graph component, including isolated
vertices, as in \noderef{TwoColorComponent}.
\end{enumerate}
Let $\sigma$ be proper and leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored.  Assume that
$\varphi_r$ is also proper and leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and that
$\sigma$ is $(A_n,D_r,\varphi_r)$-stable.

Let $\mathcal P$ be a maximum ETT with the same input $\varphi_0$,
displayed tree $A_n$, generating coloring $\varphi_r$, rung number $r$,
and certified series $\mathcal S^-$.  Require the exact retained maps
\[
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{tree}(i)=A_i\quad(0\leq i\leq r+1)
\]
and, for every $0\leq i\leq r$,
\[
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{coloring}(i)=\varphi_i,\quad
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{stored}(i)=S_i,\quad
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{connectingEdges}(i)=F_i,\quad
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{kind}(i)=\Theta_i,\quad
 \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}(\mathcal S^-,i)=D_i.
\]
Let $C$ be a \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate
for $\mathcal P$ under $\sigma$ on $A_n$.  Its replacement input may
differ from $\varphi_0$.  Write $\widehat{\mathcal S}$ for its replacement
series and $\widehat{\mathcal P}$ for its replacement ETT.  Thus the
replacement rung is $r$, its series is precisely
$\widehat{\mathcal S}$, its generating coloring is $\sigma$, its
positive ladder trees agree with those of $\mathcal P$, its raw stored
sets, exact connecting-edge sets, and kinds agree at every index, all
its colorings are proper and leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and each
positive coloring is stable on the corresponding original ladder tree
with the original computed stored set.  In addition require explicitly
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal S}.\mathit{tree}(r+1)=A_n,\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal S}.\mathit{coloring}(r)=\sigma,
 \qquad \noderef{MaximumProperty}(\widehat{\mathcal P}).
\]
Finally assume that there is no
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate} for the original series
$\mathcal S$ at current index $r$.
Then there is no \noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate} for
$\widehat{\mathcal S}$ at its final index $r$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Suppose a replacement revisiting certificate exists.  By
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate} it specifies a positive index
$h\leq r$, a PE at $h$, an ordered pair $\gamma,\delta$, RE kinds at
every index strictly after $h$ through $r$, a full two-color cycle
$\widehat O$ under $\sigma$, and an individual boundary edge $f$ of
$A_n$ on that cycle.  It also specifies inside and outside endpoints
$a,b$ with $G.\mathit{ends}(f)=\{a,b\}$, and a simple incidence-graph
return path $L$ from the edge-node $f$ to a vertex $w\in V(A_h)$.
Every graph-vertex node of $L$ lies in $V(A_n)$, and every node of $L$
lies on $\widehat O$.

The kind agreements make the slot $h$ PE in $\mathcal S^-$ as well.
Inspect the actual certificates supplied by \noderef{TashkinovSeries}
at this slot.  The initial, SE, and RE constructors of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} contradict the PE kind; thus both
certificates are PE.  Write their selected edge, support, outside
endpoint, delta, and gamma as
$(f_h,v_h,u_h,\delta_h,\gamma_h)$ and their starred counterparts.
The equality of singleton connecting-edge sets identifies the exact
edges.  The endpoint equations in \noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}
and the common ladder tree identify their inside endpoints and outside
endpoints: the crossed identification would put an outside endpoint in
that tree.  The equality of raw stored sets identifies their unordered
color pairs, but does not yet orient those pairs.

For either PE certificate, its gamma rule
\noderef{ParallelExtensionColor} makes gamma missing at its selected
vertex in the selected coloring.  The second-stable coloring retains
this missingness and the selected edge's delta value by
\noderef{StableColoring}.  Consequently gamma and delta are distinct.
The switch \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} puts gamma on that exact
edge, so gamma is present at the support after the switch.  Delta is
missing there after the switch, by
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapMissingBehavior}.  Positive-stage
stability of $C$ identifies the post-switch missing sets at the common
support.  Apply \noderef{TdEarlierPEWitnessIdentification}, with the
common singleton edge, common unordered pair, and these missing and
present statuses.  It identifies both ordered colors as well as the
edge, support, and outside endpoint.  In particular the common ordered
colors are the certificate's $\gamma,\delta$.

Inspecting each certified RE in the intervening interval shows that its
new coloring is literally its predecessor.  The kind agreements copy
the entire interval to $\mathcal S^-$.  Induction through its indices
therefore gives
\[
 \mathcal S^-.\mathit{coloring}(h)=\varphi_r,\qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal S}.\mathit{coloring}(h)=\sigma.
\]
In these respective colorings, delta is missing and gamma is present at
the common support $v_h$.

We verify the full root-component intersection claims using actual
certified lower ETTs.  For either $\mathcal P$ or
$\widehat{\mathcal P}$, if $h=r$ use that ETT itself.  If $h<r$, apply
\noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at rung $h$, with its positive-index
and strict-rung hypotheses.  This supplies a positive ETT, its maximum
witness, all retained tree and data maps, and its certified history.
The terminal ladder tree is exactly $A_h$, and the terminal support,
gamma, delta, kind, and coloring are precisely the ones just identified.
The series-presentation and rung fields of $C$ identify the replacement
ETT's series with the supplied replacement series, so this also applies
on the replacement side.  These ETTs all have rung strictly below $n$.
Their post-PE colorings are proper, leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and
are stable relative to themselves by the defining clauses of
\noderef{StableColoring}.  Apply the second lower-rung assertion.
Both root components meet $V(A_h)$ only at $v_h$.  Since the root misses
delta and has an incident gamma edge, each component is nontrivial and
its root has degree one; the degree bound
\noderef{TwoColorIncidenceDegreeAtMostTwo} gives a path component in the
sense of \noderef{TwoColorPathComponent}.  The literal coloring
equalities through the RE interval preserve these entire components,
not merely their roots.

Next copy the return path, without asserting that $\widehat O$ is fixed.
The certified ordered-extension fields of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} compose to exact
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} relations from each positive
prefix ladder tree to $A_n$.  In particular the support $v_h$ and the
return vertex $w$ lie in $V(A_n)$.  Both colors lie in the raw pair
$S_h$.  Expanding \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors} at the positive index
$r$, each is either in $D_r$ or missing on $A_r$.  The exact prefix
relation $A_r\preccurlyeq A_n$ carries a missing witness in the latter
case into $A_n$, by \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset}.
Thus
\[
 \{\gamma,\delta\}\subseteq\overline\varphi_r(A_n)\cup D_r.
\]
The path $L$ cannot be empty, since its endpoints have different node
types.  Every edge-node on it therefore has an adjacent graph-vertex
node on the path.  This vertex is in $A_n$ by the certificate's internal
path field, so the exact edge is incident with $A_n$.  Its replacement
color is gamma or delta, as follows directly from adjacency in
\noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph}.  The reverse-value implication of
\noderef{sc1}, applied to the assumed
$(A_n,D_r,\varphi_r)$-stability and proper one-uncolored colorings,
fixes each such edge.  Transfer $L$ edge by edge into the original
incidence graph.  Its node sequence, support, endpoints, and simplicity
are unchanged.  Denote the transferred path by $L_0$.

Let $Q$ be the full original two-color component containing the
edge-node $f$.  The transferred path puts $w$ in $Q$.  The edge $f$
itself occurs on $L$, so it too retains its two-color value.  The
structural endpoint equation then puts the outside endpoint $b$ in
$Q$, although $b\notin V(A_n)$.

We have $w\ne v_h$.  Indeed, $w$ lies on $\widehat O$, whereas $v_h$
has degree at most one under $\sigma$, since delta is missing there.
For precision, \noderef{MissingColorIncidenceEndpoint} gives that
degree bound.  Rotating the cycle to a hypothetical vertex
$w=v_h$ would give two distinct cycle neighbors there, a contradiction.
The original root component meets $A_h$ only at $v_h$, while $Q$
contains $w\in A_h$; hence these components are distinct.

Apply the first lower-rung assertion to $\mathcal P$, whose last output
is exactly $A_n$ by the retained tree maps.  Its last coloring is
$\varphi_r$, proper and one-uncolored by hypothesis and stable relative
to itself.  Delta is missing at $v_h\in V(A_n)$, so the required
tree-missing premise holds.  It follows that $A_n$ is interchangeable
for gamma and delta.  The original root component is a path component
meeting $A_n$; $Q$ is a distinct component also meeting $A_n$ at $w$.
Apply \noderef{TwoColorInterchangeableDistinctComponentCycle}.  It
produces a full original \noderef{TwoColorCycle} $O$ whose support is
exactly $Q$.  Thus the copied path lies on $O$, and $O$ contains both
$w$ and the outside endpoint $b$.

Finally inspect the original series' certified slot $h$.  The retained
raw kind, stored-pair, singleton-edge, and tree maps identify its PE
constructor.  Repeat the earlier witness identification, now between
this constructor and the prefix constructor.  Their post-PE colorings
are literally equal by the retained coloring map.  This proves equality
of their exact edge, support, outside endpoint, and ordered gamma and
delta, again by \noderef{TdEarlierPEWitnessIdentification}; it does not
assume that the retained maps already include chosen vertices or
ordered colors.

Construct a \noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate} for $\mathcal S$
at index $r$.  Copy $h$, its positivity and upper bound, and the
intervening RE interval through the pointwise kind maps.  Use the PE
kind and ordered colors just identified.  Take the cycle to be $O$,
the boundary edge to be the individual edge $f$, and the inside and
outside endpoints to be $a,b$.  Boundary membership and the endpoint
equation are structural and are unchanged because the replacement
current tree is exactly $A_n$.  The cycle meets $A_h$ at $w$ and leaves
$A_n$ at $b$.  Use $w$ as return vertex and $L_0$ as return path.
Its simplicity is preserved by transfer; every node lies in
$Q=\operatorname{supp}(O)$; and its graph-vertex nodes lie in $A_n$
by the copied internal-path field.  These verify every certificate
field, contradicting the assumed original no-revisiting premise.
\end{proof}
