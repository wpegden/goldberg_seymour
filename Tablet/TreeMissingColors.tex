\begin{definition}
For a tree-sequence $T$ and a partial coloring $\varphi$, the set of colors
missing on $T$ is
\[
 \overline\varphi(T)=\bigcup_{v\in V(T)}\overline\varphi(v).
\]
Thus a color belongs to $\overline\varphi(T)$ exactly when it is missing at
at least one vertex of the tree-sequence.
\end{definition}
