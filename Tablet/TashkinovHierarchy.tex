\begin{definition}
Let $T$ be the terminal ETT and let $T_{n,0}^{*}$ be a hierarchy base.
A \emph{hierarchy} of $T$ is an ordered chain
\[
 T_n=T_{n,0}\subset T_{n,1}\subset\cdots\subset
 T_{n,q}\subset T_{n,q+1}=T
\]
of proper tree-sequence segments.  It also records positions of vertices
$u_1\prec\cdots\prec u_q$ in $T$ such that
$T_{n,i}=T(u_i)$ for $1\leq i\leq q$; these vertices are the dividers.
The starred base is required to be an ordered initial segment of
$T_{n,1}$.  In the PE case this is exactly the requirement
$T_n\vee R_n\subseteq T_{n,1}$, while otherwise it reduces to
$T_n\subseteq T_{n,1}$.  Every containment retains the displayed order
and the individual edge identities.
\end{definition}
