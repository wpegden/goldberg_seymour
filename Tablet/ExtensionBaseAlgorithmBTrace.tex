\begin{definition}
Fix the Case 1 vertex \(u\), the exact increasing support-class
enumeration
\(L_1=\{1_1<\cdots<1_{p+1}\}\), and the selected position \(b\) for
which \(\gamma_{1_{b+1}}\) is the first missing gamma at \(u\).  An
Algorithm (B) trace is a finite list of exact certified slots enumerating
\(L_1^*=\noderef{ExtensionBaseCaseOneIndices}\) in strictly decreasing
order, together with every intermediate coloring.  Each listed slot is
certified to have PE kind and records its exact position in the
enumeration, its certified gamma and
delta fields, and the equality saying its chosen PE support is the
terminal vertex \(v_n\).  The first coloring is \(\varrho_1\), and the
successor coloring is obtained by switching the two-color component
through the fixed vertex \(u\):
\[
 \varrho^{a+1}=\varrho^a/P_u
       (\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\varrho^a).
\]
All slot equalities are equalities of the certified finite indices, not
only equalities of their numerical values.
\end{definition}
