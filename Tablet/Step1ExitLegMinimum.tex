\begin{definition}
A \emph{Step 1 exit-leg minimum} of size $(n,q)$ is a Step 1 exit-leg
presentation $\mathcal P$ together with the following certificates.
First, its terminal vertex set is elementary.  Second, no corresponding
same-rung maximum replacement, equipped with its own proper
exactly-one-uncolored stable coloring, large estimate, terminal
minus-closed good hierarchy, and two qualifying exit legs, has fewer
dividers than $\mathcal P$.  Third, either the second color of
$\mathcal P$ is missing on its terminal tree, or $\mathcal P$ contains
a third exit leg.  In the latter case the three exact exit legs are
pairwise support-disjoint, have distinct outside and tree ends ordered
by the last two tree ends, retain the exact second-color boundary edge
on the third leg, and no corresponding presentation with the same
divider count has its second color missing or has a qualifying triple
of smaller total walk length.
\end{definition}
