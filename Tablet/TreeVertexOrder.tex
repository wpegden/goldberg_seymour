\begin{definition}
The ordered vertices of a tree-sequence
$T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_n,y_n)$ induce the strict order $\prec_T$ on
$V(T)$: one has $u\prec_T v$ exactly when $u=y_i$ and $v=y_j$ for indices
$i<j$.  Thus the order remembers positions in the displayed sequence, not
only the underlying vertex set.
\end{definition}
