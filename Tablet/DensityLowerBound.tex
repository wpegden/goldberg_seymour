\begin{lemma}
Every finite multigraph $G$ satisfies
$\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil\le\chi'(G)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $k=\chi'(G)$ and take a full proper $k$-edge-coloring from
\noderef{ChromaticIndexSpec}.  Fix an eligible odd vertex set $U$ of size
$n$.  Within $E_G(U)$, the edges of any fixed color form a matching: if two
distinct edges of that color shared a vertex, they would be adjacent and
properness would be violated.  A matching on the odd set $U$ covers two
vertices per edge and therefore has at most $(n-1)/2$ edges.  Summing this
bound over all $k$ color classes counts every edge of $E_G(U)$ exactly once
and gives
\[
  2|E_G(U)|\le k(n-1).
\]
Because $n\ge3$, division by the positive number $n-1$ yields
$2|E_G(U)|/(n-1)\le k$.  This holds for every eligible $U$, while the
adjoined degenerate value $0$ is also at most $k$.  Taking the maximum in
\noderef{Density} gives $\Gamma(G)\le k$.  Since $k$ is a natural number,
the least natural upper bound in \noderef{DensityCeil} satisfies
$\lceil\Gamma(G)\rceil\le k=\chi'(G)$.
\end{proof}
