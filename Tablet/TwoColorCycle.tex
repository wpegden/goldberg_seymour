\begin{definition}
For a partial coloring \(\varphi\) and colors \(\alpha,\beta\), a full
\((\alpha,\beta)\)-cycle is a cycle walk in the two-color incidence graph
of \(\varphi\).  Its vertices are tagged elements of \(V(G)\sqcup E(G)\):
graph vertices and individual edge identities alternate along the cycle.
Consequently two parallel edges remain different members of the cycle, even
when they have the same endpoints.
\end{definition}
