\begin{definition}
For a vertex set (X), coloring (arphi), and color (delta), a
vertex (v) belongs to (I[\partial_{\varphi,\delta}(X)]) if
(v\in X) and (v) is incident with at least one edge identity in the
(delta)-colored boundary (\partial_{\varphi,\delta}(X)).  Parallel
boundary edges retain their individual identities.
\end{definition}
