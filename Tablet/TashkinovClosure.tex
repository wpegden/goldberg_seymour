\begin{definition}
A tree-sequence $T'$ is a closure of $T$ under $\varphi$ if it is reached
from $T$ by a Tashkinov augmentation sequence under $\varphi$ and its final
vertex set $V(T')$ is closed with respect to $\varphi$.  The latter
condition is equivalent to saying that no further Tashkinov augmentation is
available: a further step would require a boundary edge whose color is
missing on $T'$.
\end{definition}
