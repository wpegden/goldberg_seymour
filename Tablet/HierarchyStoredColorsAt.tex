\begin{definition}
For $0\leq j\leq q$, let
\[
 D_{n,j}=D_n-\overline{\varphi_n}(T_{n,j}^{*}).
\]
Here $D_n$ is the accumulated stored-color set at the terminal rung and
$T_{n,j}^{*}$ is the starred hierarchy stage.  Since every starred stage
contains $T_n$, this is the paper's set
$\bigcup_{h\leq n}S_h-\overline{\varphi_n}(T_{n,j}^{*})$ and in
particular $D_{n,j}\subseteq D_n$.
\end{definition}
