\begin{definition}
At a current series index (i), an available revisiting extension is
certified by an earlier positive index (h\leq i) with
\(\Theta_h=\mathrm{PE}), carrying its ordered connecting colors
\((\gamma_h,\delta_h)), and by a full
\((\gamma_h,\delta_h))-cycle (O) under \(\varphi_i\).  The cycle must
meet both (V(T_h)) and (V(G)\setminus V(T_{i+1})).

The certificate further chooses an individual edge identity
\(f\in O\cap\partial(T_{i+1})), records its inside and outside endpoints,
and supplies a path in the incidence-cycle from the edge-node (f) back to
a vertex of (T_h).  Every graph-vertex node on this return path lies in
\(V(T_{i+1})\), and every node of the path lies on (O).  Thus this is
exactly the RE witness tested with priority in Algorithm 3.1, expressed in
the incidence graph so that parallel edge identities are not collapsed.
\end{definition}
