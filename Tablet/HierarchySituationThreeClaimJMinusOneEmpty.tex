\begin{lemma}[Situation 7.3, Claim \(j-1\): empty case]
Fix a rung-local minimum hierarchy counterexample \(C\), set

\[
 j=\noderef{HierarchyPathNumber}(C.\mathit{hierarchy}),\qquad
 \ell=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailLength}(C),
\]

and assume \(2\le j<\ell\).  Assume every vertex has a missing color
under every proper exactly-one-uncolored replacement coloring.  If
\(\noderef{HierarchyEligibleCommonMissingIndices}\) is empty, then there
nevertheless are an actual
\noderef{HierarchyReplacementPresentation} \(P\) and a color \(\alpha\)
missing under \(P.\mathit{coloring}\) both at the terminal vertex and
somewhere in the exact prefix \(T(y_{j-2})\), such that, with the notation
\(D=D_{n,q}\), \(\Gamma^q=\bigcup_{\eta\in D}\Gamma^q_\eta\), and
\(M_0=\overline\varphi(P.\mathit{replacementBase}.\mathit{core}-T_n)\),
either

\[
                       \alpha\notin\Gamma^q\cup M_0,          \tag{A}
\]

or \(\alpha\in\Gamma^q_\eta\) for some \(\eta\in D\) whose first
missing vertex occurs no later than \(y_{j-2}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $H=C.\mathit{hierarchy}$, let $q$ be its last stored index, and write
\[
 A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q),\quad
 B_{\rm pen}=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,\ell-1),\quad
 Y=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,j-2).
\]
Use $D,\Gamma^q,M_0,T_n$ as in the statement, for the original data
when no replacement is specified. The fields of
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} give an exact
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} $A\preccurlyeq T$:
at divider zero use the core-inside-first field and at positive divider
count use the last nesting field. The definition of
\noderef{HierarchyFullTailLength} gives $\ell=|T|-|A|$.
Since $2\le j<\ell$, one has $\ell\ge3$ and the exact prefix chain
\[
 A\preccurlyeq Y\preccurlyeq B_{\rm pen}\prec T,\qquad
 |Y|=|A|+j-2,\qquad |B_{\rm pen}|=|T|-1.
\]
These inclusions are entrywise, including every individual edge identity.
The inclusion $A\preccurlyeq Y$ can be equality at $j=2$.

We describe the identity presentation used below. For every slot of
the certified \noderef{TashkinovSeries}, properness and exact
one-uncoloredness follow by cases on its
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}: the initial case uses the two coloring
clauses of its \noderef{KTriple}; SE uses
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}'s selected-coloring clauses; PE uses
the explicit current-coloring clauses; RE copies the previous coloring.
Choose the same input, series, and ETT for the reflexive
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}; use reflexive rung,
presentation, ladder, stored-set, connecting-edge, and kind equalities,
the original generating-coloring equality, the just verified slotwise
coloring laws, and reflexive \noderef{StableColoring} at each positive
slot. Define $P_0$ by coloring $\varphi$, that certificate, maximum
$C.\mathit{maximum}$, base $\mathcal B$, counterexample $C$, reflexive
\noderef{SameGoodHierarchy}, and the supplied context $\mathit{ctx}$.
These are all seven fields of
\noderef{HierarchyReplacementPresentation}.

Apply \noderef{HierarchyCounterexamplePenultimateElementary} to
the six projections of the supplied minimum context, in order
$\mathit{proper}$, $\mathit{oneEdgeUncolored}$, $\mathit{lowerRung}$,
$\mathit{fewerDividers}$, $\mathit{lexicographicMinimality}$, and
$\mathit{largeMissingSet}$. This derives elementarity of $B_{\rm pen}$;
it is not an assumed context field.
Apply \noderef{HierarchyCounterexampleTerminalCommonMissing} with
the same ambient data and the same six projections to obtain
\[
 \alpha\in\overline\varphi(B_{\rm pen})\cap\overline\varphi(y_\ell).
\]

We first establish the elementary-prefix reduction used twice below,
now registered as \noderef{P2905_T2_EmptyCommonMissingLocation}.
Let $P$ be any original-relative
\noderef{HierarchyReplacementPresentation} and let $c$ be missing
at $y_\ell$ and on the original exact $B_{\rm pen}$ under its coloring.
Expanding \noderef{TreeMissingColors}, choose a missing witness
$v=B_{\rm pen}.\mathit{vertex}(x)$, with $0\le x\le|B_{\rm pen}|$.
If $x\le|A|$, the exact-prefix vertex equality puts that witness in $A$.
If $x>|A|$, put $i=x-|A|$. Then $1\le i\le\ell-1$ and
\[
 |\,\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,i)\,|=|A|+i=x .
\]
Thus its last vertex is exactly $v$. The original-relative $P$, color
$c$, and the two missing witnesses satisfy the defining filter of
\noderef{HierarchyEligibleCommonMissingIndices} at $i$.
That makes the family nonempty, contrary to the empty-case hypothesis.
Hence every such $c$ is missing on $A$.
Apply this reduction to $P_0,\alpha$. It follows that $\alpha$ is
missing on $A$ and therefore on $Y$; transfer of witnesses uses
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset}.

If $\alpha\notin\Gamma^q\cup M_0$, the identity presentation and
$\alpha$ give alternative (A). Otherwise consider the Gamma membership.
If $\alpha\notin\Gamma^q$, it belongs to $M_0\setminus\Gamma^q$.
Apply \noderef{P2905_T2_BaseExtraReplacement} with the fixed data,
context, $2\le j<\ell$, terminal missingness, the just derived
missingness on $A$, and these two membership conditions.
It returns $P,\beta$ missing at $y_\ell$ and on the original $A$,
outside the forbidden union computed from $P$'s own base, ladder,
stored sets, and Gamma pairs. Transfer the $A$-witness to $Y$ using
the exact-prefix inclusion. This proves alternative (A) with $P,\beta$.
The helper performs the counted ladder-missing choice and orients the
starred-tail stable change with that newly selected color first.

It remains that $\alpha\in\Gamma^q$. Expanding the finite biunion,
choose $\eta\in D$ with $\alpha\in C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^q_\eta$.
First suppose $\eta$ is not missing on $B_{\rm pen}$.
Apply \noderef{P2905_T2_AbsentOwnerReplacement} to these memberships
and the same ambient, context, range, and two $\alpha$-missing data.
It gives $P,\beta$ missing at $y_\ell$ and on the original
$X=T(y_{\ell-2})$, with $\beta$ outside $P$'s forbidden union.
The prefix $X$ lies in $B_{\rm pen}$, so $\beta$ is missing on
$B_{\rm pen}$. Apply the empty-family reduction above to this actual
original-relative $P,\beta$. It puts $\beta$ on $A\preccurlyeq Y$,
which completes alternative (A). Here the helper uses FullTail:
the absent owner does not satisfy a strict split-prefix cutoff.

Finally suppose $\eta$ is missing on $B_{\rm pen}$.
The definition \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt} makes
$\eta\notin\overline\varphi(A)$. Its actual least missing position
$i_\eta=\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}(\varphi,T,\eta)$
is no later than its $B_{\rm pen}$-witness, hence
$i_\eta\le|T|-1$. It is strictly after $|A|$: otherwise its least
witness would belong to $A$, contrary to stored membership.
Set $t=i_\eta-|A|$. Then $1\le t<\ell$, the exact last vertex of
$T(y_t)$ is the first missing vertex, and $\eta$ is actually missing
there. If $i_\eta\le|Y|$, use $P_0,\alpha$ and this owner to obtain
the Gamma alternative in the conclusion.

Otherwise $i_\eta>|Y|=|A|+j-2$, so $t\ge j-1$.
Apply \noderef{P2905_T2_LateOwnerReplacement} with $t$, its positive,
upper, and late bounds, the exact first-position equality, and the
actual missing witness, in addition to the same fixed data and Gamma
memberships. It gives an original-relative $P$ under whose coloring
$\eta$ is missing at both $y_t$ and $y_\ell$. Thus $t$ belongs to
the defining finite filter of
\noderef{HierarchyEligibleCommonMissingIndices}. The family is
nonempty, and \noderef{HierarchySituationThreeClaimJMinusOneNonempty},
with the original context, path-number bounds, and the stated
every-vertex-missing-color assumption, gives exactly the required
conclusion. The late-owner helper supplies the genuine SplitTail
reconstruction, including the actual terminal certificate and its
zero-divider seed exception, rather than a prefix repackaging alone.
These alternatives exhaust the empty case.
\end{proof}
