\begin{definition}
Let $T$ be a tree-sequence and let $\alpha\ne\beta$.  A coloring
$\sigma$ is obtained from $\varphi$ by an
\emph{outside $(\alpha,\beta)$ Kempe recoloring} if one may choose
finitely many full $(\alpha,\beta)$-components of $\varphi$, each having
no vertex in $V(T)$, such that $\sigma$ exchanges $\alpha$ and $\beta$
on exactly the edge-nodes lying in the union of those components and
fixes every other edge value.  Components are represented by roots in
\noderef{TwoColorComponent}; repeated roots for the same component have
no effect.  The definition retains each edge identity separately.
\end{definition}
