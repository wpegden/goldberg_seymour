\begin{lemma}
If the vertex type of $G$ is finite, then its incident-vertex type
$V^{\circ}(G)$ is finite.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IncidentVertex}, $V^{\circ}(G)$ is a subtype of the finite type
$V$.  A subtype of a finite type is finite: enumerate $V$ and retain exactly
the vertices for which an incident edge identity exists.  This gives a finite
enumeration of $V^{\circ}(G)$.
\end{proof}
