\begin{lemma}[Interchangeability on the terminal tree constituent of a PE]
Let $T$ be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree constructed from a
$k$-triple $G,e_0,\varphi$, with arbitrary certified input coloring,
terminal ladder tree $T_n$, terminal generating coloring $\varphi_n$,
and terminal stored-color set $D_n$.  Assume that $T$ has maximum
property and that its terminal extension is a PE.  Let $R_n$ and
$T_n\vee R_n$ be the closure and exact ordered union in a hierarchy
base under $\varphi_n$ (respectively `base.closure' and `base.core'
in Lean).

Assume, uniformly over every certified input coloring and generating
coloring, that every positive-rung ETT $T'$ of rung number $s<n$ with
maximum property satisfies all six lower-rung assertions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V(T')$ is elementary under its generating coloring;
\item under every proper coloring leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored and
stable on its final ladder closure, that closure is interchangeable for
two colors whenever at least one of them is missing there;
\item stability descends at each PE support to the prefix before the
support and to its first-appearance ladder stage, and stored pairs at
distinct PE slots with distinct supports and the same first-appearance
index are disjoint;
\item at a terminal PE, the stored two-color component through the
support meets the terminal ladder tree only at that support;
\item every nonminimum endpoint of a defective boundary color precedes
each later PE support or SE extension vertex allowed by its
first-appearance index; and
\item every proper terminal stable coloring leaving exactly $e_0$
uncolored is a mod-coloring of the ETT.
\end{enumerate}

Let $\sigma_n$ be proper, leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and be
$T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n$-stable.

For arbitrary $\alpha,\beta\in[k]$,
\[
 \alpha\in\overline\sigma_n(T_n)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \alpha,\beta\text{ are $T_n$-interchangeable under }\sigma_n.
\]
All boundaries, trees, and two-color components use the original edge
type, so distinct parallel edges retain their identities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Eliminate the certified terminal step using
\noderef{TashkinovSeries} and its PE-kind equation.  The PE constructor
of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} supplies the exact support $v_n$,
connecting-edge identity $f_n$, and colors $\gamma_n,\delta_n$, along
with the last-slot chosen-vertex, gamma, and delta equalities required by
\noderef{InterchangePETreeAwayFromDelta}.  Its selected coloring has
$\gamma_n$ missing at $v_n$ by \noderef{ParallelExtensionColor}, while
the exact identity $f_n$ is incident there and has color $\delta_n$ by
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}.  Properness makes these colors
distinct.  The terminal \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} changes that
unique incident $\delta_n$-edge to $\gamma_n$, so $\delta_n$ is
missing at $v_n$ under $\varphi_n$; the vertex clause of
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring} transfers this missingness to
$\sigma_n$.

The parallel field of \noderef{HierarchyBase} puts $v_n$ in the
ordered prefix of $T_n$ used to generate $R_n$.  The augmentation
sequence in \noderef{TashkinovClosure} retains that prefix, so
$v_n\in V(T_n)\cap V(R_n)$, and hence $\delta_n$ is missing on both
constituents.  Applying
\noderef{InterchangePECommonMissingClosed} shows that $\delta_n$ is
closed on the exact ordered union $T_n\vee R_n$.

Now let $\alpha$ be missing on $T_n$.  If
$\alpha\ne\delta_n$ and $\beta\ne\delta_n$, apply
\noderef{InterchangePETreeAwayFromDelta} to the exact terminal data.
If $\alpha=\delta_n$, then $\delta_n$ is missing and closed on the
core, so \noderef{InterchangePEClosedCore} makes
$\delta_n,\beta$ interchangeable on the core.  Expanding
\noderef{TreeInterchangeable}, two path components meeting $T_n$ also
meet the core, so this conclusion restricts to $T_n$.

The only remaining case has $\beta=\delta_n$.  Apply
\noderef{InterchangePEClosedCore} with the colors in reverse order:
it gives $\delta_n,\alpha$ interchangeability on the core.
The definitions \noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph},
\noderef{TwoColorComponent}, \noderef{TwoColorPathComponent}, and
\noderef{TreeInterchangeable} are invariant when their two displayed
colors are exchanged.  Hence $\alpha,\delta_n$ is interchangeable on
the core and therefore on $T_n$.  This proves the claim in every case,
including singleton path components.
\end{proof}
