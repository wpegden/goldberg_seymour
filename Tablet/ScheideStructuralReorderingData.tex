\begin{definition}
Given a \noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration} $C$ and a
\noderef{ScheideStructuralSelectionData} $D$, a \emph{Scheide structural
reordering datum} is a tree-sequence $T'$ of the same length as $T$ whose
vertices, in zero-based order, are
\[
 C.y(q),\quad
 C.\operatorname{blockVertex}(D.\upsilon(h),j),\quad
 C.\operatorname{blockVertex}(D.\rho(t),j),
\]
over respectively $q<C.p$, $h<D.a,j<2$, and $t<D.r,j<2$, and whose exact
edge identities, in zero-based order, are
\[
 C.\operatorname{baseEdge}(q),\quad
 C.\operatorname{blockEdge}(D.\upsilon(h),j),\quad
 C.\operatorname{blockEdge}(D.\rho(t),j),
\]
over respectively $q<C.p-1$, $h<D.a,j<2$, and $t<D.r,j<2$.  The second
vertex interval starts at $C.p$, the third at $C.p+2D.a$; the analogous
edge intervals start at $C.p-1$ and $C.p-1+2D.a$.  The datum additionally
certifies that $T'$ is a \noderef{TashkinovTree}, that
$V(T')=V(T)$ as defined by \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}, and that its
exact \noderef{ScheideExitVertices} set under $\varphi$ equals
that of $T$.
\end{definition}
