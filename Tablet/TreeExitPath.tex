\begin{definition}
Let $P$ be a path in the two-color incidence graph
$I_G(\varphi;\alpha,\beta)$ from the vertex-node $u$ to the vertex-node
$v$.  It is a $T$-exit path if it uses at least one multigraph edge
(equivalently, its incidence-graph length is at least two), $u\notin V(T)$,
$v\in V(T)$, the only vertex-node of $P$ belonging to $V(T)$ is $v$, and
at least one of $\alpha,\beta$ is missing at $u$.  The vertex $v$ is the
tree end of the exit path.
\end{definition}
