\begin{lemma}[Scheide replacement pairs are equal or disjoint (18)]
For any two defective colors $\delta,\delta'$, the two-vertex sets
$U_\delta$ and $U_{\delta'}$ are either equal or disjoint.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The common color $\alpha_0$ is missing at $y_0$, which belongs to every
$Z_{\gamma(\delta)}$, and differs from every block color.  Therefore
\noderef{ScheideReplacementInternalMissing}, applied with
$\alpha=\alpha_0$, supplies an $\alpha_0$-colored edge identity internal
to each $U_\delta$.

Suppose $U_\delta$ and $U_{\delta'}$ share a vertex $u$.  Properness of the
original coloring, supplied by the globally maximum field in
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveSetup}, says that at most one
$\alpha_0$-colored edge identity is incident with $u$.  Hence the two
internal $\alpha_0$ edges just obtained are the same edge identity.
Looplessness gives it exactly two distinct endpoints, so the two unordered
endpoint sets are equal.  If there is no shared vertex, the finite sets are
disjoint.  This proves the stated alternative while retaining the internal
edge identity rather than collapsing parallel edges.
\end{proof}
