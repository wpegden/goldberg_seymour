\begin{definition}
For a type $V$, $\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)$ is the type of unordered pairs of
elements of $V$: $(u,v)$ and $(v,u)$ determine the same unordered pair.
Membership $v\in p$ means that $v$ is one of the two entries of the unordered
pair $p$.  We use finite types and their finite subsets in the standard sense,
and for a nonnegative rational number $q$ write $\lceil q\rceil$ for its
least natural-number upper bound.
\end{definition}
