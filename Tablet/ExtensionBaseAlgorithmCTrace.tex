\begin{definition}
Fix an ETT, its terminal supporting vertex \(v_n\), the exact terminal
PE slot \(n\), a coloring \(\mu_0\), and a vertex \(u\).  The trace data
certifies that \(n\) is the final series slot and belongs to the exact
support class \(L_1\).  An
\emph{Algorithm (C) trace} is a finite witness for the decreasing-order
Kempe changes on
\[
 L_1^*=L_1\setminus\{n\},\qquad
 L_1=\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}(v_n).
\]
It consists of a natural number of steps, an exact PE slot \(t_a\), exact
colors \(\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a}\), and a coloring \(\mu^a\) at every
stage, subject to the following conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\mu^0=\mu_0\), and the image of the listed exact slots is
      precisely \(L_1^*\);
\item \(t_0>t_1>\cdots>t_{r-1}\) in the original certified slot order;
\item every \(t_a\) is a PE supported at \(v_n\), and its displayed gamma
      and delta are exactly the certified fields at that slot; and
\item every successor coloring is the component switch
\[
 \mu^{a+1}=\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}
   (G,\mu^a,\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\mathsf{inl}(u)).
\]
\end{enumerate}
The image equality and strict decrease retain exact finite slot identities;
the component switch retains exact graph-edge identities, including
parallel edges.  If \(L_1^*=\varnothing\), the trace has no steps and its
sole coloring is \(\mu_0\).
\end{definition}
