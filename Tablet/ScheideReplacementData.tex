\begin{definition}
Fix a defective color $\delta$ in a defective-color setup.  Its
\emph{replacement data} name the two boundary edge identities other than
$f^0_\delta$ that have color $\delta$, have their tree ends in
$Z_{\gamma(\delta)}=V(T)\setminus
\{u^1_{\gamma(\delta)},u^2_{\gamma(\delta)}\}$, and have their other ends
$u^1_\delta,u^2_\delta$ outside $V(T)$.  The three identities are required
to be pairwise distinct and to be exactly all such edges.  The data also
record the replacement ordered tree-sequence $T_\delta$ exactly: entries
before the deleted block keep their positions, later old entries shift by
two positions, and $f^1_\delta,u^1_\delta,f^2_\delta,u^2_\delta$ occupy
the final four positions.  Its vertex set is
$Z_{\gamma(\delta)}\cup\{u^1_\delta,u^2_\delta\}$ and its edge identities
are exactly those of $T$ after deleting the two named
$\gamma(\delta)$-block edges and adjoining the two named
$\delta$-edges.  This records the source's replacement construction
without identifying parallel edges having equal endpoints.
\end{definition}
