\begin{definition}
Let \(T\) be an extended Tashkinov tree with \(n\) rungs and certified
series \(\mathcal T\).  Its ordered ladder-stage family is indexed by
\(j=0,\ldots,n\).  For \(j<n\), stage \(j\) is the complete ordered
tree-sequence \(T_{j+1}\) stored by \(\mathcal T\), while stage \(n\) is
the displayed extended Tashkinov tree \(T\) itself.  Thus the family is
exactly
\[
                     T_1,T_2,\ldots,T_n,T,
\]
with all vertex and edge identities and their order retained.
\end{definition}
