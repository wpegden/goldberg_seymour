\begin{definition}
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a loopless multigraph.  Its \emph{incident core}
$G^{\circ}$ is the loopless multigraph with vertex type $V^{\circ}(G)$ and
with exactly the original edge type $E$.  If the endpoint pair of an edge
identity $e$ in $G$ is $\{u,v\}$, then its endpoint pair in $G^{\circ}$ is
the unordered pair consisting of $u$ and $v$ regarded as incident vertices,
using $e$ itself as the witness of incidence.  In particular, different
parallel edge identities remain different edges of $G^{\circ}$.
\end{definition}
