\begin{definition}
For a hierarchy counterexample \(C\), let \(T\) be its terminal tree, let
\(H\) be its hierarchy, and put
\(A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,\operatorname{Fin.last}
(H.\mathit{dividerCount}))\).  For every natural number \(i\), define the
totalized \emph{\(i\)-th full-tail prefix} by
\[
 T_C(i)
   =T\big|_{\min\{|A|+i,|T|\}}.
\]
The ordered vertices and exact edge identities are those of \(T\) up to this
length.  This definition depends only on \(C\) and is distinct from the least
path-tail index used later in the hierarchy argument.
\end{definition}
