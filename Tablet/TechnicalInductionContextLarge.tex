\begin{theorem}[Large-order staged technical induction]
Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite loopless multigraph, retaining the identity of
each edge, let \(e_0\in E\), and let the palette have \(k\) colors.  Assume

\[
  \noderef{MaximumDegree}(G)+1\leq k
  \qquad\hbox{and}\qquad
  11\leq\noderef{TashkinovOrder}(G).
\]

Then, for every natural number \(n\), there is a
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} for \(G,k,e_0\) up to rung \(n\).
Thus all six technical assertions hold for every positive-rung maximum ETT
of rung strictly below \(n\), while assertions (iii)--(vi) hold uniformly
for every maximum ETT whose rung is exactly \(n\), with arbitrary certified
input and generating colorings throughout.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We use strong induction on \(n\).  Fix \(n\), and assume that a
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} has already been constructed at every
bound \(s<n\).  We first assemble the six conclusions for an arbitrary
positive-rung ETT

\[
 \mathcal E:\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}(G,k,e_0,\rho,T,\varphi)
\]

with rung \(s<n\) and \noderef{MaximumProperty}.  The induction hypothesis
at \(s\) gives a technical context \(C_s\).  Its four current-rung fields
already give assertions (iii), (iv), (v), and (vi) for \(\mathcal E\), with
all dependent trees, stored sets, colorings, first-appearance indices, and
membership witnesses belonging to \(\mathcal E\).

For assertion (i), apply
\noderef{TechnicalCurrentRungElementaryLarge} to \(\mathcal E\), its maximum
property, \(C_s\), and the two numerical hypotheses.  Its conclusion is
exactly that

\[
 \noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}
   (G,\varphi,\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet}(T)).              \tag{1}
\]

We next derive assertion (ii), rather than adding it as a field of the
context.  Apply \noderef{RutgersCurrentRungInterchangeability} to
\(\mathcal E\).  We verify each of its four functional premises.

First, consider any maximum ETT \(\mathcal E'\) whose rung equals \(s\),
with arbitrary input, displayed tree, and generating coloring.  Transport
\(C_s\) along the given rung equality and apply
\noderef{TechnicalCurrentRungElementaryLarge}; this proves current-rung
elementarity for \(\mathcal E'\).  Second and third, the
\texttt{currentFour} and \texttt{currentSix} fields of \(C_s\), applied to
the same rung equality and maximum property, give respectively the exact
terminal PE one-vertex component assertion and the terminal mod-coloring
assertion.  Finally, for every positive-rung maximum ETT of rung strictly
below \(s\), the \texttt{lowerTwo} field of \(C_s\) gives the required
lower-rung interchangeability assertion.  The degree hypothesis is the
one fixed above.  All premises of
\noderef{RutgersCurrentRungInterchangeability} therefore hold, so its
conclusion gives assertion (ii) for the exact final series tree of
\(\mathcal E\), under every proper, exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored stable
coloring.  Together with (1) and the four current fields, this proves all
six assertions for every such \(\mathcal E\) of rung \(s<n\).  Call this
uniform conjunction \(A_{<n}\).

We now construct the ten fields of the context at bound \(n\).  For each
of \texttt{lowerOne}, \texttt{lowerTwo}, \texttt{lowerThree},
\texttt{lowerFour}, \texttt{lowerFive}, and \texttt{lowerSix}, take the
corresponding component of \(A_{<n}\).  This gives exactly the six lower
fields specified by \noderef{TechnicalInductionContext}, including their
arbitrary-input quantification and their strict inequalities
\(0<s<n\).

It remains to build the four current fields.  Let
\(\mathcal F\) be an arbitrary maximum ETT whose rung equals \(n\).  Its
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate supplies positive rung.  To
prove \texttt{currentThree}, apply \noderef{ExtensionRules}.  Any lower ETT
requested in that theorem has positive rung \(t<n\), so all six of its
required assertions are supplied by \(A_{<n}\); the theorem returns the
entire conjunction constituting assertion (iii), including stability before
the supporting vertex, conditional predecessor stability, and disjointness
of the two raw stored sets.

Apply \noderef{ExtensionBase} in the same way, now retaining the arbitrary
certified-input quantifier in \(A_{<n}\).  Its conclusion is exactly
\texttt{currentFour}: at a terminal PE slot, the component of the recorded
gamma and delta colors through the chosen vertex meets the terminal ladder
tree precisely at that vertex.  Applying \noderef{Basic2} with the same
strict-lower package gives \texttt{currentFive}, the required order bound
for every nonminimum defective boundary-color vertex relative to each PE or
SE chosen vertex.  Finally, applying \noderef{Td} with that package gives
\texttt{currentSix}: every proper, exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored terminal stable
coloring is a \noderef{ModColoring} of the current ETT.

These six lower fields and four current fields form a
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} at \(n\).  This completes the strong
induction and proves the result for every natural number \(n\).
\end{proof}
