\begin{lemma}[No minimum bad pair]
For a fixed finite loopless multigraph \(G\), integer \(k\), and exact
uncolored edge \(e\), no minimum bad pair \((\varphi,T)\) exists.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Assume that \((\varphi,T)\) is a minimum bad pair.  By
\noderef{TashTreeMinimumBadPathIndex}, its path index \(q=\rho(T)\) is
greater than two.  The definition \noderef{TashTreePathIndex} always gives
\(q\le |E(T)|\).

If \(q=|E(T)|\), \noderef{TashTreeCasePathIndexLast} gives a
contradiction.  Otherwise \(q<|E(T)|\).  In that situation, either some
color missing at the final vertex is also missing at a vertex \(y_i\) with
\(q\le i<|E(T)|\), or there is no such color and vertex.  The first
alternative contradicts \noderef{TashTreeCaseRepeatedOnPath}; its logical
negation contradicts \noderef{TashTreeCaseNoRepeatedOnPath}.  These are
exhaustive alternatives, so the assumed minimum bad pair cannot exist.
\end{proof}
