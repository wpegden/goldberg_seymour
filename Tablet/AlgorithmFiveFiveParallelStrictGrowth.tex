\begin{helper}[Strict PE growth in the restricted Algorithm 5.5 chain]
Let $J$ be the displayed tree of a positive-rung PE ETT with generating
coloring $\varphi_n$.  Suppose that a zero-divider
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} $K_J$ has base $B_J$, that
$J=B_J.\mathit{core}$, and that $K_J$ has a
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} witness $\mathcal G_J$.  Let a restricted
Put
\[
 A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(K_J,0),\qquad
 D=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(K_J,0).
\]
For an ordered extension $W$ and $\eta\in D$, let $C_W(\eta)$ be the set
of colors on displayed edges whose positions are at least $|A|$ and are
strictly below both $|W|$ and the totalized
\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition} of $\eta$ in $W$.  Let a restricted
augmentation chain have endpoint $U$ and length $m$, with
\[
 |U|=|J|+m,
 \qquad m=0\Longrightarrow U=J.
\]
Assume its endpoint is maximal in the following precise sense: no
one-step $\varphi_n$-\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation} $W$ of $U$ satisfies
\[
 \mathcal G_J.\Gamma^0_\eta\cap C_W(\eta)=\varnothing
 \quad\hbox{for every }\eta\in D.
\]
Then $|J|<|U|$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Write
$D=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(K_J,0)$ and
$R=\bigcup_{\eta\in D}\mathcal G_J.\Gamma^0_\eta$.
The PE clause of \noderef{GoodHierarchy} says that
$J=B_J.\mathit{core}$ is not $R^-$-closed.  Expanding
\noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}, choose a color
$c\in\noderef{TreeMissingColors}(\varphi_n,J)-R$ and an exact edge
$f\in\noderef{BoundaryColorEdges}(G,\varphi_n,V(J),c)$.
Unfolding \noderef{BoundaryColorEdges} and \noderef{BoundaryEdges} gives
vertices $x\in V(J)$ and $y\notin V(J)$ with
$G.\mathit{ends}(f)=\{x,y\}$.

Construct an ordered tree $W=J+f$ using the data of
\noderef{TreeSequence}: copy every old vertex and edge entry, append $y$
as the last vertex, and append the individual identity $f$ as the last
edge.  The old entries retain their injectivity.  The new vertex is fresh
because $y\notin V(J)$.  The new edge is also fresh: every old displayed
edge has both endpoints among the old vertices by the attachment field,
whereas $f$ has the endpoint $y$ outside that set.  The old attachment
equations copy, and the boundary endpoint equation supplies the final
attachment to the occurrence of $x$ in $J$.  Thus $W$ is an actual exact
ordered tree-sequence, $J$ is its exact initial segment, and
$|W|=|J|+1$.  Since $\varphi_n(f)=c$ and $c$ is missing on $J$, these are
exactly the fields of a
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation} from $J$ to $W$.

Because the divider count is zero,
\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}$(K_J,0)=B_J.\mathit{core}=J$.  Hence the
only possible displayed edge in $C_W(\eta)$ is the newly appended edge,
and its color is $c$.  (If $\eta$ first becomes missing at the appended
vertex, the strict first-missing cutoff excludes even that edge.)  The
choice $c\notin R$ says that $c$ belongs to no Gamma pair.  Therefore $W$
satisfies all of the fixed restricted-augmentation avoidance conditions.

Suppose $m=0$.  The endpoint hypothesis gives $U=J$, so the preceding
augmentation becomes an admissible one-step augmentation of $U$.  This
contradicts endpoint maximality.  Thus $m>0$, and the length formula gives
$|U|=|J|+m>|J|$, as required.
\end{proof}
