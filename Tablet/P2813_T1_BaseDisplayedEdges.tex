\begin{helper}[Base stability fixes every displayed core edge]
Let $\mathcal E$ be an ETT generated by $\varphi$ on $T$ in a finite
loopless multigraph, with distinguished edge $e_0$, and let $B$ be a
\noderef{HierarchyBase} for it under $\varphi$.  Write $T_n,D_n$ for
the terminal ladder tree and computed stored set.  Suppose both
$\varphi$ and $\sigma$ leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored.  Suppose $\sigma$
is $(T_n,D_n,\varphi)$-stable in non-PE and is
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring} on $(T_n,B.\mathit{closure},D_n,\varphi)$
in PE.  Then every exact displayed edge of $B.\mathit{core}$ has the
same value under $\sigma$ and $\varphi$, including the distinguished
first edge.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Extract $(T_n,D_n,\varphi)$-stability in either branch by
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring} and \noderef{StableColoring}.
The first displayed edge is $e_0$ by \noderef{TreeSequence}, and both
values are uncolored by \noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored}.
Every other displayed edge of $T_n$ has a nonempty old color, since
injectivity separates its identity from $e_0$.  At the valid terminal
predecessor index $n-1$, the tree-used-color conclusion of
\noderef{HkuTreeColorsControlled} puts its color in
$\overline\varphi(T_n)\cup D_n$.  The attachment law of
\noderef{TreeSequence} supplies an endpoint in $T_n$, so the incident-edge
clause of \noderef{StableColoring} fixes its value.  Thus all displayed
edges of $T_n$ are fixed.

In non-PE, \noderef{HierarchyBase} identifies the core with $T_n$,
which finishes that branch.  In PE, write $R=B.\mathit{closure}$ and
$P$ for the exact positive support prefix of $T_n$.
The parallel clause of \noderef{HierarchyBase} supplies a
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} from $P$ to $R$ and an
\noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} of $T_n,R$ with output the core.
The closure's augmentation sequence makes $P$ an exact prefix of $R$ by
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment}.
For an edge of $R$ whose index is below $|P|$, its identity is the
corresponding identity in $P$, hence in $T_n$, and is already fixed.

For an edge at index $t\ge |P|$, apply
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequencePosition} to the closure route.
Its exact step gives a color $c$ for this edge and a missing witness
on $R|_t$.  Exact-prefix vertex inclusion from
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset} puts that witness
in $R$, so $c\in\overline\varphi(R)$ by \noderef{TreeMissingColors}.
The attachment law gives an endpoint in $R$.  The $R$ conjunct of
combined stability has empty additional control set; its incident-edge
clause therefore fixes this exact edge.  Every displayed edge of $R$
is fixed.

Finally the union's left-prefix clause identifies all core edges at
indices below $|T_n|$ with displayed edges of $T_n$.  Its
new-entry-from-right clause identifies every remaining core edge with
an exact displayed edge of $R$.  Both kinds were proved fixed, so every
core edge is fixed.  No assertion that the swapping component avoids
the entire core is needed.
\end{proof}
