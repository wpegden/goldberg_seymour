\begin{definition}
For a node $z\in V\sqcup E$, the $(\alpha,\beta)$-component through $z$ is
the support of the connected component of $z$ in the two-color incidence
graph $I_G(\varphi;\alpha,\beta)$.  It contains both vertex-nodes and the
identities of the $\alpha$- or $\beta$-colored edges which connect them.
When $z$ is a vertex-node, this is the incidence-graph realization of the
paper's unique $(\alpha,\beta)$-chain through that vertex.
\end{definition}
