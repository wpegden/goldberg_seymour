\begin{lemma}[Elementariness before the terminal certified step]
Let $P$ be a Step 1 exit-leg presentation and let $X$ be its terminal
ladder tree.  Assume that every positive-rung maximum ETT of rung number
strictly smaller than that of $P$ has elementary terminal vertex set
under its generating coloring.  Then $V(X)$ is elementary under the
coloring immediately preceding the terminal certified step of $P$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $n$ be the positive rung number of the ETT in $P$.  If $n=1$, the
pre-terminal coloring is the initial series coloring and $X$ is the
first ladder tree.  The first conclusion of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase}, applied to the certified series of $P$,
states exactly that this vertex set is elementary under that coloring.

Suppose $n>1$.  Apply \noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor} to the maximum
ETT in $P$.  It supplies a maximum ETT of rung number $n-1$, with exact
terminal tree $X$ and generating coloring equal to the coloring before
the terminal certified step.  Its rung number is positive and strictly
less than $n$.  The assumed lower-rung elementary assertion applied to
this predecessor therefore gives precisely the desired elementariness
of $V(X)$.
\end{proof}
