\begin{lemma}[Elementariness before the terminal certified step]
Let $P$ be a Step 1 exit-leg presentation and let $X$ be its terminal
ladder tree.  Assume that every positive-rung maximum ETT of rung number
strictly smaller than that of $P$ has elementary terminal vertex set
under its generating coloring.  Then $V(X)$ is elementary under the
coloring immediately preceding the terminal certified step of $P$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{Step1ExitLegPresentation}, $P$ contains an actual maximum
ETT $\mathcal E=P.\mathit{ett}$ and its exact terminal ladder tree is the
last ladder-tree entry of the certified series of $\mathcal E$.  Write
$n=\mathcal E.\mathit{rounds}$.  The fields of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} give $1\leq n$, the complete certified
series, and the identification of the displayed generating coloring
with its last series coloring.

If $n=1$, the terminal ladder tree is the series entry $T_1$, while the
coloring immediately preceding the terminal certified step is the
series entry $\varphi_0$.  The first conclusion of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase}, applied to the certified series just
extracted from $\mathcal E$, says exactly that $V(T_1)$ is elementary
under $\varphi_0$.  This is the required conclusion in this case.

Now suppose $n>1$.  The maximum-property field supplied by
\noderef{Step1ExitLegPresentation} allows us to apply
\noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor} to $\mathcal E$.  It supplies an
actual maximum ETT $\mathcal E^-$ of rung number $n-1$, whose exact
terminal tree is the old terminal ladder tree $X$ and whose generating
coloring is exactly the old series coloring at index $n-1$, namely the
coloring immediately preceding the terminal certified step.  Since
$n>1$, one has $0<n-1<n$.  The assumed lower-rung elementary assertion,
applied to $\mathcal E^-$ and its supplied maximum property, therefore
makes $V(X)$ elementary under that pre-terminal coloring, as claimed.
\end{proof}
