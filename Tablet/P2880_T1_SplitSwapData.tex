\begin{definition}[Exact split-swap certificates for Situation 7.3]
Fix the graph, palette, distinguished edge, input, exact displayed tree
\(T\), generating coloring, ETT, base, counterexample \(C\), minimum
context, and replacement presentation \(P\) of
\noderef{HierarchyReplacementPresentation}. Write \(\mu=P.\mathit{coloring}\),
\(H=P.\mathit{counterexample.hierarchy}\), \(q=H.\mathit{dividerCount}\),
\(j=\noderef{HierarchyPathNumber}(C.\mathit{hierarchy})\),
\(A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)\),
\(B=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,j-2)\),
\(X=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,j-1)\), and
\(Z=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(P.\mathit{counterexample},
\noderef{HierarchyFullTailLength}(P.\mathit{counterexample})-1)\).
For colors \(\alpha,\beta\) and a coloring \(\sigma\), a split-swap
certificate consists of the following data and laws. Here \(y_\ell\)
denotes the last displayed vertex of \(T\).
The colors are distinct, and \(\sigma\) is the
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} of the
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} rooted at \(y_\ell\); this component
contains no vertex of \(X\). The exact prefixes satisfy
\(B\preccurlyeq X\preccurlyeq T\), \(A\preccurlyeq X\) with
\(|A|<|X|\), \(X\preccurlyeq Z\), and
\(P.\mathit{replacementBase.core}\preccurlyeq X\).
The old coloring makes \(Z\) elementary. The new coloring is proper
with exactly the distinguished edge uncolored, fixes every edge incident
with a vertex of \(X\), is
\noderef{TreeColoringInvariant} on \(X\), fixes every displayed edge
with index below \(|X|\), and fixes every displayed edge with index
\(|A|\le r<|A|+j\).
Both colors are missing on \(B\) and \(X\) under \(\sigma\), and
on \(Z\) under \(\mu\); \(\beta\) is missing at \(y_\ell\)
under \(\sigma\). All vertexwise missing sets on \(Z\) are unchanged.
For each \(\eta\in\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,q)\), its
\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition} on \(T\) is unchanged; if
either affected color belongs to its last Gamma pair, its old first
missing position is at most \(|X|\).
The switched component is disjoint from each component rooted at the
first \(\alpha\)-missing and first \(\beta\)-missing vertex of \(T\).
Finally there is an exact \(\sigma\)-augmentation route to \(X\)
from the terminal ladder tree in PE, or from the actual retained
singleton connecting-edge seed in SE/RE, with its
\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge} certificate. There are exact
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} suffixes \(X\to T\) under
both \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\).
\end{definition}
