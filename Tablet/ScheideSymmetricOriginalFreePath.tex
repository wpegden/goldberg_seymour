\begin{helper}[Original free-color full path]
Fix a defective color $\delta$.  Put
$X=V(T)$, $r=\gammaIndex(\delta)$,
$U_\gamma=\{u^1_{\gamma(r)},u^2_{\gamma(r)}\}$, and
$Z=X\setminus U_\gamma$.  There are a color $\beta$, vertices
$v\in Z$ and $z$, and a simple full $(\delta,\beta)$ incidence path
$P$ from $v$ to $z$ such that $\beta$ is missing at $v$, is free for
$(T,e_0,\varphi)$, differs from $\gamma(r)$, and $P$ has precisely the
support of the two-color component through $v$ and ends outside $X$.
All vertices and edges here are the exact identities of the supplied
reduced configuration and replacement data.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
The globally maximum field of \noderef{ScheideReducedConfiguration},
together with \noderef{ScheideGlobalMaximumOrder}, says that $X$ is
elementary and closed.  The ordered layout in
\noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration} has $|X|=p+2s$ and hence
$|Z|=p+2s-2$.  It also places both ends of $e_0$ among the base vertices,
which all lie in $Z$: the two deleted vertices occur later in one of the
two-vertex blocks, and vertex injectivity is part of
\noderef{TreeSequence}.

Apply \noderef{ScheideGlobalMaximumMissingCounts} at every vertex of
$Z$.  Every such vertex misses at least one color, while each of the two
ends of $e_0$ misses at least two.  Elementariness makes missing-color
sets at different vertices disjoint.  Consequently at least
$(p+2s-2)+2=p+2s$ distinct colors are missing on $Z$.

There are at most $p-2+s$ colors in
$\noderef{TreeUsedColors}(\varphi,T)$.  Indeed, after the uncolored first
edge, the base contributes at most $p-2$ colors, and the two displayed
edges of each of the $s$ blocks have the single common block color given
by \noderef{ScheideStructuralConfiguration}.  Every used color has a
missing witness in the base: for a nonfirst base edge this is the earlier
vertex in the defining condition of \noderef{TashkinovTree}, and for a
block color it is the explicit base witness in the structural
configuration.  Thus all used colors occur among the colors just counted
on $Z$.

The index $r$ exists, so $s\ge1$, and therefore
$p-2+s<p+2s$.  Choose a color $\beta$ missing at a vertex $v\in Z$ but
not used on the displayed colored edges.  By the definition
\noderef{TreeFreeColors}, $\beta$ is free.  It differs from $\gamma(r)$,
because the latter colors the two displayed edges of the $r$-th block and
is therefore used.  Finally apply
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveChainPathExists} to the globally maximum triple,
the defective color $\delta$, this free color $\beta$, and its missing
vertex $v$.  It supplies an outside endpoint $z$ and the required walk;
its conclusion is exactly the full simple-path predicate
\noderef{ScheideDefectiveChainPath} asserted here.
\end{proof}
