\begin{lemma}
Every finite multigraph $G$ with
$\chi'(G)\ge\Delta(G)+2$ is elementary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose from \noderef{CriticalCoreExists} an edge restriction $H=G|F$ that
is critical and satisfies $\chi'(H)=\chi'(G)$.  By
\noderef{MaximumDegreeRestrict}, $\Delta(H)\le\Delta(G)$.  Therefore
\[
 \chi'(H)=\chi'(G)\ge\Delta(G)+2\ge\Delta(H)+2.
\]
The critical theorem \noderef{ThmGS2} applies to $H$ and shows that $H$ is
elementary.  Finally \noderef{ElementaryTransfer}, using the equality of
chromatic indices supplied by the critical core, transfers elementariness
from $H$ to $G$.
\end{proof}
