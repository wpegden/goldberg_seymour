\begin{definition}
A finite Tashkinov series of (r) noninitial iterations constructed from a
(k)-triple ((G,e_0,\varphi)) consists of indexed Tashkinov-series data
together with an Algorithm 3.1 step certificate at every index
\(0\leq i\leq r\).  Index (0) is therefore the certified initial tuple,
and every positive index is certified by exactly one of the witness-carrying
SE, PE, or RE constructors.  The object stores all trees, colorings, exact
finite (S_i) and (F_i), extension kinds, and nondeterministic witnesses;
it is a relation certificate and not the output of a choice function.
\end{definition}
