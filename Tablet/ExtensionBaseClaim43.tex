\begin{lemma}[Claim 4.3: output of Algorithm (A)]
Use the extension-base setting and let
\(\mathcal R\) be a restoration trace satisfying
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant}.  Write
\(\sigma_n\) for its initial coloring and \(\varrho_1\) for its output.
Let \(q+1=n\) be a certified PE slot with exact chosen support \(v_n\),
let \(j+1=m(v_n)\) with \(j\leq q\), assume \(v_n\in V(T_j)\), and suppose that
\(\sigma_n\) is proper, leaves precisely \(e_0\) uncolored, and is stable
on the terminal ladder tree.  Assume also that it is
\[
 (T(v_n)-v_n,C,\varphi_{j-1})\text{-stable}.                  \tag{A}
\]
For missing colors, assume explicitly that every unsupported vertex
\(v\in V(T_j)\) has
\(\overline{\sigma}_n(v)=\overline{\varphi}_{j-1}(v)\), and that every
nonterminal support class \(i_0<\cdots<i_p\) has its exact gamma/delta
fields, satisfies
\[
 \gamma_{i_{a+1}}=\delta_{i_a},
 \qquad
 \gamma_{i_0},\delta_{i_0},\ldots,\delta_{i_p}
 \text{ are pairwise distinct},
\]
and has endpoint identity
\[
 \overline{\varphi}_{j-1}(w)
  =(\overline{\sigma}_n(w)\setminus\{\delta_{i_p}\})
      \cup\{\gamma_{i_0}\}.                                  \tag{B}
\]
Here (A) is the instance of \noderef{ExtensionRules} used by the paper,
while the unsupported equality and (B) are supplied by
\noderef{ExtensionBaseClaimMissingColors}, and the chain data by
\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClassColors}.

Then \(\varrho_1\) has all four properties of Claim 4.3:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(b1)] it is
 \((T(v_n)-v_n,C,\varphi_{j-1})\)-stable;
\item[(b2)] its missing colors agree with
 \(\varphi_{j-1}\) on \(V(T_j)\setminus\{v_n\}\), its missing colors at
 \(v_n\) agree with \(\sigma_n\), and on every exact displayed edge
 identity \(f\) of \(T_j\),
 \[
                  \varrho_1(f)=\sigma_n(f)=\varphi_{j-1}(f);
 \]
\item[(b3)] if \(\varrho_1(f)\ne\sigma_n(f)\), then \(f\) is not
 internal to \(T_j\), and both colors of \(f\) belong to
 \(\bigcup_{t\in L^*}S_t\); and
\item[(b4)] every pair belonging to
 \(L(v_n)\setminus\{q\}\) remains strongly interchangeable at its tree.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{ExtensionBaseClaim43Stability} to the trace invariant and
(A).  It gives (b1).

Apply \noderef{ExtensionBaseClaim43MissingColors} to the unsupported
vertex equality, the exact class enumerations, their chain and
injectivity, and (B).  It gives the two missing-color assertions in
(b2).  Apply \noderef{ExtensionBaseClaim43TreeEdges} to the stated
terminal stability of \(\sigma_n\).  It supplies the exact two
equalities on every displayed edge identity, completing (b2).

The trace invariant alone satisfies the hypotheses of
\noderef{ExtensionBaseClaim43EdgeEffects}; its conclusion is exactly
(b3), including explicit nonempty initial and final colors and retention
of each edge identity.  Finally apply
\noderef{ExtensionBaseClaim43StrongInterchangeable}.  Since the trace
enumerates \(L^*=L\setminus L(v_n)\), its final preservation clause
applies to every nonterminal slot in \(L(v_n)\), giving (b4).
These four cited nodes have distinct conclusions and together establish
all clauses of Claim 4.3.
\end{proof}
