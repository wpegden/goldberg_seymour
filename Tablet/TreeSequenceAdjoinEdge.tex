\begin{definition}
Let (T) be an ordered tree-sequence rooted at (e_0), and let (f) be an
edge identity.  The relation
“(T^+) is (T+f)”, denoted here by
\(operatorname{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}(T,f,T^+)\), means that (T^+) has
exactly one more edge than (T), that (T) is an ordered initial segment of
(T^+), and that the new final edge identity is exactly (f).  The
tree-sequence axioms of (T^+) in particular record the new vertex and the
attachment of (f); parallel edge identities are not identified.
\end{definition}
