\begin{definition}
A \emph{Scheide structural configuration} consists of a globally maximum
triple $(T,e_0,\varphi)$ together with integers $p\geq2$ and $s$, distinct
colors $\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_s$, base vertices
$y_0,\ldots,y_{p-1}$, base edge identities
$e_1,\ldots,e_{p-1}$, and, for every $i$, two further vertex identities
$u^1_{\gamma_i},u^2_{\gamma_i}$ and two further edge identities
$f^1_{\gamma_i},f^2_{\gamma_i}$.  These data occupy the indicated positions
of the ordered tree-sequence exactly:
\[
 T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_{p-1},y_{p-1},
 f^1_{\gamma_1},u^1_{\gamma_1},f^2_{\gamma_1},u^2_{\gamma_1},\ldots,
 f^1_{\gamma_s},u^1_{\gamma_s},f^2_{\gamma_s},u^2_{\gamma_s}).
\]
Writing $Y=\{y_0,\ldots,y_{p-1}\}$ and
$\Gamma=\{\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_s\}$, the data satisfy:
each $f^j_\gamma$ has precisely the endpoints $u^j_\gamma$ and a vertex
of $Y$; both named edges have color $\gamma$ and $\gamma$ is missing at a
vertex of $Y$; the colored base edges other than the distinguished first
edge have colors outside $\Gamma$; and every Scheide exit vertex misses a
color in $\Gamma$.  Edge and vertex identities, including parallel edge
identities, are retained by the ordered data.  Finally every graph vertex
is incident with an edge, which records the source's critical-graph
convention alongside the tablet's edge-critical convention.
\end{definition}
