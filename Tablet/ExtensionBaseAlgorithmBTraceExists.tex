\begin{helper}[Decreasing trace for Algorithm (B)]
Let \(1_1<\cdots<1_{p+1}\) be the strictly increasing list of PE slots
with the prescribed common support and prescribed gamma and delta fields, and
fix \(b\in\operatorname{Fin}(p+1)\).  There is an
\noderef{ExtensionBaseAlgorithmBTrace} with exactly \(b\) steps whose step
\(a\) has position \(b-1-a\), slot \(1_{b-a}\), and the gamma and delta
colors prescribed at that position.  In particular, in zero-based notation,
the trace satisfies
\[
 \mathtt{position}(a)+a+1=b,
 \qquad
 \mathtt{gamma}(a)=\gamma_{\mathtt{position}(a)},
 \qquad
 \mathtt{delta}(a)=\delta_{\mathtt{position}(a)}.
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Set the number of steps to \(b\), and for \(a\in\operatorname{Fin}(b)\)
set \(r_a=b-1-a\).  Since \(a<b\), one has \(r_a<b<p+1\), so \(r_a\)
is a well-defined member of \(\operatorname{Fin}(p+1)\).  Put
\[
 \mathtt{position}(a)=r_a,\quad
 \mathtt{slot}(a)=\mathtt{indices}(r_a),\quad
 \mathtt{gamma}(a)=\gamma_{r_a},\quad
 \mathtt{delta}(a)=\delta_{r_a}.
\]
Starting with \(\varrho_1\), define coloring \(a+1\) from coloring \(a\)
by the rooted component swap required in the transition field of
\noderef{ExtensionBaseAlgorithmBTrace}.  Finite recursion supplies these
\(b+1\) colorings, including the sole initial coloring when \(b=0\).

The displayed definition gives \(r_a+a+1=b\).  Its values are precisely all
positions \(r<b\): for such an \(r\), take \(a=b-1-r\).  Therefore the image
of the slot map is exactly the image under \(\mathtt{indices}\) of the
positions below \(b\), which is
\noderef{ExtensionBaseCaseOneIndices}.  If \(a<c\), then
\(r_c<r_a\); strict increase of the supplied index list gives
\(\mathtt{slot}(c)<\mathtt{slot}(a)\).  Finally, the prescribed support,
PE-kind, gamma-field, and delta-field equalities at \(r_a\), together with
the recursive coloring equation, give all remaining fields of
\noderef{ExtensionBaseAlgorithmBTrace}.  The three asserted identities are
immediate from the definitions.
\end{proof}
