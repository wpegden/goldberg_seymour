\begin{lemma}
If $F\subseteq E(G)$, then
$\chi'(G|F)\le\chi'(G)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{ChromaticIndexSpec}, choose a full proper
$\chi'(G)$-edge-coloring $\varphi$ of $G$.  Its restriction to the retained
edge identities is proper by \noderef{ProperColoringRestricts}; it is full
because every retained edge was colored by $\varphi$.  Hence $G|F$ has a
$\chi'(G)$-edge-coloring.  The minimality clause of
\noderef{ChromaticIndexSpec}, applied to $G|F$, gives
$\chi'(G|F)\le\chi'(G)$.
\end{proof}
