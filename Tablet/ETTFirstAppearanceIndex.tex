\begin{definition}
Let \(T\) be an extended Tashkinov tree with ordered ladder-stage family
\(T_1,T_2,\ldots,T_n,T\).  For a vertex \(v\in V(T)\), its
first-appearance index \(m(v)\) is the least manuscript subscript
\(i\in\{1,\ldots,n+1\}\) for which \(v\) belongs to the corresponding
complete ordered stage, where subscripts \(1,\ldots,n\) mean
\(T_1,\ldots,T_n\) and subscript \(n+1\) means the displayed final tree
\(T\).  The final tree is included in the finite minimum, so every
\(v\in V(T)\) has a candidate even when it belongs to none of
\(T_1,\ldots,T_n\).
\end{definition}
