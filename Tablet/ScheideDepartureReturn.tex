\begin{definition}
Let $P$ be a full defective-chain path directed from its tree end $u$.
Indices $i_1<i_2$ and vertices $v_1,v_2$ form its
\emph{first-departure/last-return data} when the vertex-node at $i_1$ is
$v_1\notin V(T)$ and every earlier vertex-node lies in $V(T)$, while the
vertex-node at $i_2$ is $v_2\in V(T)$ and every later vertex-node lies
outside $V(T)$.  Thus $v_1$ is the first outside multigraph vertex and
$v_2$ is the last later tree vertex in path order.  Indices count steps in
the incidence path; successive multigraph vertices are two incidence
steps apart.
\end{definition}
