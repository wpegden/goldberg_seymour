\begin{definition}
Let $P$ be an $(\alpha,\gamma)$-incidence walk from the vertex-node $u$ to
the vertex-node $z$.  Indices $i_1,i_2$ and vertices $v_1,v_2$ satisfy the
\emph{first-departure/last-return predicate} for
$(T,\varphi,\alpha,\gamma,u,z,P)$ when $P$ is a full defective-chain path
for these data, the vertex-node at $i_1$ is $v_1\notin V(T)$ and every
earlier vertex-node lies in $V(T)$, $i_1<i_2$, the vertex-node at $i_2$ is
$v_2\in V(T)$, and every later vertex-node lies outside $V(T)$.  Thus
$v_1$ is the first outside multigraph vertex and $v_2$ is the last tree
vertex after that first departure.  Indices count steps in the incidence
path; successive multigraph vertices are two incidence steps apart.
\end{definition}
