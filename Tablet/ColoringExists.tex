\begin{lemma}
Every finite loopless multigraph has a proper $k$-edge-coloring for some
natural number $k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $E$ be the finite edge set and take $k=|E|$.  Choose a bijection from
$E$ to $[k]$ and give each edge its image under this bijection.  Every edge
is colored, so the coloring is full in the sense of
\noderef{IsFullEdgeColoring}.  If two distinct adjacent edges had the same
color, injectivity of the chosen bijection would make the edge identities
equal, a contradiction.  Thus the coloring is proper by
\noderef{IsProperEdgeColoring}, and it witnesses the assertion of
\noderef{HasKEdgeColoring}.
\end{proof}
