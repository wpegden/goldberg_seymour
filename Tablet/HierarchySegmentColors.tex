\begin{definition}
Let $v_\eta$ be the first vertex of the terminal tree $T$ missing
$\eta$, with the last vertex used when no such vertex exists.  For
$0\leq j\leq q$, the \emph{$(j,\eta)$ segment-color set} consists of
the colors used on the displayed edges of
\[
 T_{n,j+1}(v_\eta)-T_{n,j}^{*}.
\]
If $v_\eta$ lies outside $T_{n,j+1}$, the prefix
$T_{n,j+1}(v_\eta)$ is, by convention, all of $T_{n,j+1}$.  The
definition uses the exact ordered edge entries between the starred-stage
length and the smaller of the first-missing position and the next-stage
length.
\end{definition}
