\begin{lemma}[Part III, Algorithm (A) and invariant (6)]
Let \(\mathcal E\) be an ETT with final displayed tree \(T\), terminal PE
index \(q\), terminal supporting vertex \(v_n\), first-appearance slot
\(j\), and a proper coloring \(\sigma_n\) leaving exactly \(e_0\)
uncolored.  Put
\(L=\noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}\) and
\(L^*=\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices}=L\setminus L(v_n)\),
where \(L(w)=\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}\).  Here terminality
means \(q+1=n\), the certified slot \(q.\mathsf{succ}\) has kind PE and
exact chosen vertex \(v_n\), and \(j+1=m(v_n)\).  Assume the following
explicit conclusions of the preparations preceding Algorithm (A).
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every \(t\in L^*\) has exact certified data
      \((v_t,\gamma_t,\delta_t)\), one has \(v_n\prec v_t\), and \(T_j\)
      is an ordered initial segment of \(T_t\).  These are the order
      consequences proved by \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportOrder}.
\item Every active nonterminal pair, that is, every \(t\in L\setminus
      \{q\}\), is \(T_t\)-strongly interchangeable under \(\sigma_n\),
      as in \noderef{ExtensionBaseClaimStrongInterchangeable}.
\item For every support class \(L(w)\subseteq L^*\), let \(t\) be its
      greatest member and let \(\delta_t\) be its exact delta field.  If
      \(\mathcal S_w=\bigcup_{s\in L(w)}S_s\) is the union of all exact
      stored pairs in that full support class, then
      \[
          \overline\sigma_n(w)\cap\mathcal S_w=\{\delta_t\}.    \tag{A4}
      \]
      This is the full first equality in Claim 4.1(a4), supplied by
      \noderef{ExtensionBaseClaimMissingColors}.
\item For all active indices \(s<t\) in \(L\), with exact pairs
      \(S_s=\{\gamma_s,\delta_s\}\) and
      \(S_t=\{\gamma_t,\delta_t\}\), these pairs intersect if and only
      if their supports agree and no member of the full support class
      \(L(v_s)\) lies strictly between \(s\) and \(t\).  In that case
      \[
                S_s\cap S_t=\{\gamma_t\}=\{\delta_s\}.          \tag{O}
      \]
      This is the exact active-set overlap law of
      \noderef{ExtensionBaseStoredPairOverlap}.
\end{enumerate}
Then there is an \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace} which enumerates
\(L^*\) in strictly decreasing order, starts at \(\sigma_n\), and
satisfies the \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant}.  In
particular, before every switch its chosen component is a path meeting
\(T_t\) exactly at \(v_t\), the color \(\delta_t\) is missing there, and
after the switch every still-unprocessed active nonterminal pair remains
strongly interchangeable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Because \(L^*\) is finite, enumerate its exact slot identities once in
strictly decreasing order,
\[
                         t_0>t_1>\cdots>t_{r-1}.               \tag{1}
\]
For each \(t_a\), assumption 1 supplies exact values
\(v_{t_a},\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a}\).  Define \(\sigma^0=\sigma_n\)
and recursively define
\[
 \sigma^{a+1}=\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}
   (\sigma^a,\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},v_{t_a}).                \tag{2}
\]
The exact range in (1), strict decrease, the certified field equalities,
and (2) give all fields of an
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace}.  We prove the remaining clauses
of \noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant} by induction on \(a\).

We first record a certificate consequence needed at every step.  Membership
\(t_a\in L^*\), expanded using
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices} and
\noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}, says in particular that the positive
slot \(t_a+1\) has kind PE.  Inspect the certificate
\(\mathcal E.\mathsf{series}.\mathsf{certified}(t_a+1)\) supplied by
\noderef{TashkinovSeries}.  The initial constructor is impossible at a
successor slot, and the SE and RE constructors contradict the exact PE-kind
equation.  Hence its constructor in \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} is PE.
In that constructor the exact chosen-vertex field identifies \(v_{t_a}\)
with the vertex of the recorded
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}.  Its `vertexDefective' field and the
first conjunct in \noderef{DefectiveVertex} give
\[
             v_{t_a}\in V(T_{t_a}).                            \tag{3}
\]
Thus (3) is derived from restoration membership and the certified step;
it is not an additional membership assumption.

We next establish the missing and present colors used by the induction.
Fix a support \(w\) which occurs in \(L^*\).  Exact chosen-vertex
equalities show that \(w\ne v_n\); consequently every active slot with
support \(w\) lies outside \(L(v_n)\).  Hence its full class satisfies
\(L(w)\subseteq L^*\).  Enumerate this nonempty class increasingly as
\[
                         i_0<i_1<\cdots<i_p.                    \tag{4}
\]
The PE constructor just inspected identifies each stored field with the
exact pair \(S_{i_b}=\{\gamma_{i_b},\delta_{i_b}\}\).  Assumption 2,
expanded through \noderef{ETTStrongInterchangeable}, also gives
\(\gamma_{i_b}\ne\delta_{i_b}\), since every member of \(L^*\) is
different from \(q\).

Apply (A4) to the greatest member \(i_p\).  It says not merely that
\(\delta_{i_p}\) is missing, but that \(\delta_{i_p}\) is the only color
of the entire union \(\mathcal S_w\) missing at \(w\) under \(\sigma_n\).
In particular every \(\gamma_{i_b}\) is present initially.  Indeed it
belongs to \(S_{i_b}\subseteq\mathcal S_w\), and it cannot equal
\(\delta_{i_p}\): for \(b=p\) this is the displayed color inequality;
for \(b<p\), law (O) makes \(S_{i_b}\) disjoint from \(S_{i_p}\) when
they are nonconsecutive, while in the consecutive case its exact
intersection is \(\{\gamma_{i_p}\}=\{\delta_{i_{p-1}}\}\), which still
excludes \(\gamma_{i_{p-1}}\) by
\(\gamma_{i_{p-1}}\ne\delta_{i_{p-1}}\).  The same law for consecutive
members gives the full chain
\[
          \gamma_{i_{b+1}}=\delta_{i_b}\qquad(0\leq b<p).       \tag{5}
\]

We now follow the steps belonging to this class, ignoring intervening
steps from other classes.  Immediately before \(i_p\) is processed,
\(\delta_{i_p}\) is missing and \(\gamma_{i_p}\) is present by the
preceding paragraph.  Expanding \noderef{MissingColors} and
\noderef{PresentColors}, the former assertion gives no incident
\(\delta_{i_p}\)-edge at \(w\), while the latter gives an incident
\(\gamma_{i_p}\)-edge.  Properness, expanded through
\noderef{IsProperEdgeColoring}, gives at most one incident edge of either
fixed color.  The component in (2) contains that edge, and
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} exchanges its two colors.  Therefore
after the switch \(\gamma_{i_p}=\delta_{i_{p-1}}\) is missing at \(w\).
Law (O) says that this switched pair meets \(S_{i_{p-1}}\) only in
\(\delta_{i_{p-1}}\), so it does not change the incident edge witnessing
that \(\gamma_{i_{p-1}}\) is present.  The identical argument descends
through (4): immediately before processing \(i_b\), the color
\(\delta_{i_b}\) is missing and \(\gamma_{i_b}\) is present.  Pairs from
other support classes are disjoint by the active-set law (O), so
intervening switches in (1) change neither assertion.  This proves, for
every step \(a\),
\[
 \delta_{t_a}\in\overline{\sigma^a}(v_{t_a}),\qquad
 \gamma_{t_a}\notin\overline{\sigma^a}(v_{t_a}).               \tag{6}
\]

We prove the precise component assertion.  Before the step at \(t_a\),
the induction hypothesis supplies strong interchangeability under
\(\sigma^a\): at \(a=0\) this is assumption 2, and subsequently it is the
unprocessed-pair clause from the preceding step.  By (6), the root
vertex has no incident delta edge and has an incident gamma edge.  Thus
the definition \noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph} makes its degree in the
two-color component exactly one, and the component contains a genuine
edge.  Together with the spanning path from
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSpanningPath}, these facts give the
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} clause rooted at \(v_{t_a}\).

The root belongs to \(V(T_{t_a})\) by (3).  If another vertex
\(x\in V(T_{t_a})\setminus\{v_{t_a}\}\) belonged to the root component,
membership and the connected-component definition
\noderef{TwoColorComponent} identify the component through \(x\) with the
component through \(v_{t_a}\).  But
\noderef{ETTStrongInterchangeable} says that the former component avoids
the support \(v_{t_a}\), whereas the latter contains its root.  This is a
contradiction.  Consequently the component's vertex-node intersection
with \(V(T_{t_a})\) is exactly \(\{v_{t_a}\}\).  The terminal-order and
initial-segment clauses follow directly from assumption 1 and are
independent of the coloring.

The switch preserves properness by
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapProper}.  By
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}, it only transposes two
\(\mathsf{some}\)-values and fixes every \(\mathsf{none}\)-value, so the
same edge \(e_0\) remains the unique uncolored edge in the sense of
\noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored}.

It remains to verify preservation for every unprocessed active slot, not
only for slots in \(L^*\).  Let \(s\in L\setminus\{q\}\) have exact
support \(v_s\) and exact colors and suppose it has not occurred through
step \(a\).  If \(v_s\ne v_{t_a}\), then \(s\ne t_a\), so one of
\(s<t_a\) and \(t_a<s\) holds.  Apply the active-set overlap law (O) in
that orientation.  Its right side is false because the supports differ,
and hence \(S_s\cap S_{t_a}=\varnothing\).  This applies in particular
when \(s\in L(v_n)\setminus\{q\}\), even though such an \(s\) is not in
\(L^*\).

If \(v_s=v_{t_a}\), then \(v_{t_a}\ne v_n\), so active membership puts
\(s\) in the full class \(L(v_{t_a})\subseteq L^*\).  The trace therefore
processes \(s\), and since it has not yet done so, strict decrease in (1)
gives \(s<t_a\).  In the different-support case we have the required
pair disjointness; in the same-support case we have the required index
inequality.  All other premises, including strong interchangeability of
both pairs and the exact path component, are the induction hypothesis and
the preceding paragraphs.  Therefore
\noderef{StrongInterchangeabilityKempePreserved} proves that the pair at
\(s\) remains strongly interchangeable under \(\sigma^{a+1}\).  This is
exactly the preservation clause of invariant (6).

The induction now supplies at every step properness, the exact
one-uncolored property, (3), (6), the exact component intersection, the
order and initial-segment clauses, and preservation for every unprocessed
active nonterminal pair.  After the last step the same preservation
argument gives the final absent-slot clause.  If \(L^*=\varnothing\),
the trace has length zero and its final clause is assumption 2 directly.
Thus the constructed trace satisfies every clause of
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationInvariant}.
\end{proof}
