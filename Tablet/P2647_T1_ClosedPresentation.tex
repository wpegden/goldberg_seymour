\begin{lemma}[A mod-coloring closure gives an original-relative presentation]
Let $\mathcal E$ be a positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree with maximum
property, terminal ladder tree $T_n$, terminal kind $\Theta_n$, and exact
connecting-edge set $F_n$. Let $\sigma$ be a mod-coloring of
$\mathcal E$. Let $F$ be an exact ordered initial extension of $T_n$ of
strictly greater length. Assume either $\Theta_n=PE$ and $F$ is a
$\sigma$-closure of $T_n$, or $\Theta_n\in\{SE,RE\}$ and there are an
exact edge $f$ and an ordered seed $S$ with $F_n=\{f\}$,
$\noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}(T_n,f,S)$, and $F$ a $\sigma$-closure
of $S$. Then there is a Rutgers closed presentation relative to the
fixed original $\mathcal E$ whose coloring is exactly $\sigma$ and
whose displayed tree is exactly $F$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand each closure with \noderef{TashkinovClosure}. In the PE case,
its augmentation-sequence clause is a route from $T_n$ to $F$.
In the SE/RE case it is a route from the supplied exact seed $S$ to
$F$, retaining both $F_n=\{f\}$ and the exact adjunction of $f$.
Together with the assumed exact initial extension and strict length
inequality, these are exactly the eligibility conditions of the universal
clause in \noderef{ModColoring}. Apply that clause at the particular
ordered tree $F$. It supplies an actual
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate $R$ relative
to $\mathcal E$, for coloring $\sigma$ and displayed tree $F$.
Apply \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} to $\mathcal E$, its assumed
maximum property, the mod-coloring hypothesis, and this particular $R$.
The result is maximum property for $R$'s replacement ETT.

We verify the route using that same replacement ETT, not a separate
replacement certificate. By \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree},
its rung number is $n$ and its series is the recorded replacement series
after transporting this equality. For $q=n-1$, positivity in
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} gives $0\leq q<n$. The certificate's
ladder agreement at this $q$ identifies the replacement terminal ladder
tree with the original $T_n$. Its extension-kind agreement at index $n$
identifies its terminal kind with $\Theta_n$, and its connecting-set
agreement at the same index identifies its exact connecting-edge set
with $F_n$. In PE, rewrite the assumed closure using the ladder and kind
identities: it is the required closure from the replacement ladder to
$F$. In SE/RE, reuse the same edge identity $f$ and the same ordered
seed $S$. The connecting-set identity gives the required singleton set;
the ladder identity gives the identical adjunction $T_n+f=S$; the
closure from $S$ to $F$ is unchanged. Thus the supplied route rewrites
to the terminal route for the actual replacement ETT in $R$.

Define the presentation with coloring $\sigma$, displayed tree $F$,
corresponding certificate $R$, the transported maximum property, and
the terminal route just verified. These are all the fields of
\noderef{RutgersClosedPresentation}. Its coloring and displayed tree
have the asserted exact equalities by construction.
\end{proof}
