\begin{definition}
Let
\(\mathcal C=(t_a,\gamma_{t_a},\delta_{t_a},\mu^a)\) be an
\(\noderef{ExtensionBaseAlgorithmCTrace}\), and let \(T_j\) be the
reference stored tree.  The trace satisfies \emph{Algorithm (C)
invariant (24)} if every intermediate coloring is proper and leaves
exactly \(e_0\) uncolored, and the following assertions hold immediately
before each step at \(t=t_a\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(T_j\) is an ordered initial segment of \(T_t\), and
      \(\delta_t\in\overline\mu^a(u)\);
\item the \((\gamma_t,\delta_t)\)-component through \(u\) is a path;
\item among the vertex nodes of \(T_t\), that path contains at most
      \(v_n\), and, if it contains \(v_n\), then \(v_n\) is not an end
      of the path; explicitly the neighbor set of \(v_n\) in the
      two-color incidence graph is not subsingleton; and
\item after the switch, every slot
      \(s\in L_1\setminus\{n\}\) not yet processed, with its exact
      certified gamma and delta fields, remains \(T_s\)-strongly
      interchangeable under \(\mu^{a+1}\).
\end{enumerate}
At the output, the same strong-interchangeability assertion is required
for every member of \(L_1\setminus\{n\}\) absent from the entire trace.
Here \(L_1=\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}(v_n)\), the path component
is \(\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent}\), its full node set is
\(\noderef{TwoColorComponent}\), and strong interchangeability is
\(\noderef{ETTStrongInterchangeable}\).  The absent-slot output clause
also covers the zero-step trace.  This is exactly the witness-level form
of (24), including the manuscript's decreasing support-slot order and
the possible single intersection vertex \(v_n\).
\end{definition}
