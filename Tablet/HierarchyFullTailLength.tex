\begin{definition}
For a hierarchy counterexample \(C\) with terminal tree \(T\), let \(H\) be
its hierarchy and let
\(A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,\operatorname{Fin.last}
(H.\mathit{dividerCount}))\) be its last starred stage.  The
\emph{full-tail length} is
\[
  \ell(C)=|T|-|A|.
\]
It counts all exact displayed additions above \(A\); it is not the hierarchy
path number.
\end{definition}
