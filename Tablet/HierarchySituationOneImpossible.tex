\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext}.  Assume the paper's

\end{lemma}

\noderef{HierarchySituationOneShorteningContradiction} recolors the exact
last edge, constructs the shorter same-rung equal-divider hierarchy
counterexample, and contradicts lexicographic minimality.  Hence Situation
7.1 is impossible.
\end{proof}
