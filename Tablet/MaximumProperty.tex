\begin{definition}
Let \(T\) be an extended Tashkinov tree with rung number \(n\) and
certified series
\(\{(T_i,\varphi_{i-1},S_{i-1},F_{i-1},\Theta_{i-1})
:1\leq i\leq n+1\}\).  The tree \(T\) has the maximum property under its
coloring sequence when both of the following conditions hold.

First, the number of vertices of \(T_1\) equals the global Tashkinov order
of \(G\), namely the maximum order of any Tashkinov tree based at any
distinguished edge and any admissible initial coloring.

Second, for each \(i\) with \(1\leq i<n\), every proper coloring \(\pi\)
leaving exactly the distinguished edge uncolored and
\((T_i,D_i,\varphi_i)\)-stable satisfies the following bound.  If
\(\Theta_i=PE\), take the seed to be exactly \(T_i\).  If
\(\Theta_i\) is \(SE\) or \(RE\), take the unique displayed edge identity
\(f_i\), certified by the exact equality \(F_i=\{f_i\}\), and take the
seed to be the exact one-edge extension \(T_i+f_i\).  Every closure
\(T'_{i+1}\) of that prescribed seed under \(\pi\) has
\[
                       |V(T'_{i+1})|\leq |V(T_{i+1})|.
\]
There is deliberately no maximality requirement for \(T_{n+1}\) over
colorings stable on \(T_n\).
\end{definition}
