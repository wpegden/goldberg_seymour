\begin{helper}[Reconstructing the exact starred prefix from edge control]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph with palette $[k]$ and
distinguished edge $e_0$.  Fix an ETT $\mathcal E$ on $T$ generated
by $\varphi$, a \noderef{HierarchyBase} $B$, a
\noderef{HierarchyCounterexample} $C$, and a
\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext} for these data.
Let $H=C.\mathit{hierarchy}$, $q=H.\mathit{dividerCount}$,
$A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)$, and let $T_n,D_n$
be the terminal ladder tree and computed stored set.
Suppose $\sigma$ is proper and leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored.
Suppose it is $(T_n,D_n,\varphi)$-stable in non-PE, and is
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring} on $(T_n,B.\mathit{closure},D_n,\varphi)$
in PE.  Suppose every exact displayed edge of $A$ has its old value
and every vertex of $A$ has its old missing-color set.
Then \noderef{ModColoring}$(\mathcal E,\sigma)$ holds.
Moreover either $A=T_n$, or there exists a
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate relative to
$\mathcal E$ whose replacement tree is exactly $A$, whose generating
coloring is $\sigma$, and whose replacement ETT has maximum property.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Compose the nested exact stage inclusions in
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}.  The core is an exact prefix of $A$, and
$A$ is an exact prefix of $T$.  In PE the left-prefix field of
\noderef{OrderedTreeUnion}, supplied by \noderef{HierarchyBase}, puts
$T_n$ inside the core.  In non-PE the core is $T_n$.  Thus
$T_n\preccurlyeq A\preccurlyeq T$.

The assumed base relation has $(T_n,D_n,\varphi)$-stability as a conjunct
in PE and is that relation outright otherwise, by
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring}.  The generating-color equation of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} identifies $\varphi$ with the stored
terminal coloring.  With the context's lower package and the
counterexample's positive rung and maximum property,
\noderef{Td} therefore proves
\noderef{ModColoring}$(\mathcal E,\sigma)$.

If $A=T_n$, the stated alternative is immediate.  Otherwise the exact
prefix $T_n\preccurlyeq A$ has strictly smaller length: equal lengths
would identify every vertex and edge entry, and hence the two
\noderef{TreeSequence} records.  Obtain the original terminal route
from \noderef{P2813_T1_TerminalRoute}.
In PE restrict its augmentation sequence from $T_n$ to $A$ by
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict}.
In SE/RE its exact seed $S=T_n+f$ has length $|T_n|+1\le |A|$.
It and $A$ are exact prefixes of $T$, by
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment} and the stage
inclusions above, so their entry equations give $S\preccurlyeq A$.
Restrict the route from this actual seed to $A$.
The terminal singleton connecting-edge set and seed identity remain
those of the terminal-route helper.

Transport either restricted route from $\varphi$ to $\sigma$.
Induct on its finite chain using \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}.
For a final step $U\to W\preccurlyeq A$, all preceding trees are
prefixes of $A$, by
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceInitialSegment}.
The step's exact added edge is therefore a displayed edge of $A$ and
its value is fixed by assumption.  Its old missing witness on $U$
lies in $A$, by \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset},
and keeps its missing set.  The same color is thus missing at the same
vertex under $\sigma$.  Reuse the step's unchanged length equation and
exact-prefix equations: these are all the clauses of
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}.  The reflexive chain needs no step.
This induction gives the identical restricted sequence under $\sigma$.

We now have precisely the PE route from $T_n$, or the SE/RE route from
the actual singleton connecting seed, required in the reconstruction
clause of \noderef{ModColoring}.  Apply it to the strict exact prefix
$A$ to obtain its corresponding certificate.  Finally
\noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} transfers the original maximum
property to the certificate's own replacement ETT.
This claim produces a maximum prefix, not a retained good hierarchy;
it does not identify this certificate with any independently selected
PE splitter certificate.
\end{proof}
