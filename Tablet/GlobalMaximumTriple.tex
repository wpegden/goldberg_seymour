\begin{definition}
A triple $(T,e_0,\varphi)$ is globally maximum for $G$ when
$(G,e_0,\varphi)$ is a $k$-triple, $T$ is a corresponding Tashkinov tree,
and $|V(T)|=t(G)$.  Thus its order is maximum over every distinguished edge
identity, every corresponding coloring, and every corresponding Tashkinov
tree, rather than merely maximal for the fixed pair $(e_0,\varphi)$.
\end{definition}
