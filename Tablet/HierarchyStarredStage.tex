\begin{definition}
For a hierarchy with $q$ dividers, define its starred stages by
\[
 T_{n,0}^{*}=T_n\vee R_n\quad\text{when }\Theta_n=PE,
 \qquad T_{n,0}^{*}=T_n\quad\text{otherwise},
\]
and by $T_{n,j}^{*}=T_{n,j}$ for $1\leq j\leq q$.  The PE union is the
chosen ordered union stored in the hierarchy base.
\end{definition}
