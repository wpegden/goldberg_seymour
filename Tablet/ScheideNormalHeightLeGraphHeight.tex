\begin{lemma}[Every normal height is at most the graph height]
For every normal globally maximum triple on a finite loopless multigraph
$G$, its height is at most $h(G)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let the normal witness have tree $T$ and height $p$.  Its height field in
\noderef{NormalGlobalMaximumTriple} gives
$p\leq T.length+1$.  The vertex map of \noderef{TreeSequence} is an
injection from its $T.length+1$ indices into $V(G)$, so
$T.length+1\leq |V(G)|$.  Thus $p$ belongs to the finite range used in
\noderef{ScheideGraphHeight}.  The supplied normal globally maximum
witness places $p$ in the filtered set of attainable heights in that
definition.  Since $h(G)$ is the maximum of that set together with zero,
$p\leq h(G)$.
\end{proof}
