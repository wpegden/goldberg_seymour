\begin{lemma}
Every finite multigraph $G$ satisfies
$\Delta(G)\le\chi'(G)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $k=\chi'(G)$ and choose a full proper $k$-edge-coloring using
\noderef{ChromaticIndexSpec}.  Fix a vertex $v$.  Assign to each edge
incident with $v$ its color.  Any two distinct such edges are adjacent at
$v$, so properness makes their colors different.  This assignment is
therefore an injection from the $d_G(v)$ incident edge identities into the
$k$ colors, and $d_G(v)\le k$.  Taking the maximum over all vertices in the
definition \noderef{MaximumDegree} gives
$\Delta(G)\le k=\chi'(G)$; the empty-vertex convention gives the same
inequality when there is no vertex.
\end{proof}
