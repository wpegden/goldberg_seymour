\begin{definition}[Penultimate-prefix reconstruction data]
Let $C$ be a hierarchy counterexample on the exact tree $T$, let $B^+$
be a tree-sequence, and let $\sigma$ be a coloring. A
\texttt{P2752\_T2\_PrefixData} consists of the following data.
The exact prefix relation $B^+\preccurlyeq T$, the equation
$|T|=|B^+|+1$, and the exact inclusion $T_n\preccurlyeq B^+$ are recorded.
It contains an old ETT on $B^+$ with maximum property, the same rung and
certified series as the ETT of $C$, a base with the same ordered core,
closure, and numerical support position, and a good hierarchy on that
base. Its divider count, nonterminal ordinary and starred stages, and
numerical divider positions agree with those of $C$. At every internal
index, its computed stored set, stage-missing set, and every Gamma pair
agree with the corresponding old objects; its segment-color sets are
subsets of the original segment-color sets.
The data also contain properness and exact one-uncoloredness of $\sigma$,
invariance on $B^+$, and equality of the old and new values on every exact
edge incident with $B^+$. Finally they contain a corresponding replacement
ETT on $B^+$, its maximum property, a replacement base and good hierarchy,
and a SameGoodHierarchy relation between the two prefix hierarchies.
These are actual prefix certificates and ordered hierarchy objects, not
an assertion identifying any of them with a certificate on $T$.
\end{definition}
