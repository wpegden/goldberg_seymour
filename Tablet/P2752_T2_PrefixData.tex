\begin{definition}[Penultimate-prefix reconstruction data]
Let $C$ be a hierarchy counterexample on the exact tree $T$ with palette
$[k]$, let $B^+$ be a tree-sequence, and let $\sigma$ be a coloring. A
\texttt{P2752\_T2\_PrefixData} consists of the following data.
The exact prefix relation $B^+\preccurlyeq T$, the equation
$|T|=|B^+|+1$, and the exact inclusion $T_n\preccurlyeq B^+$ are recorded.
It contains an old ETT on $B^+$ with maximum property, the same rung and
certified series as the ETT of $C$, a base with the same ordered core,
closure, and numerical support position, and a good hierarchy on that
base. Its divider count, nonterminal ordinary and starred stages, and
numerical divider positions agree with those of $C$. Write $H^{\rm old}$
and $\mathit{oldGood}$ for the old prefix hierarchy and its good witness,
and $H=C.\mathit{hierarchy}$. For every internal index
$0\leq j\leq H^{\rm old}.\mathit{dividerCount}$, let $j_o$ be its
transport to $H$ under the equality of divider counts. The computed
stored sets and stage-missing sets agree:
\[
 D^{H^{\rm old}}_{n,j}=D^H_{n,j_o},\qquad
 \noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors}(H^{\rm old},j)
   =\noderef{HierarchyStageMissingColors}(H,j_o).
\]
Separately, for every such $j$ and every palette color $\eta\in[k]$,
including colors outside the common stored set, the total Gamma-function
values are copied and the segment-color containment holds:
\[
 \mathit{oldGood}.\mathit{gamma}(j,\eta)
   =C.\mathit{good}.\mathit{gamma}(j_o,\eta),\qquad
 \noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}(H^{\rm old},j,\eta)
   \subseteq\noderef{HierarchySegmentColors}(H,j_o,\eta).
\]
Off-domain Gamma-function values are copied bookkeeping data, not
additional paper-defined Gamma pairs. This copying requirement does not
alter \noderef{SameGoodHierarchy}, which compares Gamma pairs only on
the common stored set.
The data also contain properness and exact one-uncoloredness of $\sigma$,
invariance on $B^+$, and equality of the old and new values on every exact
edge incident with $B^+$. Finally they contain a corresponding replacement
ETT on $B^+$, its maximum property, a replacement base and good hierarchy,
and a SameGoodHierarchy relation between the two prefix hierarchies.
These are actual prefix certificates and ordered hierarchy objects, not
an assertion identifying any of them with a certificate on $T$.
\end{definition}
