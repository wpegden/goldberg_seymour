\begin{definition}
The \emph{trunk} of normal data of height $p$ is the initial tree-sequence
$T|y_{p-1}$: it has the first $p$ vertices and the first $p-1$ tree-edge
identities.  The definition uses $p-1$ as a tree-sequence edge count, in
contrast with the $2(p-1)$ steps of the stored incidence path.
\end{definition}
