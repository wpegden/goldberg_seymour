\begin{lemma}[Elementarity before the terminal extension]
Let \(\mathcal E:\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}(G,k,e_0,\rho,T,\varphi)\)
be a maximum ETT in a finite loopless multigraph, and suppose a
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext}\((G,k,e_0,n)\) is given, where
\(n=\mathcal E.\mathit{rounds}\). The terminal ladder tree
\(T_n=\mathcal E.\mathit{series.data.tree}(\operatorname{last}(n).\mathit{castSucc})\)
is elementary under the predecessor coloring
\(\mathcal E.\mathit{series.data.coloring}(n-1)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree}, \(n\geq1\). If \(n=1\), the first
conclusion of \noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase}, applied to the given certified
series, makes its tree at data index one elementary under its coloring at
index zero. These indices are exactly \(n\) and \(n-1\), so this is the
required assertion. No zero-rung ETT is needed.

If \(n>1\), \noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor}, applied to the given ETT and
its maximum property, provides a maximum ETT of rung \(n-1\), with displayed
tree exactly the original series tree at index \(n\) and generating coloring
exactly the original series coloring at index \(n-1\). Its rung is positive
and strictly below \(n\). Apply the \(\mathit{lowerOne}\) field of the given
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} to that actual predecessor and its
maximum witness. It asserts elementarity of that displayed tree under that
generating coloring, which are exactly the tree and coloring in the claim.
The two cases exhaust \(n\geq1\).
\end{proof}
