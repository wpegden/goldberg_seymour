\begin{definition}
Let $T$ be a tree-sequence for $(e_0,\varphi)$.  A vertex
$v\notin V(T)$ is \emph{absorbing} if, whenever
$\delta\in\overline\varphi(v)$ and
$\gamma\in\mathcal F(T,e_0,\varphi)\setminus\{\delta\}$, the full
$(\gamma,\delta)$-component containing $v$ contains a vertex
$u\in V(T)$ at which $\gamma$ is missing.  Here the component is the
component of the bipartite incidence graph, so its edge-nodes retain the
identities of parallel multigraph edges.  The witness also asserts that
$u$ is the unique vertex of $V(T)$ missing $\gamma$.
\end{definition}
