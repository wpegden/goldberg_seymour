\begin{lemma}[Extension-base Part I(5): exact stored-pair overlap]
Let \(G\) be a finite loopless multigraph, let \(e_0\) be its
distinguished edge, and fix \(k\) and an initial partial \(k\)-edge-coloring.
Let \(T\), with generating coloring, be an ETT whose certified series has
\(n>0\) noninitial rounds, and assume that this ETT has
\noderef{MaximumProperty}.  Choose the last noninitial Lean index \(q\),
so \(q+1=n\), and assume that the positive slot \(q.\mathsf{succ}\) is a
PE whose exact chosen vertex is \(v_n\in V(T)\).  Put \(j=m(v_n)\), choose
a Lean index \(jSlot\) satisfying \(jSlot+1=j\), and write \(T_j\) and
\(\varphi_{j-1}\) for the displayed tree and coloring at that index.
Define, locally,
\[
 L=\{s:j\leq s\leq n,\ \Theta_s=\mathrm{PE},\
                         m(v_s)\leq j\}.                       \tag{A}
\]
Membership includes the exact chosen-vertex equality at the positive PE
slot and membership of that vertex in the final displayed ETT.

Assume the following complete lower-rung package.  For every ETT \(T'\)
over the same \((G,e_0)\) and initial coloring, with generating coloring
\(\psi'\), positive rung number \(n'<n\), and
\noderef{MaximumProperty}, all six conclusions below hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The set \(V(T')\) is elementary under \(\psi'\).
\item If \(T'_{n'+1}\) is the final closed tree in its certified series,
      then, under every proper coloring \(\sigma\) leaving exactly
      \(e_0\) uncolored and stable on \(T'_{n'+1}\) relative to the
      terminal stored colors and displayed coloring, every pair
      \(\alpha,\beta\) with at least one member missing on
      \(T'_{n'+1}\) is
      \noderef{TreeInterchangeable} on \(T'_{n'+1}\).
\item If a positive slot \(b\) of the lower series is a PE with exact
      support \(v\), and \(m_{T'}(v)=h+1\), then every proper,
      exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored coloring stable at slot \(b\) is both
      stable before \(v\) in \(T'\) and stable on the ladder tree
      \(T'_h\), in both cases relative to \(D'_h\) and
      \(\varphi'_h\).  Moreover, two distinct PE
      slots with distinct supports of the same first-appearance index
      have disjoint exact stored pairs.
\item If the terminal slot of the lower series is a PE with exact support
      \(v\) and exact colors \(\gamma,\delta\), then, under every proper,
      exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored coloring stable on its terminal tree, the
      \((\gamma,\delta)\)-component containing \(v\) meets the vertex set
      of that tree exactly in \(\{v\}\).
\item Under every proper, exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored coloring stable on the
      lower terminal tree, if \(\delta\) is defective and a
      \(\delta\)-boundary vertex \(x\) has an earlier
      \(\delta\)-boundary vertex, then for every PE or SE positive slot
      \(b\) with exact chosen vertex \(v_b\) and
      \(m_{T'}(x)\leq b\), one has
      \(x=v_b\) or \(x\prec_{T'}v_b\).
\item Every proper, exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored coloring stable on the lower
      terminal tree, relative to its terminal stored colors and displayed
      coloring, is a \noderef{ModColoring} of that lower ETT.
\end{enumerate}

Finally assume the two ladder-stage instances used at the current ETT.
For every Lean index \(r<q\), the vertex set of \(T_{r+1}\) is elementary
under \(\varphi_r\).  For all noninitial Lean indices \(a<r\), every
proper, exactly-\(e_0\)-uncolored
\((T_{r+1},D_r,\varphi_r)\)-stable coloring \(\pi\), every defective
color \(\delta\), and every \(\delta\)-boundary vertex \(x\) with an
earlier \(\delta\)-boundary vertex satisfy the following: if
\(x\in V(T)\), \(m_T(x)\leq a+1\), and slot \(a.\mathsf{succ}\) is a PE
or SE with exact chosen vertex \(v_{a+1}\), then
\[
         x=v_{a+1}\quad\hbox{or}\quad x\prec_{T_{r+1}}v_{a+1}. \tag{B}
\]

For active indices \(s<t\), write their exact supporting vertices as
\(v_s,v_t\), their certified gamma and delta fields as
\(\gamma_s,\delta_s,\gamma_t,\delta_t\), and their exact stored pairs as
\(S_s,S_t\).  Then
\[
 S_s\cap S_t\ne\varnothing
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 v_s=v_t\ \hbox{ and no }r\in L(v_s)\hbox{ satisfies }s<r<t.    \tag{1}
\]
When these equivalent conditions hold,
\[
                  S_s\cap S_t=\{\gamma_t\}=\{\delta_s\}.       \tag{2}
\]
Thus two active stored pairs overlap exactly when their indices are
consecutive members of one support class, which is Part I(5).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportOrder} with the explicit lower-rung
assertion (v).  Its second conclusion says that every active support has
first-appearance index \(j\), and its third conclusion says that distinct
support classes occur in reverse index blocks.  Apply
\noderef{ExtensionBaseDeltaExclusion} with the stated lower-rung
elementarity and assertion-(v) instances.  It gives, for active
\(a\leq b\),
\[
                         \delta_b\ne\gamma_a.                  \tag{3}
\]

Fix one nonempty support class \(L(w)\), and choose
\(r_0\in L(w)\).  Unfolding
\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass} and
\noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}, the active certificate supplies a
vertex \(u\) of the final displayed ETT whose exact chosen-vertex field is
\(\mathsf{some}(u)\), while the support-class filter says that the same
field is \(\mathsf{some}(w)\).  Injectivity of the
\(\mathsf{some}\) constructor gives \(u=w\).
Thus \(r_0\) is active, its positive slot is a PE, its exact chosen vertex
is \(w\), and \(w\) belongs to the final displayed ETT.  The second
conclusion of \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportOrder} now gives
\[
                              m(w)=j.                          \tag{4}
\]

We verify rather than assume that this active class contains every PE
through the terminal round supported at \(w\).  Let \(r\leq q\) be any
such PE slot, so its kind field is PE and its exact chosen-vertex field is
\(w\).  The certificate at \(r.\mathsf{succ}\), supplied by
\noderef{TashkinovSeries}, is one of the constructors of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  The exact PE kind eliminates the initial,
SE, and RE constructors.  In the remaining PE constructor, let \(C_r\)
be its recorded \noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}.  The constructor's
\(\mathsf{currentVertex}\) field and the assumed chosen-vertex equality
give
\(C_r.\mathsf{vertex}=w\).  Its
\(\mathsf{vertexDefective}\) field, unfolded with
\noderef{DefectiveVertex}, has first conjunct
\[
 w\in V\bigl(T_{r+1}\bigr),                                  \tag{5}
\]
where \(T_{r+1}\) is exactly that slot's current ladder tree.
By \noderef{ETTFirstAppearanceIndex}, \(m(w)\) is the least complete
ladder-stage subscript containing \(w\).  Equations (4)--(5) therefore
give \(j=m(w)\leq r+1\).  Thus no terminal-bounded PE supported at
\(w\) precedes \(j\).  Together with \(r\leq q\), the PE kind, the exact
chosen vertex, final-tree membership, and (4), these are precisely the
conditions in \noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}; hence
\(r\in L(w)\).  Conversely, unfolding the two displayed definitions
shows that every member of \(L(w)\) is a PE supported at \(w\); its index
is at most \(q\), because \(q+1=n\) and every Lean index in
\(\operatorname{Fin}(n)\) is at most \(q\).

Consequently the finite nonempty set \(L(w)\) has a unique strictly
increasing enumeration
\[
                         i_0<i_1<\cdots<i_p.                    \tag{6}
\]
It is at the same time the exact enumeration of all terminal-bounded PEs
supported at \(w\).  For completeness, apply the PE-constructor argument
above to the enumerated slot \(i_0\).  Its
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice} vertex is \(w\), and
\noderef{DefectiveVertex} gives \(w\in V(T_{i_0+1})\).  Since
\(i_0\leq q\), successively use the
\(\mathsf{orderedExtension}\) field at each intervening noninitial
constructor of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  Unfolding
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} gives equality of every old vertex
entry with its new entry, and the image description in
\noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} therefore preserves vertex-set
membership at each step.  A finite induction from \(i_0\) to \(q\)
gives \(w\in V(T_{q+1})\), exactly the current-tree membership required
by \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClassColors}.

Supply (6), its exact-range equivalences, the exact gamma and delta fields,
and (3) to \noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClassColors}.  That lemma gives
\[
 \gamma_{i_{a+1}}=\delta_{i_a}\quad(0\leq a<p)                 \tag{7}
\]
and says that
\[
 \gamma_{i_0},\delta_{i_0},\delta_{i_1},\ldots,\delta_{i_p}    \tag{8}
\]
are pairwise distinct.  The PE constructor of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} identifies every exact stored set as
\(S_{i_a}=\{\delta_{i_a},\gamma_{i_a}\}\).  Equations (7)--(8) now show
directly that two stored pairs in this class are disjoint unless their
positions in (6) are consecutive.  For consecutive positions \(a,a+1\),
their only common color is
\(\delta_{i_a}=\gamma_{i_{a+1}}\), proving (2) within one class.

It remains to exclude overlap between different classes.  Invoke
\noderef{ExtensionRules} with the present graph, ETT, MP witness,
positivity witness, and the full explicit six-assertion lower-rung package
listed in the statement.  This supplies current-rung assertion (iii), not
an uncited hypothesis from its proof.  Its second conclusion says that
two distinct PE supporting vertices with equal first-appearance index
have disjoint exact stored sets.  The order lemma gives that equal index,
namely (j), for every active support.  Hence (v_s\ne v_t) implies
\(S_s\cap S_t=\varnothing\).

We can now prove (1).  If the intersection is nonempty, the preceding
paragraph forces \(v_s=v_t=w\).  In enumeration (6), nonconsecutive
positions have disjoint pairs by (7)--(8), so no member of \(L(w)\) lies
strictly between \(s\) and \(t\).  Conversely, if the supports agree and
there is no intervening class member, \(s,t\) are consecutive in (6),
and (7) places the common color \(\delta_s=\gamma_t\) in both stored
pairs.  This proves nonemptiness, (1), and the exact singleton equalities
(2).
\end{proof}
