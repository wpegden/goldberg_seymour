\begin{helper}[Exact terminal route and controlled connecting seed]
Let $\mathcal E$ be an \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} on an exact
tree-sequence $T$, on a finite loopless multigraph with palette $[k]$ and
distinguished edge $e_0$, generated by $\varphi$.  Write $n$ for its
positive rung number, $T_n$ for its terminal ladder tree, and $D_n$ for
its computed terminal stored set.  Either the terminal kind is PE and
$T$ is obtained from $T_n$ by a \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence}
under $\varphi$, or the terminal kind is SE or RE and there are an exact
edge identity $f$ and a seed $S$ such that the terminal connecting-edge
set is exactly $\{f\}$, \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}$(T_n,f,S)$
holds, $f$ is incident with $T_n$, $\varphi(f)=c$ for some
$c\in\overline\varphi(T_n)\cup D_n$, and $T$ is obtained from $S$ by
a \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} under $\varphi$.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let $F$ be the series' final output.  The fields of
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} give $T_n\preccurlyeq T\preccurlyeq F$,
$|T_n|<|T|$, and equality of $\varphi$ with the stored terminal
generating coloring.  Inspect the certificate at the actual last index
in \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  Its initial constructor is impossible
because its index is zero while $n>0$.

First observe that a color in the raw stored set at this last index
belongs to $\overline\varphi(T_n)\cup D_n$.  If it is already missing
on $T_n$, this is immediate.  Otherwise the last raw set occurs in the
finite union defining \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}; subtracting the
terminal missing set does not delete this color, so it belongs to $D_n$.

In PE the constructor supplies a \noderef{TashkinovClosure} from $T_n$
to $F$ under the terminal coloring.  Its augmentation clause and
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} at the exact prefix $T$
give the first alternative.

In SE let $f$ be the exact edge in the supplied
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice}.  The constructor identifies its
singleton with the terminal connecting-edge set, records an exact
adjoined seed $S=T_n+f$, and records a closure from $S$ to $F$.
The choice's defective-vertex and incidence fields put an endpoint of
$f$ in $T_n$.  Its edge-color field gives
$\varphi(f)=c$, and the current-stored equation places $c$ in the raw
stored set.  Apply the preceding observation to obtain the required
controlled-color membership.  Since $|S|=|T_n|+1\le |T|$, the two exact
prefixes $S,T$ of $F$ give $S\preccurlyeq T$: at every index their
entries equal the same entry of $F$.
Restrict the closure's augmentation sequence to $T$ using
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict}.

In RE take the exact edge $f$ in the supplied
\noderef{RevisitingExtensionCertificate}.  Its inside endpoint belongs
to $T_n$, and its endpoint equation proves the required incidence.
The first adjacency of its return path from the edge node $f$ to a
vertex node says that the old color of $f$ is one of its two cycle
colors.  Such a first adjacency exists because the path's endpoints,
an edge node and a vertex node, are distinct.  By
\noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph}, adjacency to another edge node is
impossible.  The constructor's raw stored set contains both cycle
colors, and its current-coloring equation identifies the preceding
coloring with $\varphi$.  The preceding stored-color observation
therefore gives a controlled color for $f$.  Its current-edges equation,
exact adjoined seed, and closure now give all remaining fields exactly
as in SE, restricting the augmentation sequence to $T$.
In particular the seed and singleton are those supplied by the actual
terminal certificate, not an arbitrary one-edge extension.
\end{proof}
