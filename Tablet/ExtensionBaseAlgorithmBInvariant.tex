\begin{definition}
An \noderef{ExtensionBaseAlgorithmBTrace} satisfies invariant (16) when
all its intermediate colorings are proper and leave exactly the same
distinguished edge uncolored, and the following assertions hold before
each step at slot \(t\in L_1^*\).

The manuscript tree \(T_j\) is an ordered initial segment of \(T_t\);
\(\delta_t\) is missing at the fixed Case 1 vertex \(u\); and the
\((\gamma_t,\delta_t)\)-component through \(u\) is a nontrivial path
component in the sense of
\noderef{TwoColorNontrivialPathComponent}.
Among the vertices of \(T_t\), that component contains at most the
terminal supporting vertex \(v_n\).  If it contains \(v_n\), then
\(v_n\) is not an end: its node in the two-color incidence graph does not
have a subsingleton neighbor set.  After the switch, every unprocessed
slot in \(L_1^*\), with its exact certified gamma, delta, and supporting
vertex fields, remains strongly interchangeable.  The same survivor
assertion is recorded at the output for every slot absent from the whole
trace.
\end{definition}
