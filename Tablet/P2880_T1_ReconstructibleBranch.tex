\begin{lemma}[The Cases 1/2 reconstruction branch of the disjoint swap]
Fix the graph, palette, edge, input, displayed tree, generating coloring,
ETT, base, counterexample \(C\), minimum context, and replacement \(P\).
Let \(S\) be a \noderef{P2880_T1_SplitSwapData} for colors
\(\alpha,\beta\) and coloring \(\sigma\).
Write \(\mu=P.\mathit{coloring}\), \(H=P.\mathit{counterexample.hierarchy}\),
\(q=H.\mathit{dividerCount}\), \(A=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q)\),
\(T_{n,q}=H.\mathit{stage}(q)\), and let \(T_n,D_n\) be the terminal
ladder tree and computed Tashkinov stored set of \(P\)'s replacement ETT.
For an ordered pair \((a,b)\), say here that it qualifies if
\[
 (q>0\ \text{and }a\in\overline\mu(T_{n,q}))
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 (q=0\ \text{and }[a\in\overline\mu(T_n)\ \text{or }
 (a\in\overline\mu(A),\ a\notin D_n,\ b\notin D_n)]).
\]
Assume at least one of \((\alpha,\beta)\), \((\beta,\alpha)\) qualifies.
There exists a \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} on the full
\(T\) under \(\sigma\), relative to \(P\)'s replacement ETT, with
maximum property, a hierarchy base, and a good hierarchy having
\noderef{SameGoodHierarchy} with \(P.\mathit{counterexample.good}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put \(X=\noderef{HierarchyFullTailPrefix}(C,j-1)\), with
\(j=\noderef{HierarchyPathNumber}(C.\mathit{hierarchy})\).
If \((\alpha,\beta)\) qualifies, apply
\noderef{StableChangeCasesOneTwoSplitTail} to the replacement input,
ETT, base, counterexample, and minimum context belonging to \(P\).
The distinctness, old penultimate missingness, rooted path certificate,
swap equality, and component separation are respectively the corresponding
fields of \(S\). The qualification just stated is its literal case
premise. The split prefix and strict length, containment in the penultimate
prefix, exact edge fixation below \(X\), retained PE or SE/RE seed route,
old augmentation suffix, two new missing witnesses on \(X\),
penultimate missing-set equality, stored first-position equality, and
affected-Gamma cutoff are exactly the remaining fields of
\noderef{P2880_T1_SplitSwapData}. Thus every premise is supplied,
including the exact non-PE seed and the non-strict boundary
\(A\preccurlyeq B\) when \(j=2\). The provider returns precisely
the full correspondence, maximum witness, base, and same good hierarchy
claimed here; its weak-stability and mod-coloring outputs can be discarded.
If instead \((\beta,\alpha)\) qualifies, use
\noderef{P2880_T1_KempeOrientation}. The graph, component, swap, and
path predicate are unchanged when the two colors are reversed.
Use the separation field for the first \(\beta\)-missing vertex,
reverse the two missing-witness conjunctions, and reverse the disjunction
in the Gamma-cutoff premise. All geometric and route premises are
unchanged. The same split-tail provider, now in that orientation, yields
the asserted tuple under the identical coloring \(\sigma\).
\end{proof}
