\begin{helper}[Support-class color state in Algorithm (A)]
Let \(\mathcal R\) be an
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace}, let \(q\) have exact support
\(v_n\), and suppose initially every active non-\(q\) pair is strongly
interchangeable. Assume the support-class missing-color law (A4): for the
greatest member \(t\) of every full support class \(L(w)\subseteq L^*\),
\[
 \overline\sigma_n(w)\cap\bigcup_{s\in L(w)}S_s=\{\delta_t\},
\]
and assume the active-pair overlap law (O): two exact stored pairs at active
slots \(s<t\) overlap exactly when their supports agree and no member of that
support class lies strictly between them; in the overlap case their
intersection is both \(\{\gamma_t\}\) and \(\{\delta_s\}\). Then immediately
before every step \(a\) of \(\mathcal R\),
\[
 \delta_{t_a}\in\overline{\sigma^a}(v_{t_a})
 \quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \gamma_{t_a}\in\sigma^a(v_{t_a}).
\]
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Fix a support \(w\) that occurs in the trace. Since the trace enumerates
\(L^*=\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationIndices}\), its slot \(t\) is active
and does not belong to the support class of \(v_n\). The exact chosen-vertex
field of the trace therefore implies \(w\ne v_n\). Conversely, every active
slot supported at \(w\) lies outside the support class of \(v_n\), so the
full class \(L(w)=\noderef{ExtensionBaseSupportClass}\) is contained in
\(L^*\). List this nonempty class increasingly as
\[
                         i_0<i_1<\cdots<i_p.
\]
Membership in \(L^*\), expanded through
\noderef{ExtensionBaseActiveIndices}, says that each positive certified slot
has PE kind. Inspect its certificate in \noderef{TashkinovSeries}. The
initial, SE, and RE constructors of \noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} are
excluded by the positive index and exact PE-kind field. In the PE constructor
the stored set is the exact pair
\(S_{i_b}=\{\gamma_{i_b},\delta_{i_b}\}\). Also \(i_b\ne q\): otherwise the
exact chosen-vertex fields would give \(w=v_n\). Initial strong
interchangeability, expanded using \noderef{ETTStrongInterchangeable}, hence
gives \(\gamma_{i_b}\ne\delta_{i_b}\).

Apply (A4) to the greatest member \(i_p\). It makes
\(\delta_{i_p}\) missing at \(w\), and it makes every other color of the
union of the stored pairs present there, by the complementary definitions
\noderef{MissingColors} and \noderef{PresentColors}. In particular every
\(\gamma_{i_b}\) is present. For \(b=p\), this follows from
\(\gamma_{i_p}\ne\delta_{i_p}\). For \(b<p\), law (O) either makes
\(S_{i_b}\) disjoint from \(S_{i_p}\), or, if they are consecutive in the
class, identifies their intersection with
\(\{\delta_{i_b}\}=\{\gamma_{i_p}\}\); in either event
\(\gamma_{i_b}\ne\delta_{i_p}\). Applying (O) to each consecutive pair also
gives
\[
                 \gamma_{i_{b+1}}=\delta_{i_b}
                 \qquad(0\le b<p).                 \tag{1}
\]

The trace processes the members of this class in decreasing order. Before
processing \(i_p\), the preceding paragraph supplies the claimed missing and
present colors. Every incident edge whose color is
\(\gamma_{i_p}\) lies in the two-color component rooted at \(w\), while no
incident edge has color \(\delta_{i_p}\). The transition formula in
\noderef{ExtensionBaseRestorationTrace}, expanded by
\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}, therefore changes every such
\(\gamma_{i_p}\)-edge to \(\delta_{i_p}\) and creates no new
\(\gamma_{i_p}\)-edge at \(w\). Consequently
\(\gamma_{i_p}=\delta_{i_{p-1}}\) is missing after the switch. Law (O) says
that the switched pair meets the next pair only in this delta color, so the
incident edge witnessing that \(\gamma_{i_{p-1}}\) is present is fixed.
Repeating the argument down (1) proves the two assertions before each step
supported at \(w\).

Finally, a switch belonging to a different support class has a color pair
disjoint from every pair in \(L(w)\), by (O). Its transition therefore fixes
all edges whose colors occur in the preceding argument. Thus intervening
steps from other support classes do not alter either assertion. Since every
trace slot has some support, the conclusion holds for every step \(a\).
\end{proof}
