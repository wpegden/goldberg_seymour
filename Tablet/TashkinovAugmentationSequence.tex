\begin{definition}
A Tashkinov augmentation sequence under a fixed coloring $\varphi$ is a
finite, possibly empty, sequence of successive Tashkinov augmentations, all
performed under that same coloring.  Relationally, a tree-sequence $T'$ is
reached from $T$ by such a sequence exactly when $T'$ lies in the
reflexive-transitive closure of the one-step augmentation relation.
\end{definition}
