generating coloring \(\varphi_n\), positive-rung ETT, hierarchy base,
minimum hierarchy counterexample \(C\), and its
\noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext}.  A

\[
 \alpha\in\overline\varphi_n(y_{p-1})\cap
              \overline\varphi_n(y_p),
 \qquad
 \alpha\notin
 \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}
   (C.\mathit{hierarchy},\operatorname{Fin.last}
      (C.\mathit{hierarchy}.\mathit{dividerCount})),
\]
and by the additional branch condition
\[
                 \theta\in\overline\varphi_n(y_{p-1}).
\]

last-edge field of the common-tail datum, rather than an additional
field.

layer.  In particular, it does not extend the theta-present Case 2 datum
and makes no contradictory theta-present assumption.
\end{definition}
