\begin{definition}
Let $T=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_p,y_p)$ and let $\eta$ be a color.  The
\emph{first missing position} of $\eta$ is the least index $i$ for which
$\eta\in\overline\varphi(y_i)$, when such an index exists, and is $p$
otherwise.  The paper's vertex $v_\eta$ is the displayed vertex at this
position.  Thus $v_\eta$ is the first vertex missing $\eta$, or the last
vertex of $T$ if no vertex misses it.
\end{definition}
