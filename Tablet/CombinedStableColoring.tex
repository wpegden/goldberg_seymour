\begin{definition}
In the terminal PE case, let $T_n$ be the last ladder tree, let $R_n$ be
the chosen closure of the supporting prefix, and let $D_n$ be the stored
color set.  A coloring $\sigma$ is
\emph{$(T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable} if it is simultaneously
$(T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable and
$(R_n,\varnothing,\varphi_n)$-stable.  Thus it preserves the controlled
incident edges of $T_n$, the missing-color incident edges of $R_n$, and
all missing-color sets at vertices of both trees.
\end{definition}
