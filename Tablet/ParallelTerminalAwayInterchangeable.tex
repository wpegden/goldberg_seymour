\begin{helper}[Terminal-ladder interchangeability away from the PE pair]
Under the terminal parallel hypotheses and the condition (5.1) induction
context (lower assertions (i)--(vi) and current assertions (iii)--(vi)), let
$v_n,\gamma_n,\delta_n$ be the exact support and color fields of
the last PE step.  If $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are both missing on $T_n$ under
$\sigma_n$ and neither belongs to $\{\gamma_n,\delta_n\}$, then they are
$T_n$-interchangeable under $\sigma_n$, with isolated path components
included.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Write $n=r(T)>0$ and let $T_n$ be the terminal ladder tree.  Inspect the
last certified slot supplied by \noderef{TashkinovSeries}.  Its exact
PE-kind identity selects the PE constructor of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep}.  Injectivity of the exact chosen-vertex,
gamma, and delta field equations identifies its maximum-defective choice
with the stated $v_n,\gamma_n,\delta_n$.  In particular,
\noderef{MaximumDefectiveChoice} gives an exact selected edge $f_n$
incident with $v_n$ and colored $\delta_n$ by the selected coloring
$\pi_{n-1}$.  Its defective-vertex field and
\noderef{DefectiveVertex} give the premise
\[
                         v_n\in V(T_n).                         \tag{1}
\]

The PE color rule \noderef{ParallelExtensionColor} says that
$\gamma_n$ is missing at $v_n$ under $\pi_{n-1}$.  On the other hand,
the exact incidence and color equations for $f_n$ put $\delta_n$ in the
set \noderef{PresentColors} at $v_n$.  Since
\noderef{MissingColors} is the complement of that set,
\[
                         \gamma_n\ne\delta_n.                   \tag{2}
\]
Thus the required inequality is a consequence of the actual PE witness,
not an unstated field of the step constructor.

Instantiate the \texttt{currentFour} field of
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} on the present ETT.  Its common
hypotheses are the exact current rung and maximum property; its PE, support,
gamma, and delta identities are the given field equations; and properness,
\noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored}, and terminal stability are hypotheses of the
lemma.  It follows that, for every $x\in V(T_n)$,
\[
 \operatorname{inl}(x)\in
 P_{v_n}(\gamma_n,\delta_n,\sigma_n)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 x=v_n.                                                         \tag{3}
\]
Together with the separately established membership (1), this is the
singleton-intersection premise of
\noderef{ParallelExtensionUndoStable}.  That lemma applies to
\[
 \tau=\sigma_n/P_{v_n}(\gamma_n,\delta_n,\sigma_n)
\]
and makes $\tau$ stable on the preceding data
$(T_n,D_{n-1},\varphi_{n-1})$.

By (2), \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwapProper} makes $\tau$ proper.  The
definition \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} fixes uncolored values and
maps colored values to colored values, so expanding
\noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored} shows that $\tau$ still leaves exactly
$e_0$ uncolored.  It changes only the values $\gamma_n$ and $\delta_n$.
Because each of $\alpha,\beta$ is distinct from both, for every exact edge
identity $a$ one has
\[
 \tau(a)=\alpha\Longleftrightarrow\sigma_n(a)=\alpha,
 \qquad
 \tau(a)=\beta\Longleftrightarrow\sigma_n(a)=\beta.             \tag{4}
\]
By \noderef{TwoColorIncidenceGraph}, (4) gives equality of the entire
$(\alpha,\beta)$ incidence graphs on the common node set $V\sqcup E$.
Their connected-component supports in \noderef{TwoColorComponent} and
their leaf conditions in \noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} are therefore
identical, including isolated vertex components.  At each vertex, (4) also
gives equality of the alpha- and beta-memberships in
\noderef{PresentColors}; its complement \noderef{MissingColors} and the
vertex union \noderef{TreeMissingColors} show that alpha and beta are
missing on $T_n$ under $\tau$ exactly when they are missing there under
$\sigma_n$.

If $n>1$, put $r=n-1$.  Then $0<r<n$, and
\noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at $r$ gives the actual restricted
maximum ETT whose exact final series tree is $T_n$, whose generating
coloring is $\varphi_{n-1}$, and whose terminal stored set is
$D_{n-1}$.  Apply the \texttt{lowerTwo} field of
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} to this restricted ETT: positivity
and the strict bound are the displayed inequalities, maximum property is
part of the restriction, $\tau$ is proper and exactly-$e_0$-uncolored as
proved above, and its required stability is the conclusion of
\noderef{ParallelExtensionUndoStable}.  Since both alpha and beta are
tree-missing under $\tau$, \texttt{lowerTwo} gives their
$T_n$-interchangeability under $\tau$.

If $n=1$, $T_n$ is the exact output of the certified initial tuple.
The same properness, exact-one-uncoloredness, preceding-data stability,
and tree missingness meet the interchangeability clause of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase}, with the same conclusion under $\tau$.
Finally, the graph equality (4) identifies every full component and every
path-component predicate occurring in
\noderef{TreeInterchangeable}.  Transporting the component equality from
$\tau$ to $\sigma_n$ proves the desired interchangeability, including the
isolated-component cases.
\end{proof}
