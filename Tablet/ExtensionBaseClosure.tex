\begin{lemma}[The closure after Claim 4.3 and equation (12)]
Let \(B\) be the lower-rung ETT whose terminal ladder tree is
\(T_{j-1}\), terminal extension kind \(\Theta_{j-1}\), and exact
connecting-edge set \(F_{j-1}\).  Let \(U_j\) be an exact ordered
tree-sequence having \(T_{j-1}\) as a proper initial segment; in the
extension-base application, \(U_j\) is the exact prefix \(T_j(v_n)\).
Assume the
following exact eligibility disjunction from \noderef{ModColoring}:
\begin{itemize}
\item if \(\Theta_{j-1}=PE\), then \(U_j\) is obtained from
  \(T_{j-1}\) by a Tashkinov augmentation sequence under \(\varrho_1\);
\item or \(\Theta_{j-1}\in\{SE,RE\}\), and there are an exact edge
  identity \(f_{j-1}\) and an exact one-edge seed \(U\) such that
  \[
    F_{j-1}=\{f_{j-1}\},\qquad
    U=T_{j-1}+f_{j-1},
  \]
  in the sense of \noderef{TreeSequenceAdjoinEdge}, and \(U_j\)
  is obtained from \(U\) by a Tashkinov augmentation sequence under
  \(\varrho_1\).
\end{itemize}
Assume that \(\varrho_1\) is a mod-coloring of \(B\), that \(B\) has
the maximum property, and that the lower-rung elementarity assertion
applies to every corresponding ETT with the transported maximum property.

Fix a vertex \(v\) and a finite color set \(S\); in the extension-base
application these are \(v_n\) and
the exact terminal-support color set
\(S(L_1)=\bigcup_{i\in L_1}S_i\).  There is a closure \(T'_j\) of
\(U_j\) under \(\varrho_1\), together with an actual corresponding
ETT certificate, such that this corresponding ETT has the maximum
property and \(V(T'_j)\) is elementary under \(\varrho_1\).  Moreover,
exactly one of the following finite alternatives holds:
\[
\overline\varrho_1(T'_j-v)\cap S\ne\varnothing,
 \qquad
 \overline\varrho_1(T'_j-v)\cap S=\varnothing.                \tag{12}
\]
Here \(j\) is a manuscript ladder stage.  It is not identified with the
terminal manuscript stage \(n\), nor with either stage's zero-based Lean
predecessor slot.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{TashkinovClosureExists}, choose a closure \(T'_j\) of the
displayed seed \(U_j\) under \(\varrho_1\).  Its
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} certificate contains a finite
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} from the seed to \(T'_j\).
Each individual augmentation, by \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}, retains
all old vertex and edge identities as an ordered initial segment.
Composing those initial-segment maps with the given map from
\(T_{j-1}\) to \(U_j\) gives an ordered initial-segment map from
\(T_{j-1}\) to \(T'_j\).  The displayed seed was already strictly longer
than \(T_{j-1}\), so this composite extension is strict.

We next retain the appropriate branch of the eligibility disjunction.  In
the PE branch, transitivity of the reflexive-transitive augmentation
relation \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} composes the sequence
from \(T_{j-1}\) to \(U_j\) with the closure sequence, giving a
sequence from \(T_{j-1}\) to \(T'_j\).  In the SE/RE branch, keep the
same exact edge identity \(f_{j-1}\), the equality
\(F_{j-1}=\{f_{j-1}\}\), and the same one-edge seed
\(U=T_{j-1}+f_{j-1}\).  Transitivity now composes the sequence from
\(U\) to \(U_j\) with the closure sequence, giving a sequence from
\(U\) to \(T'_j\).  Thus in either branch \(T'_j\) satisfies exactly the
corresponding premise of the universal clause in \noderef{ModColoring}.
Applied to the strict extension just constructed, that clause supplies an actual
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate \(C\) on
\(T'_j\).  Apply \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport} to the base maximum
property, the mod-coloring hypothesis, and \(C\).  It proves that the
replacement ETT carried by \(C\) has the maximum property.  The stated
lower-rung elementarity hypothesis now applies to this very certificate
and gives
\[
        \noderef{ColoredVertexSetElementary}
              (G,\varrho_1,V(T'_j)).                           \tag{1}
\]
This proves all mathematical assertions in (12).

Finally form the finite set
\[
 X=\overline\varrho_1(T'_j-v)\cap S
   =\left(\bigcup_{x\in V(T'_j)\setminus\{v\}}
                    \overline\varrho_1(x)\right)\cap S.
\]
Equality to the displayed union is the finite-set meaning of
\noderef{MissingColors}.  A finite set is either empty or has an element.
In the latter case \(X\ne\varnothing\); in the former case
\(X=\varnothing\).  These are precisely Case 1 and Case 2, respectively,
and they are mutually exclusive.  Thus the chosen closure carries the
claimed split as well as its corresponding-ETT, maximum-property, and
elementarity certificates.
\end{proof}
