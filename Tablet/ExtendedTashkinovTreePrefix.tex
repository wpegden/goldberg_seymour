\begin{lemma}[A positive certified series prefix is an ETT]
Let \(\mathcal E\) be an \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} with \(n\) rounds and
certified series \(\mathcal S\).  Fix \(m\) with \(1\leq m<n\), a tree index
\(t\in\operatorname{Fin}(n+2)\) of numerical value \(m+1\), and a coloring
index \(c\in\operatorname{Fin}(n+1)\) of numerical value \(m\).  There is an
ETT \(\mathcal E^{\leq m}\), displayed on exactly
\(\mathcal S.\mathit{data}.\mathit{tree}(t)\) with generating coloring exactly
\(\mathcal S.\mathit{data}.\mathit{coloring}(c)\), whose rung is \(m\).

Its tree array is the original tree array restricted through the canonical
same-value inclusion.  Its coloring, raw stored-color, exact connecting-edge,
extension-kind, chosen-vertex, outside-vertex, delta, and gamma arrays are the
corresponding restricted original arrays.  At every retained data index its
computed \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors} set equals the old computed set, and
its step certificate is heterogeneously equal to the old included certificate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{TashkinovSeriesPrefix} at \(m\), obtaining a certified series
\(\mathcal S^-\) whose data are exactly the literal prefix and whose
certificates have the stated heterogeneous equalities.  Since \(m\geq1\),
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesFinalETTExists} turns \(\mathcal S^-\) into an ETT of
rung \(m\), displayed on its final tree slot and final coloring slot, with
dependent series exactly \(\mathcal S^-\).

The final tree index of an \(m\)-round series has numerical value \(m+1\).
After inclusion into the old tree-index type it therefore equals \(t\), by
extensionality of finite indices and the hypothesis on \(t\).  Likewise, the
last coloring index has value \(m\) and its inclusion equals \(c\).  The
literal prefix definition consequently identifies the two displayed objects
with the exact objects asserted in the statement; transport the ETT along
these equalities.

The cited final-series result gives rung equality and equality of the dependent
series.  Substitute those equalities.  Every tree and raw data-array conclusion
then follows directly from \noderef{TashkinovSeriesDataPrefix}.  The computed
stored-color conclusion is \noderef{TashkinovStoredColorsPrefix}, and the
certificate conclusion is the corresponding conclusion of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesPrefix}.  Since the connecting-edge array itself is
copied, these are equalities of exact edges, including distinct parallel
edges, rather than endpoint-only comparisons.
\end{proof}
