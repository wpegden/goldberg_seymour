\begin{lemma}[First-missing cutoffs agree below an exact prefix]
Let \(G\) be a finite loopless multigraph with an exact edge type, let
\(k\) be a palette size and \(e_0\) a distinguished edge, and let
\(A,T\) be \noderef{TreeSequence}s based at \(e_0\).  Suppose that \(A\)
is an exact ordered \noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} of \(T\).  For
every partial coloring \(\sigma\), color \(\eta\), and natural number
\(u\leq |A|\),
\[
 \min\{\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}(\sigma,A,\eta),u\}
 =
 \min\{\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}(\sigma,T,\eta),u\},
\]
where first-missing positions are read as their numeric indices and use
the terminal fallback convention.  Missingness here is the vertexwise
notion given by \noderef{MissingColors}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a tree \(S\in\{A,T\}\), let
\[
 I_S=\{i:0\leq i\leq |S|,
       \ \eta\in\noderef{MissingColors}(G,\sigma,S.\mathit{vertex}(i))\}.
\]
By \noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}, the numeric first-missing
position \(p_S\) is \(\min I_S\) when \(I_S\neq\varnothing\), and is
\(|S|\) otherwise.  The exact ordered
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} relation gives \(|A|\leq|T|\) and
identifies \(A.\mathit{vertex}(i)\) with
\(T.\mathit{vertex}(i)\) for every \(0\leq i\leq|A|\).

First suppose \(I_A\neq\varnothing\), and write \(m_A=\min I_A\).
The vertex equality at \(m_A\) makes \(m_A\) an element of \(I_T\), so
\(I_T\neq\varnothing\) and its least element \(m_T\) satisfies
\(m_T\leq m_A\leq |A|\).  The same exact-prefix vertex equality is
therefore available at \(m_T\), and it carries the missing-color
membership defining \(m_T\in I_T\) back to \(m_T\in I_A\).  Minimality
of \(m_A\) gives \(m_A\leq m_T\).  Thus \(p_A=m_A=m_T=p_T\), and taking
the minimum with \(u\) gives the claimed equality.

Now suppose \(I_A=\varnothing\).  Then \(p_A=|A|\).  If
\(I_T\neq\varnothing\), its least element \(m_T\) must satisfy
\(|A|\leq m_T\): otherwise \(m_T<|A|\), so the exact-prefix vertex
equality at \(m_T\) would carry its missing-color membership back to an
element of \(I_A\), a contradiction.  Hence \(p_T=m_T\geq|A|\).  If
\(I_T=\varnothing\), then \(p_T=|T|\geq|A|\) instead.  In both subcases
\(u\leq|A|\leq p_T\), while \(u\leq p_A=|A|\).  Consequently
\(\min\{p_A,u\}=u=\min\{p_T,u\}\), as required.
\end{proof}
