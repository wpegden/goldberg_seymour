\begin{lemma}
For every edge identity $e\in E$, coercing both endpoints of $e$ in the
incident core $G^{\circ}$ back to $V(G)$ gives exactly the endpoint pair of
$e$ in $G$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IncidentCore}, the core endpoint pair of $e$ consists of the two
entries of a representative of the unordered pair $G.\operatorname{ends}(e)$,
each equipped with the incidence witness supplied by $e$.  Forgetting those
witnesses therefore returns the representative pair.  The defining quotient
property of an unordered pair identifies that representative with
$G.\operatorname{ends}(e)$ itself.  No operation has been performed on the
edge identity $e$, so this equality holds separately for every member of a
parallel class.
\end{proof}
