\begin{lemma}[Situation 7.2, Subcase 2.1]
Every \noderef{HierarchySituationTwoCaseTwoData} is impossible.
Equivalently, Situation 7.2, Case 2 cannot occur when
\(\theta=\varphi_n(e_p)\) is present at \(y_{p-1}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 D_{n,q}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(C.\mathit{hierarchy},q).
\]
If there is no \(\eta\in D_{n,q}\) which is missing at
\(y_{p-1}\) and satisfies \(\theta\in\Gamma^q_\eta\), this is exactly
the hypothesis of
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoSubcaseTwoOneNonexceptional}, which gives
a contradiction.

Otherwise choose such an \(\eta\).  Its stored membership, missingness at
\(y_{p-1}\), and Gamma membership are exactly the three additional
hypotheses of
\noderef{HierarchySituationTwoSubcaseTwoOneStoredException}; that lemma
also gives a contradiction.  The two alternatives are exhaustive, so the
Case 2 datum is impossible.
\end{proof}
