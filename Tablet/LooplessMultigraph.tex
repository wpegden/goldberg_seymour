\begin{definition}
A loopless multigraph on a vertex type $V$ and an edge type $E$ consists of
an endpoint map $\operatorname{ends}:E\to\operatorname{Sym}^2(V)$ such that
$\operatorname{ends}(e)\ne(v,v)$ for every $e\in E$ and $v\in V$.
The elements of $E$, rather than their endpoint pairs, are the edge
identities; in particular, distinct parallel edges are permitted to have the
same endpoint pair.  Whenever a finite multigraph is considered, both $V$
and $E$ are finite.
\end{definition}
