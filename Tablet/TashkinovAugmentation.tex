\begin{definition}
Let $T$ and $T'$ be tree-sequences for the same graph and distinguished
first edge, under a fixed partial coloring $\varphi$.  The relation
$\operatorname{TA}_{\varphi}(T,T')$ holds when $T'$ has exactly one more
edge than $T$, $T$ is its ordered initial segment, and the new last edge of
$T'$ has a color in $\overline\varphi(T)$.  The tree-sequence attachment
and injectivity data then say that this new edge joins its new outside
vertex to an earlier vertex of $T$.  Thus $T'$ is obtained from $T$ by one
Tashkinov augmentation under $\varphi$.
\end{definition}
