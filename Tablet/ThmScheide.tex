\begin{theorem}[Small Tashkinov order; Scheide Proposition 4.5]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless critical multigraph in which every vertex is
incident with an edge.  If
\[
 \chi'(G)\geq\Delta(G)+2
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 t(G)<11,
\]
then $G$ is elementary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $k=\chi'(G)-1$.  The displayed degree hypothesis implies
$\chi'(G)\geq2$, so subtraction is exact and
\[
 \chi'(G)=k+1,
 \qquad
 \Delta(G)+1\leq k.                                 \tag{1}
\]
Suppose for a contradiction that $G$ is not elementary.  Criticality,
the no-isolated-vertex hypothesis, (1), non-elementarity, and $t(G)<11$
are exactly the hypotheses of
\noderef{ScheideOrderNineStructuralConfiguration}.  It therefore supplies
an edge identity $e_0$, a partial $k$-edge-coloring $\varphi$, and an
ordered tree-sequence $T$ carrying a Scheide structural configuration.
The structural lemma \noderef{ScheideStructuralLemma}, applied to these
same identities and data, concludes that $G$ is elementary.  This
contradicts the supposition, so $G$ is elementary.
\end{proof}
