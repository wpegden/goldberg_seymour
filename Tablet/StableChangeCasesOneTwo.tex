\begin{lemma}[Stable change, cases 1 and 2]
Fix a finite loopless multigraph with its exact edge set, a palette of
$k$ colors, and a distinguished edge $e_0$.  Let $(T,\varphi_n)$ be a
positive-rung extended Tashkinov tree.  Let $\mathcal B$ be a hierarchy
base and let
\[
 T_n=T_{n,0}\subset T_{n,1}\subset\cdots\subset
 T_{n,q}\subset T_{n,q+1}=T
\]
be a good hierarchy $H$ under $\varphi_n$.  Put
\[
 A=T_{n,q}^{*}=\noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}(H,q),\qquad
 D_n=\noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}(\mathcal E,q_n),
 \qquad D_{n,q}=\noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}(H,q).
\]
Assume that $T$ has maximum property, that $V(T)$ is not elementary,
that $\varphi_n$ is proper and leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and that
these data form a \noderef{HierarchyCounterexample} $C$.

Assume, uniformly for every positive-rung maximum ETT on the same graph,
palette, and distinguished edge, with arbitrary certified input and
generating colorings and with rung number smaller than that of $T$, the
six lower-rung assertions of the technical theorem: elementarity,
terminal interchangeability, the unconditional PE stability-before-support
assertion, the predecessor-ladder stability assertion conditional on
first-appearance index greater than one, and the unconditional stored-pair
disjointness assertion, the terminal PE component assertion, the defective-endpoint
order assertion, and the mod-coloring assertion.  Assume also that every
same-rung maximum ETT with a good hierarchy having fewer than $q$
dividers is elementary.  Assume that $C$ is lexicographically minimal
among same-rung hierarchy counterexamples with exactly $q$ dividers, in
the sense of \noderef{HierarchyCounterexampleLess} and
\noderef{HierarchyCounterexampleMeasure}, and assume
\[
                 2n+11\leq |\overline\varphi_n(T_n)|.          \tag{L}
\]

Put $\ell=|T|-|A|$, the number of exact tail additions above $A$, and
\[
 T(y_{\ell-1})=T\big|_{\min\{|A|+(\ell-1),|T|\}}.
\]
Assume (6.6), namely that $V(T(y_{\ell-1}))$ is elementary under
$\varphi_n$.  Let $\alpha\ne\beta$ be colors missing on
$T(y_{\ell-1})$, and put
\[
 i_c=\noderef{HierarchyFirstMissingPosition}(\varphi_n,T,c),
 \qquad v_c=T.\mathit{vertex}(i_c).
\]

Let $z$ certify a possibly singleton
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent} $Q$ of the full
$(\alpha,\beta)$-component through $z$, and suppose that the full node
support of $Q$ is disjoint from the full component through
$\operatorname{inl}v_\alpha$.  Thus $Q$ is not required to contain an
edge, and no path hypothesis is imposed on the component through
$v_\alpha$.  Put
\[
 \sigma_n=\noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap}
       (G,\varphi_n,\alpha,\beta,z).
\]
Suppose exactly one of the following two paper cases holds:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $q\geq1$ and $\alpha\in\overline\varphi_n(T_{n,q})$;
\item $q=0$ and either
$\alpha\in\overline\varphi_n(T_n)$, or
$\alpha\in\overline\varphi_n(A)$ with
$\alpha,\beta\notin D_n$.
\end{enumerate}
Then all of the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The coloring $\sigma_n$ is
$(T_{n,q}^{*},D_n,\varphi_n)$-weakly stable in the exact sense of
\noderef{HierarchyWeaklyStable}.
\item The coloring $\sigma_n$ is a
$\varphi_n\bmod T_n$ coloring in the sense of \noderef{ModColoring}.
Either $A=T_n$, the non-strict endpoint which the tablet's positive-rung
ETT type intentionally does not repackage, or $A$ has an actual maximum
ETT presentation under $\sigma_n$.
\item If $q\geq1$, the same ordered base core and closure and the stages
\[
 T_n=T_{n,0}\subset\cdots\subset T_{n,q}=A
\]
form a good hierarchy of an actual maximum ETT on $A$ under
$\sigma_n$.  The terminal extension kind of this prefix ETT is exactly
the terminal extension kind of the original ETT on $T$.  Its divider
count is $q-1$; at every retained index the sets $D_{n,j}$ agree exactly,
and
$\Gamma^j_\eta$ agrees exactly for every $\eta\in D_{n,j}$.  In addition,
\[
 T_{n,q}\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,q}}\Gamma^{q-1}_\eta\right)^{-}
 \text{-closed under }\sigma_n.                                \tag{M}
\]
\end{enumerate}
No corresponding ETT or good-hierarchy assertion for the full tree $T$
is claimed in these two cases.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Package the stated properness, one-uncoloredness, lower-rung assertions,
fewer-divider induction, lexicographic minimality, and large bound as
a \noderef{HierarchyMinimumCounterexampleContext}.
The \noderef{HierarchyCounterexample} fields supply the original maximum
property, positive rung, hierarchy, and good witness.

Apply \noderef{P2813_T1_BaseControl}.  Its prefix is exactly the displayed
$T(y_{\ell-1})$; both colors are missing there, the root has the stated path
certificate, the swap equality and component disjointness are the given
ones, and its case alternative is precisely Cases 1/2 here.
It returns the combined-stable base relation in PE and ordinary
$(T_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stability otherwise, equality of every vertex
missing set on $A$, properness and exactly-one-uncoloredness of $\sigma_n$,
and outside recoloring at every starred stage on which an affected color
is both missing and singleton-closed.  Retain all five assertions.       \tag{1}

Supply the additional predecessor elementarity needed for local edge
control.  If $n=1$, the first conclusion of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase} makes $T_n$ elementary under the initial,
hence pre-step, coloring.  If $n>1$, use
\noderef{CertifiedETTPredecessor} on the original maximum ETT.
It returns an actual positive-rung maximum ETT with rung $n-1$,
tree exactly $T_n$, and generating coloring exactly the original
pre-step coloring.  Lower assertion (i) applied to this actual
certificate gives the same elementarity.  No zero-rung ETT is used.
Now apply \noderef{P2813_T1_DisplayedEdges} to the arbitrary hierarchy
and good witness, the two elementarity facts, the swap equation,
one-uncoloredness and base relation in (1), and the closed-stage outside
control in (1).  Thus
\[
 \sigma_n(f)=\varphi_n(f)\quad(f\text{ displayed in }A),\qquad
 \overline\sigma_n(v)=\overline\varphi_n(v)\quad(v\in V(A)).
                                                                    \tag{2}
\]
In particular the exact SE/RE stored connecting seed is fixed, as covered
by that helper, rather than excluded by an unused-tail assertion.

At index zero, $A$ is the base core, and the base relation in (1) is
exactly \noderef{HierarchyWeaklyStable}.  At a positive last index,
$A=H.\mathit{stage}(q)$; (2) supplies
\noderef{TreeColoringInvariant} there, including every required
noninitial displayed edge and every vertex missing set.  Together with
the base relation, this is the positive-index clause of
\noderef{HierarchyWeaklyStable}, proving conclusion 1.

Apply \noderef{P2813_T1_PrefixReconstruction} with the context,
properness and one-uncoloredness of $\sigma_n$, the base relation
in (1), and the two equalities in (2).
It supplies \noderef{ModColoring} of the original ETT and either $A=T_n$
or its own corresponding maximum ETT on exactly $A$ generated by
$\sigma_n$.  In the latter branch expose that certificate's input
coloring and replacement ETT.  This proves conclusion 2, retaining the
non-strict endpoint explicitly.

For conclusion 3 fix $q>0$.  Composing consecutive exact-prefix
relations in \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} puts every old stage of index
at most $q$ and every starred stage inside $A$, and puts $A$ inside $T$.
The core lies in all starred stages.  By \noderef{HierarchyBase},
$T_n$ is a prefix of the core: it is equal to the core in non-PE, and the
left-prefix field of \noderef{OrderedTreeUnion} gives the PE inclusion.
Thus $T_n\preccurlyeq A$.  Applying
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset} and taking unions
of (2), using \noderef{TreeMissingColors}, gives
\[
       \overline\sigma_n(U)=\overline\varphi_n(U)
                        \quad(U\preccurlyeq A).                 \tag{3}
\]

Apply \noderef{GoodHierarchyPrefixRepackage} at the positive index $q$.
It returns an old-color maximum ETT $\mathcal E^0_A$ on the exact tree
$A=H.\mathit{stage}(q)$, with the same rung and entire certified series,
a base $B^0_A$ with exactly the old core, closure, and support position,
and a hierarchy $H^0_A$ with $q-1$ dividers and exactly the retained
stages and dividers.  The equality of entire series identifies its
terminal ladder tree, kind, and computed stored set with $T_n$, the
original kind, and $D_n$.

We construct an actual $\sigma_n$-generated prefix ETT and ordinary
hierarchy before using the retained assembly lemma.
In PE, \noderef{P2813_T1_TerminalRoute} supplies an old augmentation
sequence from $T_n$ to $T$.  The stage inclusions above permit
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} to restrict it to $A$.
Transport this restricted sequence under the two equalities (2).
For a step $U\to W\preccurlyeq A$, the exact added edge is a displayed
edge of $A$ and hence keeps its value; its missing witness in $U$
lies in $A$ and keeps its missing set.  Reuse the length and exact-prefix
equations of \noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}.
Induction on \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} gives the same
sequence from $T_n$ to $A$ under $\sigma_n$.
The PE relation in (1), transported through the old prefix's terminal
tree, closure, and stored-set equalities, is precisely combined stability
for $\mathcal E^0_A$.  Its maximum property and same rung allow the given
lower package to apply.  With properness and one-uncoloredness of both
colorings, \noderef{HierarchySplitter} therefore returns its own
corresponding certificate, its own maximum witness, a replacement base,
and ordinary hierarchy with the same core, closure, stages and dividers.
Choose this certificate's replacement ETT, not the independently
obtained mod-coloring certificate.  Retain the exact core and PE closure
equalities supplied by \noderef{SameHierarchyStages}.

In non-PE, take the corresponding maximum certificate supplied by
prefix reconstruction.  Its equality endpoint $A=T_n$ is impossible:
the first strict stage inclusion gives
$|T_n|<|H.\mathit{stage}(1)|\le |A|$.
By \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}, the certificate's
terminal ladder tree is exactly $T_n$ and its terminal kind is the
original non-PE kind.  Rebuild its base with the old core and closure.
Transport the old support position and positivity along the terminal
tree equality.  The terminal missing-color equation is definitional;
the PE implication is vacuous; the nonparallel core equation is the old
core equality with $T_n$ transported along that same tree equality.
These are all fields of \noderef{HierarchyBase}.
Copy the stages and divider positions from the old prefix hierarchy.
Its base-stage equation transports along the terminal tree equality,
and its core-inside-first equation along the core equality; all other
\noderef{TashkinovHierarchy} fields are unchanged exact-tree equations.

In either branch call the resulting maximum ETT $\mathcal E_A$ and
its base $B_A$.  Copy the retained ordinary hierarchy onto it and denote
it by $K$.  It has $q-1$ dividers and exactly the old stages.
In the PE branch, correspondence with the old prefix and its same-series
equality gives equality of the actual terminal kind with the original
kind; in non-PE correspondence gives that equality directly.
Correspondence also fixes all raw stored sets.  The final generating
coloring is $\sigma_n$, the terminal ladder tree is exactly $T_n$, and
(3) fixes its missing colors.  Expanding \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}
therefore gives equality of the computed terminal stored set with $D_n$.
Retain these exact tree, kind, stored-set, core and closure equalities.

We next produce the surviving PE witness required by assembly.
Put $R_0=\bigcup_{\eta\in D_{n,0}}C.\mathit{good}.\Gamma^0_\eta$.
If the original terminal kind is PE, its old good base field says the
core is not $R_0$-minus-closed.  By
\noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed}, \noderef{TreeColorSetClosed} and
\noderef{BoundaryColorEdges}, there exist
$c\in\overline\varphi_n(B.\mathit{core})\setminus R_0$ and an exact
core-boundary edge $f$ whose old color is $c$.
If $c$ is unaffected, \noderef{TwoColorComponentSwap} fixes $f$.
Otherwise consider whether $c$ is singleton-closed on any starred stage.
If so, its core-missing witness lies in that stage by the exact
core inclusion; hence the outside-control assertion in (1) applies.
The boundary edge is incident with the core by \noderef{BoundaryEdges},
and therefore with that starred stage.
\noderef{OutsideKempeRecoloringStable} fixes its exact value.
If no starred stage is singleton-closed for $c$, its core witness lies
in $A$.  Apply the no-closed conclusion of \noderef{rutcor} using the
predecessor elementarity established above and the given prefix
elementarity.  At index zero it puts $c$ in $R_0$, contradicting its
choice.  Thus in all possible branches $\sigma_n(f)=\varphi_n(f)$.
This gives the required explicit surviving edge and its old missing
color outside the initial reserve.

Finally apply \noderef{P2813_T1_RetainedHierarchy} to the supplied
$\mathcal E_A,B_A,K$ and all their agreements just established, (2),
the swap/properness/one-uncoloredness data, outside control (1), and
the surviving PE witness.
It does not assume $K$ good.  It constructs every
\noderef{GoodHierarchy} field with the old total Gamma family, using
fallback-sensitive exact-prefix segment transport for avoidance.
It returns equality of every retained stored set and every total Gamma
value, together with minus-closedness of $H.\mathit{stage}(q)$ using
the original $D_{n,q}$ and $\Gamma^{q-1}$.
Restricting total Gamma equality to the stored colors gives exactly
conclusion 3.  Retain the chosen maximum witness and exact terminal-kind
equality.  No full-tree replacement conclusion is asserted or used.
\end{proof}
