\begin{helper}[The support-prefix closure package]
Let $T,T_n,\sigma_n,J_n$ satisfy the terminal parallel hypotheses, and assume
the lower-rung six-assertion package together with the already established
current assertions (iii)--(vi).  Then $V(J_n)$ is elementary and closed under
$\sigma_n$.  Moreover, for any two colors of which at least one is missing on
$J_n$, the two colors are $J_n$-interchangeable.  This conclusion includes
isolated two-color path components.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
Let $v_n$ be the exact terminal support recorded by the PE clause of
\noderef{HierarchyBase}, and put $j=m(v_n)$.  That clause also makes
$J_n$ a \noderef{TashkinovClosure}, under $\sigma_n$, of the ordered
prefix $T_n(v_n)$.  Since $v_n\in V(T_n)$, the minimum in
\noderef{ETTFirstAppearanceIndex} satisfies $1\leq j\leq n$.
The retained ordered-stage equalities in
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegment} identify this prefix with
$T_j(v_n)$ and show that $v_n\notin V(T_{j-1})$ when $j>1$.

Instantiate the \texttt{currentThree} field of
\noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} on the present ETT, using its exact
rung equality, maximum property, terminal PE kind, support identity, and
first-appearance equality.  Its hypotheses are the assumed properness,
exact-one-uncoloredness, and terminal stability of $\sigma_n$.  The first
conclusion is that $\sigma_n$ is
\noderef{StableColoringBeforeVertex} before $v_n$, relative to
$(D_{j-1},\varphi_{j-1})$; when $j>1$, its second conclusion is the full
\noderef{StableColoring} relation on $T_{j-1}$ relative to the same data.

We first establish exact colors on the prefix.  For every nonroot displayed
edge $g$ of $T_j$, the first edge-control inclusion in
\noderef{HkuTreeColorsControlled} puts
$\varphi_{j-1}(g)$ in
$\overline\varphi_{j-1}(T_j)\cup D_{j-1}$.  The attachment equations in
\noderef{TreeSequence} put both endpoints of $g$ in $V(T_j)$, so $g$ is
in the set defined by \noderef{InternalEdges}.  Applying
\noderef{SameColorSeriesPreserved} from this slot through the terminal
slot therefore gives
$\varphi_n(g)=\varphi_{j-1}(g)$.  Before using terminal stability, invoke
the terminal edge-control conclusion of
\noderef{HkuTreeColorsControlled}: since $T_j$ is an ordered initial
segment of $T_n$, it gives $\sigma_n(g)=\varphi_n(g)$ for every displayed
identity $g$ of $T_j$, including the root.  Thus
\[
 \sigma_n(g)=\varphi_{j-1}(g)                                  \tag{1}
\]
for every nonroot displayed edge; at the root both sides are uncolored by
\noderef{IsOneEdgeUncolored}.

Now inspect, inside the certified \noderef{TashkinovSeries}, the
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} constructor which produces $T_j$.  Its
\noderef{TashkinovClosure} field gives a
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} under $\varphi_{j-1}$ from the
prescribed seed to $T_j$: the seed is $T_{j-1}$ in the PE case, the exact
one-edge extension of $T_{j-1}$ by the recorded connecting identity in
the SE/RE cases, and the initial one-edge tree when $j=1$.  First
appearance puts all of $T_{j-1}$ strictly before $v_n$; in the SE/RE case
the adjoined connecting entry is the first entry after $T_{j-1}$, so it is
either before $v_n$ or is the entry of $v_n$ itself.  Thus the appropriate
exact seed is an ordered initial segment of $T_j(v_n)$.  Hence
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequenceRestrict} restricts the sequence at
$T_j(v_n)$.

Consider one step $A\to A'$ in this restricted sequence.  By
\noderef{TashkinovAugmentation}, $A$ is an exact ordered initial segment
of $A'$, and the new edge $a$ has a color $c$ missing at a vertex of
$A$.  The new vertex of $A'$ is no later than $v_n$, so every vertex of
$A$ is strictly before $v_n$.  The vertex clause of
\noderef{StableColoringBeforeVertex} preserves the missingness of $c$;
its incident-edge clause fixes the identity $a$, since $a$ is incident
with its old endpoint and $c$ is in the corresponding controlled set.
Equivalently, (1) gives the same exact color equality.  Thus this is also
a Tashkinov augmentation under $\sigma_n$.  Induction over the finite
relation \noderef{TashkinovAugmentationSequence} transports the entire
restricted sequence.  Composing it with the augmentation sequence from
$T_j(v_n)$ to $J_n$ already supplied by the hierarchy-base closure proves
that $J_n$ is a $\sigma_n$-closure of the same prescribed seed.  Its final
closure clause is exactly
\noderef{ColoredVertexSetClosed} for $V(J_n)$.

Suppose first that $j>1$, and put $r=j-1$.  The inequality $j>1$ gives
$0<r$, while $j\leq n$ gives $r<n$.  Applying
\noderef{CertifiedETTRestriction} at $r$ produces an actual restricted
ETT of rung exactly $r$, with maximum property, terminal ladder tree
$T_{j-1}$, displayed next tree $T_j$, generating coloring
$\varphi_{j-1}$, and all stored sets, connecting identities, kinds, and
step certificates retained exactly.  The full stage-stability conclusion
above is therefore precisely the terminal-ladder stability hypothesis for
this restricted ETT.  Instantiate the \texttt{lowerSix}, not the
\texttt{currentSix}, field of \noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} on it:
properness and exact-one-uncoloredness are assumed, positivity is $0<r$,
the strict rung bound is $r<n$, maximum property comes from the
restriction, and the required stage stability was just obtained.  Hence
$\sigma_n$ is a \noderef{ModColoring} of the restricted ETT.

We verify the exact eligibility condition in that definition.  The
retained last constructor has one of the three noninitial kinds.  In its
PE case, the transported augmentation sequence above starts at exactly
$T_{j-1}$.  In its SE or RE case, the retained constructor supplies an
exact identity $f_{j-1}$, the equality
$F_{j-1}=\{f_{j-1}\}$, and the exact one-edge seed
$T_{j-1}+f_{j-1}$; the transported sequence starts at that seed.  These
are respectively the PE and SE/RE disjuncts in
\noderef{ModColoring}, not merely vertex-set inclusions.  In every case
$T_{j-1}$ is an ordered initial segment of $J_n$.  Moreover,
$v_n\notin V(T_{j-1})$ by first appearance, while the starting prefix and
the one-step initial-segment property put $v_n\in V(J_n)$; thus the
extension is strict.  The universal clause of the mod-coloring definition
therefore supplies an actual
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree} displayed on $J_n$ and
generated by $\sigma_n$.

By \noderef{MaximumPropertyTransport}, this replacement ETT has maximum
property.  Its rung is still $r$, so $0<r<n$; applying
\texttt{lowerOne} from \noderef{TechnicalInductionContext} to this actual
replacement makes $V(J_n)$ elementary under $\sigma_n$.

Let $L$ be the exact final series tree of the replacement.  The
\noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} certificate makes $J_n$ an ordered initial
segment of $L$.  Hence \noderef{TreeSequenceVertexSet} and the union
definition \noderef{TreeMissingColors} show that every color missing on
$J_n$ is missing on $L$.  The same replacement still has positive rung
$r<n$ and maximum property.  The coloring $\sigma_n$ is proper and leaves
exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and reflexivity in \noderef{sc2} makes it stable
on $L$ relative to its own generating coloring and terminal stored set in
the sense of \noderef{StableColoring}.  Thus the \texttt{lowerTwo} field
applied to the replacement makes $L$ interchangeable for any requested
pair with a color missing on $J_n$.

To pass back to $J_n$, take two full two-color path components meeting
$J_n$, including the isolated components allowed by
\noderef{TwoColorPathComponent}.  Their meeting vertices lie in $L$ by
the exact initial segment.  Interchangeability on $L$ identifies the two
full connected components.  This is precisely
\noderef{TreeInterchangeable} on $J_n$; no restriction of an incidence
component is being taken.

It remains to treat $j=1$.  Here the reconstructed augmentation sequence
starts at the original length-one tree rooted at $e_0$ and ends in the
$\sigma_n$-closure $J_n$.  Use $\sigma_n$ as both input and initial
coloring, use empty initial stored and connecting sets and absent initial
optional fields, and use this exact closure.  The graph-criticality,
chromatic-index, and degree clauses of the original
\noderef{KTriple} do not depend on its coloring, while the hypotheses give
properness and exactly one uncolored edge for $\sigma_n$.  Consequently
these data form a $k$-triple.  They satisfy the initial constructor of
\noderef{TashkinovSeriesStep} and hence form a genuine certified
\noderef{TashkinovSeries} whose initial output is $J_n$.  Reflexivity in
\noderef{sc2} explicitly makes $\sigma_n$ stable relative to itself (and
the empty initial stored set), so every stability premise of
\noderef{TechnicalTheoremBase} is met.  That theorem gives both
elementarity of $V(J_n)$ and interchangeability for every pair with a
color missing on $J_n$.  Together with the closedness proved above, this
establishes all three conclusions in both cases.
\end{proof}
