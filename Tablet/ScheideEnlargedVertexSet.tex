\begin{definition}
For a reduced configuration with defective-color setup and a chosen
replacement pair $U_\delta=\{u^1_\delta,u^2_\delta\}$ for every defective
color, define
\[
 X=V(T)\cup\bigcup_{\delta\in\mathcal D(T,e_0,\varphi)}U_\delta.
\]
The union is a union of vertex identities indexed by the defective colors.
\end{definition}
