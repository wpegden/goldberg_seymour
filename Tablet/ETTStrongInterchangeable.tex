\begin{definition}
Let \(t\) be a PE slot of a certified extended Tashkinov tree, with
supporting vertex \(v_t\).  For two distinct colors \(\alpha,\beta\),
let \(\epsilon(t)\) be the first later slot which is not an RE: thus
\(t<\epsilon(t)\), the slot \(\epsilon(t)\) is not an RE, and every slot
strictly between them is an RE.  The colors \(\alpha,\beta\) are
\(T_t\)-strongly interchangeable under a coloring \(\varphi\) if such an
\(\epsilon(t)\) exists and, for every
\(x\in V(T_t)\setminus\{v_t\}\), the
\((\alpha,\beta)\)-component through \(x\) is a cycle, avoids \(v_t\),
and all of its vertex nodes belong to \(V(T_{\epsilon(t)})\).

The PE kind and its exact supporting vertex are part of the predicate, and
the first-non-RE condition is expressed by a witness together with its
minimality clauses rather than by an arbitrary choice function.
\end{definition}
