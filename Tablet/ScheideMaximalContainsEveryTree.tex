\begin{lemma}[Scheide maximal-tree package (c)]
Let $T$ be fixed-pair maximal for the $k$-triple
$(G,e_0,\varphi)$.  Every Tashkinov tree $S$ for the same pair satisfies
$V(S)\subseteq V(T)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $X=V(T)$, which is closed by
\noderef{ScheideMaximalElementaryClosed}.  Both ends of $e_0$ lie in $X$:
$e_0$ is the first tree edge, and the attachment clause of
\noderef{TreeSequence} identifies its two ends with the first two vertices
of $T$.

Suppose that the displayed sequence
$S=(y_0,e_1,y_1,\ldots,e_n,y_n)$ has a vertex outside $X$, and choose its
first such vertex $y_r$.  Since the first two vertices are the two ends of
$e_0$, both lie in $X$, so $r\geq2$.  Every vertex before $y_r$ lies in
$X$.  The tree edge identity $e_r$ joins $y_r$ to an earlier vertex of
$S$, hence is a boundary edge identity of $X$.  Because $S$ is Tashkinov,
$e_r$ is colored by a color missing at an even earlier vertex of $S$; that
witness also lies in $X$.  This contradicts the closure of $X$ as defined
in \noderef{ColoredVertexSetClosed}.  No first outside vertex exists, and
therefore $V(S)\subseteq X$.
\end{proof}
