\begin{definition}
Let $K$ be the $(\alpha,\beta)$-component through a node $z$ in the
two-color incidence graph.  The component swap
$\varphi/K$ is obtained by changing color $\alpha$ to $\beta$ and color
$\beta$ to $\alpha$ on every edge identity whose edge-node lies in $K$,
and leaving every other value of the partial coloring unchanged.  In
particular, uncolored edges and edges with colors outside
$\{\alpha,\beta\}$ are unchanged.  This is the paper's Kempe change on the
corresponding two-color chain.
\end{definition}
