\begin{lemma}[Positive-divider presentation after an outside recoloring]
Let $G$ be a finite loopless multigraph on finite vertex and edge types,
with the elements of the edge type retained as individual edge identities.
Let $e_0$ be a distinguished edge and let $[k]$ be the color set.  Fix an
arbitrary certified input coloring, a terminal tree-sequence $T$, and a
generating coloring $\varphi$.  Let $\mathcal T$ certify that $T$ is an ETT
on exactly these data, with positive rung number $n$, and assume that
$\mathcal T$ has the maximum property.  Assume also that $\varphi$ is
proper and leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored.

Assume the following complete lower-rung package.  For every arbitrary
certified input coloring, terminal tree-sequence $U$, generating coloring
$\psi$, and positive-rung maximum ETT $\mathcal U$ on those data whose rung
number $m$ is strictly smaller than $n$, all six of the following hold.
Write $U_m$ and $U_{m+1}$ for the last ladder tree and final series tree of
$\mathcal U$, respectively, and write $D_i$, $\psi_i$, $\Theta_i$, and the
chosen vertex at slot $i$ for its exact indexed series data.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $V(U)$ is elementary under $\psi$.
\item For every proper coloring $\sigma$ leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored
which is $(U_{m+1},D_m,\psi_m)$-stable, and every two colors $a,b$, if at
least one of $a,b$ is missing on $U_{m+1}$ under $\sigma$, then $a,b$ are
$U_{m+1}$-interchangeable under $\sigma$, with isolated vertex components
included.
\item PE support stability descends with the exact indexed control data,
and equal-first-appearance PE stored sets are disjoint.  More precisely,
if a positive slot $i$ is PE with chosen support $v\in V(U)$, the
first-appearance index of $v$ is $j+1$, and a proper coloring $\sigma$
leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored is $(U_i,D_i,\psi_i)$-stable, then it is
$(U(v)-v,D_j,\psi_j)$-stable.  When the manuscript first-appearance
index $j+1>1$, it is also $(U_j,D_j,\psi_j)$-stable.  Moreover,
if two distinct positive PE slots have distinct chosen supports in $V(U)$
with the same first-appearance index, then the exact stored-color sets at
those two slots are disjoint.
\item If the terminal slot $m$ is PE with chosen support $v$ and recorded
colors $\gamma_m,\delta_m$, then for every proper coloring $\sigma$ leaving
exactly $e_0$ uncolored and $(U_m,D_m,\psi_m)$-stable, the full
$(\gamma_m,\delta_m)$ incidence component through $v$ meets $V(U_m)$
exactly in $v$.
\item Let $\sigma$ be proper, leave exactly $e_0$ uncolored, and be
$(U_m,D_m,\psi_m)$-stable.  If $\delta$ is defective on $V(U_m)$, if
$v\in V(U)$ is an endpoint in $V(U_m)$ of a $\delta$-colored boundary
edge, and if another such endpoint precedes $v$ in the tree order, then
the following precedence conclusion holds.  Whenever $v$ first appears
no later than a positive slot $i$ which is PE or SE and whose chosen
vertex is $v_i$, either $v=v_i$ or $v\prec_U v_i$.
\item Every proper coloring $\sigma$ leaving exactly $e_0$ uncolored and
$(U_m,D_m,\psi_m)$-stable is a $\psi\bmod U_m$ coloring for
$\mathcal U$.
\end{enumerate}


Let $B$ be a hierarchy base for $\mathcal T$ under $\varphi$, and $H$
a good hierarchy over $B$, with divider count $d>0$. Put
$A=H.\mathit{starred}(d)$ and let $T_n$ denote the last ladder tree.
Assume $|\overline\varphi(T_n)|\geq 2n+11$. Let $\alpha,\theta$ be colors,
and let $\sigma$ be an $\alpha,\theta$ outside-component recoloring of
$T$ from $\varphi$, in the sense of \noderef{OutsideKempeRecoloring}.

There is an actual \noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}
$\mathcal C_A$ relative to $\mathcal T$, displayed on the exact ordered
tree $A$ and generated by $\sigma$, with its own certified replacement
input. Its replacement ETT has maximum property and carries a hierarchy
base $B_A$ and a good hierarchy $K$ such that
\[
 K.\mathit{dividerCount}=d-1,\qquad
 |\overline\sigma(T^{A}_n)|\geq 2n+11,
\]
where $T^{A}_n$ is its actual last ladder tree (and its rung is $n$).
Furthermore the separate terminal certificate is
\[
 A\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\eta\in D^K_{n,d}}\Gamma^{d-1}_{K,\eta}\right)^-
 \text{-closed under }\sigma,
 \qquad D^K_{n,d}=D^K_n\setminus\overline\sigma(A).
\]
No exit paths or minimality assumptions are required for this construction.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{HierarchyLEMStable} with the given ETT, its maximum
property, positive rung, the six lower-rung assertions stated above,
$B,H$, their good hierarchy, the proper exactly-one-uncolored coloring
$\varphi$, and the stipulated outside recoloring to $\sigma$.
It returns a corresponding certificate $\mathcal C_T$ on $T$, maximum
property of its replacement ETT $\mathcal E$, a base $B^\sigma$, a
hierarchy $L$, a good hierarchy $\Gamma^\sigma$, and
\noderef{SameGoodHierarchy} between the old and new good hierarchies.
In particular \noderef{SameHierarchyStages} identifies their divider
counts and their entire stage functions after the canonical finite-index
casts. Thus $L$ has $d$ dividers, and its $d$-th stage is exactly $A$:
by \noderef{HierarchyStarredStage}, a positive starred index is the
stage at that same numerical index. Here $d>0$ is essential.

Apply \noderef{GoodHierarchyPrefixRepackage} to $\mathcal E$ with
$r=d$. Its hypotheses hold because $1\leq d$ and $d$ is an internal
stage index of $L$. It gives an ETT $\mathcal E_A$ displayed on
$L.\mathit{stage}(d)=A$, with the same replacement input and generating
color $\sigma$ as $\mathcal E$, maximum property, the same rung as
$\mathcal E$, and heterogeneous equality of their entire certified
series. It gives $B_A,K,\Gamma_K$, with $d-1$ dividers, the retained
stage and divider identities, equality of every retained raw hierarchy
stored set and total Gamma pair, and the separate internal closure
certificate
\[
 A\text{ is }
 \left(\bigcup_{\eta\in D^L_{n,d}}\Gamma^{d-1}_{L,\eta}\right)^-
 \text{-closed under }\sigma.                                      \tag{1}
\]

We construct $\mathcal C_A$ field by field, not by identifying unrelated
existence witnesses. Keep the replacement input and the replacement
series of $\mathcal C_T$, and use $\mathcal E_A$ as the replacement ETT.
Compose its rung equality with the rung agreement of $\mathcal C_T$.
Compose its heterogeneous series equality with the series-presentation
field of $\mathcal C_T$. The final-generating-coloring equation,
positive ladder-tree equalities, raw stored-color equalities,
connecting-edge equalities, extension-kind equalities, properness and
one-uncoloredness at every replacement slot, and stable-coloring
relations at all positive slots are exactly the fields of $\mathcal C_T$,
because they are statements about this unchanged replacement series
indexed by the original rung. These are all fields of
\noderef{CorrespondingExtendedTashkinovTree}; hence this constructs the
claimed certificate. Its replacement ETT is precisely $\mathcal E_A$,
so its maximum-property witness is the one returned by prefix repackaging.

For completeness, identify its last ladder tree with the original $T_n$
using the exact dependent indices. Let $m=\mathcal E_A.\mathit{rounds}$.
The preceding rung equalities give $m=n$. Transport the series of
$\mathcal E_A$ along this equality to rung $n$; its series-presentation
equality identifies it with the replacement series of $\mathcal C_T$.
The numerical value of the last-ladder tree index is $n$. Since $n>0$,
this is the positive slot indexed by $n-1$ in the ladder-tree agreement
field of $\mathcal C_T$. That field therefore says that this exact tree
is $T_n$. No equality of terminal displayed trees or of final closure
witnesses is used.

Apply \noderef{OutsideKempeRecoloringStable} to the stipulated outside
recoloring. It fixes every edge incident with $T$ and identifies
$\overline\sigma(v)=\overline\varphi(v)$ at every $v\in V(T)$.
The above-penultimate field of \noderef{ExtendedTashkinovTree} gives
$T_n\leq T$ as exact initial segments; their numerical index $n$ is
the same last-ladder index just identified. By
\noderef{TreeSequenceInitialSegmentVertexSetSubset},
$V(T_n)\subseteq V(T)$. Unfold \noderef{TreeMissingColors}: both
$\overline\sigma(T_n)$ and $\overline\varphi(T_n)$ are unions over the
identical finite set $V(T_n)$ of the equal vertex missing-color sets.
Termwise finite-union equality therefore proves equality of the
tree-missing finsets. Equality of their cardinalities, the given large
bound, and $m=n$ prove the required large bound for $\mathcal E_A$.

Finally distinguish the terminal stored set from the last internal one.
By \noderef{HierarchyTerminalStoredColors}, the terminal set of $K$ is
the common computed stored set of its certified series minus
$\overline\sigma(A)$. By \noderef{HierarchyStoredColorsAt}, the set
$D^L_{n,d}$ in (1) is the computed stored set of the series of
$\mathcal E$ minus the missing colors on
$L.\mathit{starred}(d)$. This starred tree is $A$ because $d>0$.
The full series equality from prefix repackaging identifies the two
computed stored sets, including their exact terminal finite indices;
this uses the defining formula in \noderef{TashkinovStoredColors}, not
merely equality of raw stored sets. Hence
$D^K_{n,d}=D^L_{n,d}$. The total Gamma agreement returned by
prefix repackaging at index $d-1$ identifies
$\Gamma_K^{d-1}$ with $\Gamma_L^{d-1}$ for every color. Both indices
have numerical value $d-1$, and the final index of $K$ has that value
because its count is $d-1$. Substituting these equalities into (1) gives
exactly the terminal \noderef{TreeColorSetMinusClosed} certificate in
the conclusion. Together with the already proved divider count, this
establishes every requested field.
\end{proof}
