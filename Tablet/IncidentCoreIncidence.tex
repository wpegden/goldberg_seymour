\begin{lemma}
Let $x\in V^{\circ}(G)$ and $e\in E$.  Then $x$ is incident with $e$ in
$G^{\circ}$ if and only if the underlying vertex of $x$ is incident with
the same edge identity $e$ in $G$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose first that $x$ is an endpoint of $e$ in $G^{\circ}$.  Applying the
endpoint-forgetting map to this membership and using
\noderef{IncidentCoreEndpoints} shows that the underlying vertex of $x$ is
an endpoint of $e$ in $G$.

Conversely, suppose the underlying vertex of $x$ is an endpoint of $e$ in
$G$.  By \noderef{IncidentCoreEndpoints}, membership in the original
endpoint pair lifts to membership of some incident vertex $y$ in the core
endpoint pair, with $y$ and $x$ having the same underlying vertex.  Equality
in the subtype \noderef{IncidentVertex} is determined by the underlying
vertex, so $y=x$.  Hence $x$ is incident with $e$ in $G^{\circ}$.
\end{proof}
