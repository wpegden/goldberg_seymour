\begin{lemma}[Outside-chain stability at the hierarchy base]
Let $T$ be the terminal ETT and let $\sigma_n$ be obtained from its
generating coloring $\varphi_n$ by recoloring finitely many
$(\alpha,\beta)$-chains fully contained in $G-V(T)$.  Then $\sigma_n$
is proper, leaves exactly $e_0$ uncolored, is
$(T,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable, and is $(T,\varphi_n)$-invariant.
Furthermore, if $\Theta_n=PE$, then $\sigma_n$ is
$(T_n\oplus R_n,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply \noderef{OutsideKempeRecoloringStable} to $T$ with control set
$D_n$.  It gives properness, the unique uncolored identity,
$(T,D_n,\varphi_n)$-stability, invariance, and equality of the two
colorings on every edge incident with $T$.

Assume now that $\Theta_n=PE$.  By \noderef{HierarchyBase} and
\noderef{OrderedTreeUnion}, the vertex sets of both $T_n$ and $R_n$
are contained in the starred base $T_n\vee R_n$.  The
core-inside-first field of \noderef{TashkinovHierarchy}, followed by its
chain of ordered stage inclusions and terminal-stage equality, embeds
that starred base into $T$.  Thus every edge incident with either $T_n$
or $R_n$ is incident with $T$, and every vertex of either tree is a
vertex of $T$.

The pointwise edge equality already obtained therefore supplies the edge
clause of \noderef{StableColoring} for $T_n$ with control set $D_n$ and
for $R_n$ with empty control set.  The vertexwise missing-color equality
supplies both vertex clauses.  These are precisely the two conjuncts of
\noderef{CombinedStableColoring}, proving the PE conclusion.
\end{proof}
